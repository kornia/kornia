`RandomMotionBlur` draws every odd `kernel_size` in a tuple range, upper bound included, with equal probability. It used to truncate a float draw, so the upper bound was practically never drawn and `kernel_size=(3, 5)` always blurred with `3`. An even upper bound still caps at the largest odd size below it, and a range holding no odd size still rounds up, so `(4, 4)` draws `5` as before.

Two consequences of the larger sizes now being drawn. A range that straddles the minimum odd size of `3`, such as `(0, 3)` or `(1, 3)`, raises on the draws below it rather than on every call. And the whole range is live against the padding rule for the first time: `kernel_size=(3, 5)` with `border_type="reflect"` on a `2 x 2` image raises `RuntimeError: Padding size should be less than the corresponding input dimension` for roughly half the draws, where the constant `3` always fit.

A reversed range such as `kernel_size=(20, 3)` now raises a `ValueError` at construction. It used to draw a constant `21`, above both of its own bounds.
