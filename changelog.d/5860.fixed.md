`match_adalam`, `GeometryAwareDescriptorMatcher("adalam")`, `AdalamFilter.match_and_filter` and `AdalamFilter.filter_matches` now return their outputs in the usual order when nothing matches: with fewer than two descriptors on either side, or when no seed survives and no ratio-test match remains, `match_adalam` used to return the `(0, 2)` int64 indices first and the `(0, 1)` float32 distances second, and `AdalamFilter.match_and_filter`, and `AdalamFilter.filter_matches` when no seed survives, returned the float `(0, 1)` tensor as their indices.

`match_fginn` now masks out the candidates within `spatial_th` of the nearest neighbour when it picks the second neighbour, so the ratio no longer depends on the scale of the distances. It used to add `1e6` to their distance, so with distances of that order, such as squared distances between unnormalised SIFT descriptors, one of them could still be the second neighbour. When every candidate is within `spatial_th`, the match is accepted with ratio `0`; it used to be the nearest distance over the second-nearest plus `1e6`, which is close to `0` only for small distances.
