`kornia.filters.median_blur`, `MedianBlur` and `RandomMedianBlur` now reject an even or non-positive `kernel_size` entry with kornia's `BaseError` ("Kernel size must be an odd integer bigger than 0"), and `RandomMedianBlur` does so at construction. Previously `RandomMedianBlur` accepted such a kernel at construction, and the call failed with a raw torch `RuntimeError` from a reshape, a pad or a convolution, which `except RuntimeError` no longer catches. The one input that used to pass was an even kernel on an empty batch or an image with zero channels, which returned an empty tensor and now raises too. Integral floats and numpy integers such as `(3.0, 3.0)` are still accepted.
