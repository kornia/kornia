`VideoBoxes.compute_area` and `VideoBoxes.to_mask` now restore the temporal axis, returning tensors shaped `(B, T, N)` and `(B, T, N, height, width)` respectively instead of keeping the flattened internal storage shape `(B * T, ...)`. This makes them consistent with `VideoBoxes.get_boxes_shape` and `VideoBoxes.to_tensor`. In addition, `Boxes.filter_boxes_by_area` correctly supports `VideoBoxes` by reshaping the computed area to match `_valid`.
