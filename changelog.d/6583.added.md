`VideoKeypoints.from_tensor` and `ModelBaseMixin.save` emit a `DeprecationWarning` when `validate_boxes` or `is_batch` is passed as `True` or `False`; the arguments will be removed in a future release. Neither argument has ever had an effect, and results do not change. `VideoKeypoints.from_tensor` now has a docstring, and its shape error names keypoints instead of boxes.
