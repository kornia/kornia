The `[0, 1]` clamp of the `kornia.enhance` intensity ops, and of the augmentations built on them, now keeps the derivative at a result exactly on `0` or `1`, which was 0 on torch 2.14 and later and nonzero on earlier releases: `adjust_brightness(x, 0.0)`, for example, returned its input with a zero gradient at black and white pixels. Values are unchanged, and forward-mode `adjust_gamma` now gives 0 at a black pixel for a gamma between 0 and 1 on every release.
