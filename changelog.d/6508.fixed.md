`adjust_sigmoid` and `AdjustSigmoid` avoid NaN gradients for float16 images in `[0, 1]` at higher gains and non-default scalar cutoffs, including `gain=40, cutoff=0.3` and `gain=200`. Small tail gradients and second derivatives are preserved using float32 intermediates, while outputs remain float16. Other image dtypes and tensor-valued parameters retain their existing behavior.
