`adjust_sigmoid` and `AdjustSigmoid` avoid float16 NaN gradients at scalar `gain=30`, `cutoff=0.5` for images in `[0, 1]`, including their small tail and second derivatives. Float32 intermediates are used only for float16 images when scalar gain/cutoff bound the absolute exponent between 11 (exclusive) and 16 (inclusive). The default path, other dtypes, tensor-valued parameters and larger-exponent behavior are unchanged.
