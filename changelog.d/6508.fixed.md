`adjust_sigmoid` and `AdjustSigmoid` no longer return NaN gradients for float16 images in `[0, 1]` once `|gain * (cutoff - x)|` exceeds about 11, for example `gain=30` (default `cutoff=0.5`) or `gain=40` with `cutoff=0.3`; very large gains such as `gain=200` work as well. With Python-number `gain` and `cutoff`, float16 images are now evaluated in float32 and rounded back to float16, so float16 outputs and gradients can differ slightly from earlier releases, also at the default `gain=10` (they are more accurate). Other image dtypes and tensor-valued `gain`/`cutoff` are unchanged.
