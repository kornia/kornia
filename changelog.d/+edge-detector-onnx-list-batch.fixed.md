`EdgeDetector.to_onnx` and `SuperResolution.to_onnx` export a model whose post-processor returns a list with one output per image for a batch of one image, as `ObjectDetector.to_onnx` does for per-image detections. They used to declare a dynamic batch, and the TorchScript exporter (`dynamo=False`) turned the list into one graph output per traced image: the graph returned two maps for a batch of three images, and two maps, one of them empty, for a single image. The dynamo exporter failed on such a model, and now exports it as well. A `dynamic_axes` or `dynamic_shapes` argument that makes the batch of such an export dynamic raises a `ValueError` after the export, before anything is saved. The post-processors the builders use (`ResizePostProcessor` for `EdgeDetector`, `nn.Identity` and `OutputRangePostProcessor` for `SuperResolution`) and a post-processor that returns one tensor keep the dynamic batch.
