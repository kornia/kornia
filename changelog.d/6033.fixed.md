The mix augmentations (`RandomMixUpV2`, `RandomCutMixV2`, `RandomMosaic`, `RandomJigsaw`, `PatchMix`, `RandomTransplantation` and `RandomTransplantation3D`) raise the documented `RuntimeError: Inverse for <Class> is not supported.` from `inverse(output)` and `inverse(output, params=...)`. Previously only a call without positional arguments did; these call forms raised `TypeError: MixAugmentationBaseV2.inverse() takes 1 positional argument but 2 were given`.
