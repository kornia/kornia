`decompose_essential_matrix`, `motion_from_essential`, `motion_from_essential_choose_solution` and `project_to_essential` now have finite and correct gradients at an exact essential matrix, such as the output of `find_essential`, where the two largest singular values are equal: the gradient used to be NaN when they were bit-equal, and finite but wrong when they differed by rounding. The returned values do not change. A call that records a derivative now takes about 3 times as long, forward and backward together; a call that records none takes the same time. A matrix with no derivative, where the two smallest singular values are exactly equal, gives a finite gradient that is not meaningful, where it used to give NaN.
