`RANSAC` for homographies and fundamental matrices now defaults to `local_optimization="lm"`: it keeps the eight best minimal models of the run, refines them with Levenberg-Marquardt on the truncated Sampson or transfer error after sampling, and refines the winner on its inliers with a Cauchy loss. Previously each new best model was refitted with DLT solutions on 32-inlier subsets and then on all its inliers (`lo_sample_size=32`). `model_type="fundamental"` now draws seven-point samples like `"fundamental_7pt"`; it used to draw eight-point ones, which the new `"fundamental_8pt"` keeps. With the new default, auto batches grow from 256 samples on CPU instead of a fixed size, early stopping follows the support of the best minimal model instead of the refitted one, and the returned mask holds the inliers of the returned, refined model. Estimates for a given seed change accordingly; pass `local_optimization="dlt"` (with `"fundamental_8pt"` for fundamental matrices) to keep the previous behavior. Essential matrices and line-segment homographies are unchanged.
