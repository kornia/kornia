The `kornia.morphology` docstrings are trimmed to what a caller needs: `dilation` carries the one convention block and lists the known defects (#4734, #4735, #4762) in one place, the other six functions refer to it, and an out-of-range `reflect` or `circular` pad is documented as raising an error rather than a `RuntimeError`, since that error comes from torch. The morphology table on the Conventions & Pitfalls page now holds the scipy, scikit-image and OpenCV mapping, including the kornia calls equal to their dilation, erosion, opening and closing and the `replicate` -> `nearest` and `circular` -> `wrap` border names.
