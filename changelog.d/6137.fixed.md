`depth_to_normals` now computes float16 and bfloat16 inputs in float32 and runs its gradient convolution outside autocast. float16 depth maps gave NaN normals for near surfaces (closer than about 0.25 m at a 1000-pixel focal length) and inside zero-depth holes, normals off by tens of degrees at 0.5 to 1 m, and non-finite gradients; bfloat16 depth maps wider than 256 pixels gave zero normals; float32 depth maps inside an autocast region gave normals off by a median of 1 to 40 degrees (and, under float16 autocast, non-finite gradients); and float16 or bfloat16 depth maps inside a CPU autocast region of the other half dtype raised `RuntimeError`. The output dtype inside autocast now follows the inputs, as it does outside: under CPU autocast, float32 and integer inputs used to return the autocast dtype, and under CUDA autocast, half-precision inputs used to return float32.
