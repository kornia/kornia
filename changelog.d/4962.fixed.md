`So3.exp`, and with it `Se3.exp`, now switches to its small-angle series at a fixed 0.5 rad instead of at
`finfo(dtype).eps * 1e3`, which is 7.8 rad in bfloat16 (#4928). Every bfloat16 `exp` used the two-term series
`0.5 - theta**2 / 48` for `sin(theta / 2) / theta`, so at `theta = 3` the quaternion had norm 0.94 and the rotation
matrix was off by 0.23 in an entry; the error is now one bfloat16 ulp. The series runs through `theta**10`, so the
float32 and float64 outputs move by at most 1.5e-8 and 2.8e-17, at the switch point only.
