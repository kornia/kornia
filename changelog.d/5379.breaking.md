`filter2d` and `fft_conv` now pad for `padding='SAME'`, and for every other spelling of `'same'` (#5156). Previously the
padding check accepted any spelling but only `'same'` took the padded branch, so `padding='SAME'` silently returned the
`'valid'`-size output: a 3×3 kernel on a 5×7 image gave 3×5 and now gives 5×7, the output of `padding='same'`. `'VALID'`
still crops, and lower-case calls are unchanged.
