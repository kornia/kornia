`guided_blur` and `GuidedBlur` now compute window statistics for float16 and bfloat16 images in float32. With scalar regularization, coefficients are also computed in float32 and only the result is cast back, so a half-precision call returns the float32 result for the same pixel values, rounded once. A float64 tensor `eps` widens coefficient arithmetic and the grayscale result to float64; multichannel guidance solves in float64 before casting its coefficients back. Before, the local variance `E[I^2] - E[I]^2` was formed in half precision, whose rounding at mid-gray (about 2e-4 in float16, 2e-3 in bfloat16) swamped a small `eps`: with `eps=1e-4` on two 512 x 512 test photographs, float16 results were off by up to 1.5 with grayscale guidance and 36 with RGB guidance, gradients came back non-finite, and with RGB guidance `torch.linalg.solve` could fail on a singular matrix. A float16 or bfloat16 multichannel guidance with an `input` of another dtype (float32, uint8 or the other half-precision type) raised a dtype mismatch; it now returns float32. Autocast is now disabled inside the filter: a float32 call in an autocast region used to be filtered in the autocast dtype, with the same errors, and returned that dtype with RGB guidance; it now returns the float32 result. float32 and float64 results outside autocast are unchanged.
