`blur_pool2d`, `BlurPool2D`, `max_blur_pool2d`, `MaxBlurPool2D`, `edge_aware_blur_pool2d` and `EdgeAwareBlurPool2D` now accept an unbatched `(C, H, W)` input, like `max_pool2d`, returning the batched result of `input[None]` without its batch dimension. Inputs of any other rank raise `ShapeError` instead of failing inside `conv2d` or silently producing a wrong-shaped result.
