RANSAC's seven- and eight-point fundamental and four-point homography solvers, and `find_fundamental` with `method="7POINT"` or `"8POINT"`, are much faster on CUDA when no gradient is needed: their batched LU null space factorizes square matrices padded with zero columns in one batched kernel, where PyTorch factorized the non-square ones a matrix at a time (8,192 seven- or eight-point samples: about 300 ms to 0.3 ms on an RTX 4090). Results are unchanged, except that on this path an exactly rank-deficient minimal sample, such as one with a zero row before the last, gives a NaN model instead of an arbitrary one from its larger null space, which RANSAC already discards as degenerate. CPU, gradients and `find_homography_dlt` are unchanged.
