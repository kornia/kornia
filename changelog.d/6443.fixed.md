`kornia.contrib.KMeans` now rejects a `num_clusters` that is not a positive integer when it is constructed, and `fit` rejects more clusters than samples when the starting centres are drawn from the data, each with an error that names `num_clusters`. A negative or `None` count used to pass the constructor and fail in `fit` inside `one_hot` ("Class values must be smaller than num_classes"), and more clusters than samples drew fewer centres and failed the first update with an unrelated tensor-size `RuntimeError`.
