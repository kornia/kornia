`bilateral_blur`, `joint_bilateral_blur`, `BilateralBlur` and `JointBilateralBlur` now reject an even (or non-positive) `kernel_size` up front with kornia's "Kernel size must be an odd integer bigger than 0" error; the modules raise it when they are constructed (#5163). Previously such a size was accepted and every call then failed inside the filter: usually with a raw torch shape error (`The size of tensor a (7) must match the size of tensor b (8)`), and on small images, where the unfolded window axis has length 1 and broadcasts, with the odd-size error of `get_gaussian_kernel2d`. Odd kernel sizes are unchanged.
