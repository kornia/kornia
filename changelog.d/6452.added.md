The image matching guide compares the speed and accuracy of kornia's local-feature pipelines and matchers with the authors' code, OpenCV and faiss on CUDA, an x86 CPU and Apple M1 (CPU and MPS). The `kornia.feature` package docstring and the `ALIKED`, `SIFTFeature`, `SIFTFeatureScaleSpace`, `match_snn` and `match_smnn` docstrings carry the same guidance, including where kornia is slower and what to use instead.
