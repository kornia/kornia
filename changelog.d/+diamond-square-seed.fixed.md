`kornia.contrib.diamond_square`, and so `RandomPlasmaBrightness`, `RandomPlasmaContrast` and `RandomPlasmaShadow`, now generate an unbiased map. The seed divided its corners and centre by three, took the wrong corner for the right-edge midpoint and never interpolated the interior row-1 points of an elongated seed, and the square step at the border over-weighted a neighbour. A 9x9 map averaged 0.166 at the corners and the centre and about 0.5 elsewhere (0.61 beside the corners on the border), so a plasma brightness or shadow map darkened the corners and the centre of every image; the expected map is now a flat 0.5, and an unnormalized map stays in [0, 1] for `torch.rand`. The diamond and square centres are chosen by index parity, so a signed `random_fn` such as `torch.randn` no longer leaves most pixels at zero; a tensor `roughness` or `random_scale` follows the requested `dtype` and `device`; and an integer `normalize_range` such as `(0, 1)` is accepted. The random numbers are drawn in a different order, so a fixed seed gives a different map than before.
