`kornia.contrib.diamond_square` with `random_scale` other than 1 no longer scales the seed by `random_scale`. The expected value is 0.5 at every pixel with `torch.rand`; it was 0.5·`random_scale` at the seed nodes and moved towards 0.5 level by level elsewhere (0.25 vs 0.31–0.35 at 0.5). `random_scale=1`, the default used by `RandomPlasmaBrightness`, `RandomPlasmaContrast` and `RandomPlasmaShadow`, gives the same output as before. With `dtype=None`, the output dtype no longer depends on the map size: a float64 tensor `roughness` or `random_scale` gives a float64 map at every size, where a map with a side of 3 px or less came out float32.
