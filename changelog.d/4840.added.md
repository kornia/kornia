`rotation_matrix_to_axis_angle` and `rotation_matrix_to_quaternion` take `check_rotation`
(default `False`). With `check_rotation=True` they raise a `ValueError` unless every input is a
rotation: `max|R @ R^T - I|` within 100 eps of the input dtype (16 eps for float16 and bfloat16)
and `det(R) > 0`. Without it the behaviour is unchanged, so a reflection such as `diag(-1, 1, 1)`
is still reported as the identity rotation `[0., 0., 0.]`. Closes #4773.
