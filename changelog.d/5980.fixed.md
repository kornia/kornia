2D intensity augmentations whose arithmetic assumes unit-range images now clamp their input into `[0, 1]` before operating, as well as their output. This fixes the NaN that `RandomGamma` returned for a negative input and a non-integer gamma, `RandomPosterize`'s platform-dependent `uint8` conversion of out-of-range values, and the negative values `RandomPlanckianJitter` kept. `RandomEqualize` and `RandomClahe` clamp an out-of-range input instead of raising; `kornia.enhance.equalize` and `equalize_clahe` still raise. `RandomHue`, `RandomSaturation`, `RandomSnow`, `ColorJiggle` and `ColorJitter` (also with neutral or hue-only steps), and `RandomPosterize` at `bits=8` used to pass out-of-range values through and now clamp them. Range-independent augmentations (blurs, noise, channel operations, erasing, grayscale, inversion, `Normalize`, `Denormalize`, `RandomAutoContrast`) and samples skipped by the probability gate keep their values; `RandomBrightness` and `RandomContrast` keep the unclipped arithmetic with `clip_output=False`.
