`HomographyTracker.set_target` now caches the target's features once for `LoFTR` and `LocalFeatureMatcher` matchers, instead of re-extracting them on every frame, and starts a new track. Fewer than 4 matches count as a failed match instead of reaching RANSAC, and `track_next_frame` without a prior homography raises a clear error. `ImageStitcher` warps its validity mask bilinearly, so a sub-pixel shift no longer leaves a dark seam or an extra dark column, and a batch sample without enough matches raises an error naming the sample. `kornia.contrib.audit` accepts `(B, H, W)` masks.
