RANSAC's seven- and eight-point fundamental and four-point homography solvers, and `find_fundamental` with `method="7POINT"` or `"8POINT"`, are fast again on CUDA with torch 2.15 when no gradient is needed. Torch 2.15 factorizes batches of non-square matrices one matrix at a time on CUDA (pytorch/pytorch#200581), which made CUDA RANSAC 2-270x slower than on torch 2.14, growing with the sample budget. The batched LU null space now factorizes square matrices padded with zero columns in one batched kernel: 8,192 seven- or eight-point samples take about 0.3 ms instead of 920 ms on an RTX 4090, and on torch 2.14 RANSAC is 3-14% faster than before. Results are unchanged, except that on this path an exactly rank-deficient minimal sample, such as one with a zero row before the last, gives a NaN model instead of an arbitrary one from its larger null space, which RANSAC already discards as degenerate. CPU, gradients and `find_homography_dlt` are unchanged.
