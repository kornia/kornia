`RANSAC` gains `local_optimization` (`"lm"` or `"dlt"`), `refine_iters` for the final Levenberg-Marquardt refinement, the `"fundamental_8pt"` model type, and an `offset` argument to `RANSAC.sample` that continues the PROSAC schedule across batches of different sizes. The new pipeline normalizes the correspondences once per call, solves minimal samples through a batched LU null space and scores them with one matrix product per batch; on PhotoTourism fundamental matrices and HEB homographies it is more accurate than the previous defaults and several times faster on CUDA. `sample_is_valid_for_homography` no longer copies index tensors to the device on every call. The minimal solvers share their LU null space with the public estimators: `find_fundamental(method="7POINT")`, the eight-correspondence unweighted case of `find_fundamental`, and the four-correspondence `find_homography_dlt(solver="lu")` replace `eigh`, QR or SVD with one batched LU factorization, which is several times faster on CPU and now runs in float16 and bfloat16; the seven-point cubic is expanded without inverting the null-space basis. `sampson_epipolar_distance` and `oneway_transfer_error` (and so `symmetric_transfer_error`) score several matrices on one set of correspondences with one or two matrix products, faster on CPU and CUDA for RANSAC-sized batches, and `find_fundamental`'s eight-point solver removes the smallest singular value in closed form instead of with an SVD for batches of 128 or more on CPU and 512 or more on other devices, about twice as fast for 2048 samples.
