`RANSAC` gains `local_optimization` (`"lm"` or `"dlt"`), `refine_iters` for the final Levenberg-Marquardt refinement, the `"fundamental_8pt"` model type, and an `offset` argument to `RANSAC.sample` that continues the PROSAC schedule across batches of different sizes. The new pipeline normalizes the correspondences once per call, solves minimal samples through a batched LU null space and scores them with one matrix product per batch; on PhotoTourism fundamental matrices and HEB homographies it is more accurate than the previous defaults and several times faster on CUDA. `sample_is_valid_for_homography` no longer copies index tensors to the device on every call.
