`RANSAC` gains `local_optimization` (`"lm"` or `"dlt"`), `refine_iters` for the final Levenberg-Marquardt refinement, the `"fundamental_8pt"` model type, and an `offset` argument to `RANSAC.sample` that continues the PROSAC schedule across batches of different sizes. The new pipeline normalizes the correspondences once per call, solves minimal samples through a batched LU null space and scores them with one matrix product per batch; on PhotoTourism fundamental matrices and HEB homographies it is more accurate than the previous defaults and several times faster on CUDA. `sample_is_valid_for_homography` no longer copies index tensors to the device on every call. The minimal solvers share their LU null space with the public estimators: `find_fundamental(method="7POINT")`, the eight-correspondence unweighted case of `find_fundamental`, and the four-correspondence `find_homography_dlt(solver="lu")` replace `eigh`, QR or SVD with one batched LU factorization, which is several times faster on CPU and now runs in float16 and bfloat16; the seven-point cubic is expanded without inverting the null-space basis.
