`RANSAC` defaults changed: `score_type` is now `"msac"` (was `"ransac"`) and `lo_sample_size` is now `32` (was `None`, iterative full-inlier refits). Candidates are now ranked by truncated squared residuals instead of inlier count, and local optimization fits `max_lo_iters` random 32-inlier subsets before one full-inlier refit whenever the consensus set has more than 32 inliers. Estimates for a given seed change accordingly; pass `score_type="ransac"` and `lo_sample_size=None` to keep the previous behavior. The RANSAC time-mAA benchmark now compares the class defaults, with and without PROSAC, against PoseLib and OpenCV, and skips PROSAC configurations on pairs without a match ranking.
