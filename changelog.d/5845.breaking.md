`equalize_clahe` and `RandomClahe` now use tiles of ceil(H / grid_size[0]) by ceil(W / grid_size[1]) pixels, odd or even, and reflect-pad only as needed to complete the grid. Previously, odd tile sizes were rounded up to the next even size and the image was padded to fit, even when the grid divided it. For example, an 8 x 8 grid on a 72 x 88 image used 10 x 12 tiles over an 80 x 96 padded image, shifting tile boundaries and changing output values.
