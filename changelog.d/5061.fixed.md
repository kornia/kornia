`CropGenerator`, `CropGenerator3D` and `ResizedCropGenerator` no longer place a crop start one past the last valid
position when the crop `size`, `scale` or `ratio` tensor is float16 or bfloat16, or when a float64 generator gets a
float32 tensor: the start draw is scaled and floored in the wider dtype and the integer start is cast afterwards
(#5052).
