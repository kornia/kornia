`solve_quartic` no longer solves a small multiple of a quartic as a cubic: the leading-coefficient tolerance for the cubic fallback is now relative to the largest coefficient of the row (capped at 1), so `(x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 3)(x - 4)` scaled by `5e-7` in float32 returns `1, 2, 3, 4` instead of a single wrong root. Rows at unit scale or above keep the previous tolerance. (#4905)
