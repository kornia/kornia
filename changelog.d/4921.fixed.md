`solve_quartic` decides whether a row is really a cubic relative to the row's scale: the leading-coefficient tolerance for the cubic fallback (`1e-6`, or `1e-12` in float64) is multiplied by the largest coefficient magnitude of the row, capped at 1. `(x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 3)(x - 4)` scaled by `5e-7` in float32 (or `1e-13` in float64) now returns `1, 2, 3, 4` instead of a single wrong root. Rows whose largest coefficient is 1 or more keep the previous absolute tolerance. (#4905)
