Outputs of `warp_perspective3d`, `homography_warp3d`, `warp_affine3d`, `crop_and_resize3d`,
`crop_by_boxes3d` and `center_crop3d` change when called with `align_corners=False`. They now match
the `align_corners=True` result up to out-of-bounds handling instead of carrying a spurious
sub-voxel scale and shift; `align_corners=True` call sites are bit-identical.

Several of these default to `align_corners=False`, so plain calls that pass no flag change too.
Measured as `max|out - in|` for an identity transform, or `max|out - expected|` for a +1 voxel
shift, on `arange` volumes:

| Call, default arguments | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| `warp_perspective3d(x, I, (4, 4, 4))` | 55.125 | 0.0 |
| `homography_warp3d(x, I, (2, 3, 4))` | 20.125 | 2.4e-07 |
| `warp_perspective3d(x, +1 voxel in x, (2, 3, 4))` | 17.5 from the exact shift | exact |
| `crop_and_resize3d`, integer box on a 4x4x4 volume | interpolated off-centre | the exact voxel slice |
| `RandomPerspective3D(distortion_scale=0.)`, 4x4x6 ramp | 0.866 | 9.5e-07 |

`RandomAffine3D` and `RandomRotation3D` also default to `align_corners=False` and sample different
voxels for non-identity transforms (up to 1.5 and 1.7 on a 0..23 volume at 10 degrees); their
transform matrices are unchanged. `warp_affine3d` defaults to `align_corners=True` and is unchanged
on its default path.
