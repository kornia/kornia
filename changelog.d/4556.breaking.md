Outputs of `warp_perspective3d`, `homography_warp3d`, `warp_affine3d`, `affine3d`, `rotate3d`,
`crop_and_resize3d`, `crop_by_boxes3d`, `crop_by_transform_mat3d` and `center_crop3d` change when
called with `align_corners=False`. They now match the `align_corners=True` result up to
out-of-bounds handling instead of carrying a spurious sub-voxel scale and shift;
`align_corners=True` call sites are bit-identical.

`warp_perspective3d`, `homography_warp3d`, `affine3d`, `rotate3d`, `crop_and_resize3d` and
`crop_by_boxes3d` default to `align_corners=False`, so plain calls that pass no flag change too.
Measured as `max|out - in|` for an identity transform, or `max|out - expected|` for a +1 voxel
shift, or `max|after - before|` otherwise, on `arange` volumes:

| Call, default arguments | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| `warp_perspective3d(x, I, (4, 4, 4))` | 55.125 | 0.0 |
| `homography_warp3d(x, I, (2, 3, 4))` | 20.125 | rounding |
| `warp_perspective3d(x, +1 voxel in x, (2, 3, 4))` | 17.5 from the exact shift | rounding |
| `rotate3d(x, 10., 10., 10.)`, 2x3x4 volume | | differs by 2.80 |
| `affine3d(x, M)`, 2x3x4 volume, small shear and translation | | differs by 2.73 |
| `crop_and_resize3d`, integer box on a 4x4x4 volume | interpolated off-centre, by 10.5 | the exact voxel slice |
| `RandomPerspective3D(distortion_scale=0.)`, `linspace(0, 1, 96).view(1, 1, 4, 4, 6)` | 0.875 | rounding |

Cells marked *rounding* are `grid_sample` accumulation noise rather than a residual of the fix: a
few ULP of the data, which is below 2e-06 for every volume above and scales with the input, so the
exact figure depends on the torch build.

`RandomAffine3D` and `RandomRotation3D` also default to `align_corners=False` and sample different
voxels, except where the sampled transform commutes with the old per-axis dilation, as a centred
rotation or a uniform scale does on a cubic volume. On a 2x3x4 `arange` volume `RandomRotation3D(10.)`
differs by 1.73 at seed 0 and by up to 2.99 over seeds 0-19, and `RandomAffine3D(10.)` by 1.55 and up
to 3.43, while on a 4x4x4 volume both are unchanged; adding `translate` or `shears` breaks the
commutation and they change on a cube too. Their transform matrices are unchanged. `warp_affine3d`,
`center_crop3d` and `crop_by_transform_mat3d` default to `align_corners=True` and are unchanged on
their default path.
