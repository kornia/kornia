`ScaleSpaceDetector` now searches exactly `n_levels` response levels per octave and uses the levels below and above them only as NMS neighbours. With a per-level response such as `BlobHessian` or `CornerGFTT` and a `ScalePyramid` with three extra levels, it used to search one more level. That level has the same scale as level 1 of the next octave, so every octave-boundary scale was detected twice, and the near-identical twins failed the ratio test in matching. `HesAffNetHardNet` and `GFTTAffNetHardNet` were affected. Their scale pyramid now also builds one level fewer per octave.
