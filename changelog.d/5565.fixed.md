`confusion_matrix` and `mean_iou` now check that `pred` and `target` are integer tensors with values in
`[0, num_classes)` and raise with the offending range otherwise, and form the cell index in `int64` (#5549).
Previously an out-of-range prediction was counted in another cell, an out-of-range target ended in a raw torch error,
the type checks never fired, and `uint8` label maps wrapped from `num_classes = 17`, so a perfect `uint8` prediction
with 21 classes reported an IoU of about 5e-7 for two classes that do not occur. The `num_classes` message now says
"at least two".
