`HardNet`, `HardNet8`, `HyNet`, `SOSNet`, `TFeat`, `OriNet`, `LAFAffNetShapeEstimator`, `KeyNet`, `KeyNetDetector` and `DeFMO` default to `pretrained=True`. Constructing one downloads its weights (once) and returns the trained model in evaluation mode; for `KeyNetDetector` that is its `KeyNet` network, while the detector itself keeps its mode, so an `ori_module` or `aff_module` you pass is not switched. They defaulted to random weights, returned in evaluation mode with BatchNorm, where the network has any, on its initial statistics. Pass `pretrained=False` for random weights. An architecture argument the checkpoint does not fit now needs `pretrained=False` as well: with the default, `HyNet` with another `is_bias` or `dim_desc`, and `KeyNet` or `KeyNetDetector` with a `keynet_conf` of another `num_levels` or `kernel_size`, raise `RuntimeError` from the strict weight load, and `HyNet(is_bias_FRN=False)` loads the weights but leaves their trained FRN biases unused.

Randomly initialised models are returned in training mode: `pretrained=False` on those classes, `SOLD2(pretrained=False)`, `SOLD2_detector(pretrained=False)` and `LoFTR(pretrained=None)`. They were returned in evaluation mode. Call `.eval()` before inference, and after loading your own weights into one of them. A random `LoFTR` in training mode runs its supervised training branch, so its forward raises without ground-truth matches until `.eval()` is called.
