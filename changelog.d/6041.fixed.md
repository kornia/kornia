`Image.from_dlpack` takes `color_space` and `channels_order` arguments, as `Image.from_numpy` does, so a channels-last `(H, W, C)` capsule is read with the right height, width and channels, and `Image.from_dlpack(img.to_dlpack(), img.pixel_format.color_space, img.channels_order)` round-trips any image. Previously `from_dlpack` always read the capsule as `(C, H, W)` and labelled it by its channel count, so the round trip of a channels-last image, the `from_numpy` default, silently swapped its height, width and channels, and a `BGR` image came back labelled `RGB`. The defaults are unchanged, and `to_dlpack` now documents that the capsule keeps the image's own layout.
