`Image.from_dlpack` takes a `channels_order` argument, as `Image.from_numpy` does, so a channels-last `(H, W, C)` capsule is read with the right height, width and channels, and `Image.from_dlpack(img.to_dlpack(), channels_order=img.channels_order)` round-trips any image. Previously `from_dlpack` always read the capsule as `(C, H, W)`, so the round trip of a channels-last image, the `from_numpy` default, silently swapped its height, width and channels. The default is unchanged, and `to_dlpack` now documents that the capsule keeps the image's own layout.
