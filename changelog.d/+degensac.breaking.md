`RANSAC` with `model_type="fundamental"` or `"fundamental_7pt"` and the default `local_optimization="lm"` now runs DEGENSAC. Previously a scene whose inliers mostly lie on one plane often returned a fundamental matrix consistent with that plane and wrong elsewhere, with few of the off-plane inliers in its mask; now record-setting degenerate samples are recovered with plane and parallax, which can also stop the sampling earlier. Estimates for a given seed change whenever a record-setting sample is degenerate, which Chum's tolerance also finds in many scenes without a dominant plane, where the recovered models only compete with the minimal ones; pass `degensac=False` to keep the previous behavior.
