`Rescale` works with `interpolation="nearest"`, `"nearest-exact"` and `"area"`. Previously its default `align_corners=True` was passed to `torch.nn.functional.interpolate`, which rejects any value but `None` for these modes, so every call raised `ValueError`. `align_corners` now applies to the `linear`, `bilinear`, `bicubic` and `trilinear` modes only, and their results are unchanged.
