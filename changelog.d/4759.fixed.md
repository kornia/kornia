This is about 1.1-1.5x faster on CPU for a batch of eight 256 x 256 images, about neutral for a single small image, and unchanged on MPS; outputs are identical. Calls that record a backward graph, carry a forward-mode AD tangent on the image or the structuring element, run under torch.func transforms, or are traced or scripted keep the out-of-place reduction.
