`LightGlueMatcher` and `LightGlue` synchronize the host with the device once or twice per image pair instead of 17 times (ALIKED or DISK features, measured with `torch.cuda.set_sync_debug_mode`): the matcher computes the keypoint scales and orientations only for the weights that read them (the `"sift"`, `"doghardnet"` and `"dog_affnet_hardnet"` configurations), copies `hw1`/`hw2` to the device without blocking, and returns the match lists `LightGlue` already computed; `LightGlue` selects each pair's matches with one read of the device and checks the keypoint range of both images with one read. The matches and scores are unchanged. On CUDA (RTX 4090, torch 2.15) matching a 2048-keypoint pair at 1024 px is 2-13 % faster for ALIKED, DISK and XFeat features.
