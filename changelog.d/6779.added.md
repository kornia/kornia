`LightGlueMatcher` computes the keypoint scales and orientations only for the weights that read them (the `"sift"`, `"doghardnet"` and `"dog_affnet_hardnet"` configurations), copies `hw1`/`hw2` to the device without waiting for its queued work, and `LightGlue`'s keypoint range check reads the device once instead of twice. The matches and scores are unchanged. On CUDA (RTX 4090, torch 2.15) matching a 2048-keypoint pair at 1024 px is 7-9 % faster for ALIKED and DISK features and about 4 % faster for XFeat features, where the per-pair host synchronizations had left the GPU idle.
