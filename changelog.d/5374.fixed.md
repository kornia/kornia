`equalize`, `equalize3d`, `RandomEqualize` and `RandomEqualize3D` now count float16 and bfloat16 histograms in float32. float16 rounded counts above 2048 and their total overflowed to inf once it passed 65504, so a float16 image or volume with about 65500 or more pixels per channel could come back as NaN; bfloat16 rounded counts above 256, which shifted the output: a 300x300 two-level image equalized to 0.992 instead of 1.0.
