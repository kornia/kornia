`load_state_dict_from_url`, `download_file_from_url` and `download_hf_file` keep handling a failed transfer as such when its temporary `.partial` file cannot be removed, for example because another process still holds it open on Windows. The `PermissionError` from that cleanup used to replace the transfer error: a truncated or timed-out transfer was then not retried, the `RuntimeError` named the `PermissionError` as its cause, and a `KeyboardInterrupt` during the transfer was swallowed and the next source tried. Now the transfer error is kept and the leftover file is reported in a warning, to be deleted by hand.
