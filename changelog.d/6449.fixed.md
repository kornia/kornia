Register augmentation dispatcher children so parameter discovery, checkpoints, training modes, and device or dtype moves include the contained sequences. After `.to(device)`, `.cuda()`, or `.double()`, the children sample on that device and in that dtype, as in `AugmentationSequential`, so random draws after a move can differ from Kornia 0.9.0. `state_dict()` now includes child buffers and parameters. A compatibility fallback preserves initialized and lazy child buffers and parameters, and non-tensor extra state, for legacy checkpoints without augmentation entries. With `strict=True`, missing new-format augmentation state, including a completely absent child subtree, raises a missing-key error when checkpoint metadata is preserved.
