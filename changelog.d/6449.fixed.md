Register augmentation dispatcher children so parameter discovery, checkpoints, training modes, and device or dtype moves include the contained sequences. After `.to(device)`, `.cuda()`, or `.double()`, the children sample on that device and in that dtype, as in `AugmentationSequential`, so random draws after a move can differ from Kornia 0.9.0. `state_dict()` now includes child buffers and parameters; older checkpoints without augmentation state still load with `strict=True`, preserving the instance's initialized child state. Partially missing new-format augmentation state still raises a missing-key error.
