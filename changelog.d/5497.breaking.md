`get_motion_kernel2d` with `mode="nearest"`, and with it `motion_blur`, `MotionBlur` and `RandomMotionBlur`, used to let roundoff pick the tap where the rotated line falls exactly between two taps (some multiples of 30 degrees), so the kernel changed with the dtype, the device, the torch version, `torch.compile` and a full turn added to the angle. The tie now goes to the even coordinate, as `grid_sample` breaks it with exact arithmetic, so at those angles the CPU float32 kernel can differ from the one it used to be: `get_motion_kernel2d(5, 330.0, 0.3)` gives other taps than before. A nearest kernel still has a zero gradient in the angle.
