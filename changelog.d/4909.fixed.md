`draw_convex_polygon` now draws a one-vertex polygon as that point, leaves the image of an empty polygon in a list unchanged, and accepts an empty list with a zero-batch image, instead of raising PyTorch errors. The edge slope no longer divides by `dy + 1e-12`: in float16 that epsilon underflowed and left the scanline through a repeated vertex (including the padding of a shorter polygon in a list) unfilled, and in float64 it dropped pixels whose centre lies exactly on a sloped edge.
