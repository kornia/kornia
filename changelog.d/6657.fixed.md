`XFeat.match_xfeat_star` now anchors both matched points three pixels into their coarse cells, scaled by each point's extraction scale, before applying the fine displacement. Previously both started at the top-left pixel, biasing matches under relative scale or rotation. Match coordinates change by 5 pixels per axis at the 0.6 extraction scale and approximately 2.31 pixels at the 1.3 scale; descriptors, match indices, sparse extraction and raw dense coordinates are unchanged.
