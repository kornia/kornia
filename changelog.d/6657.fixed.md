`XFeat.match_xfeat_star` now anchors both matched points three pixels into their coarse cells before applying the fine displacement, and measures the anchor and the displacement in pixels of the image the network saw: ``r / s`` input pixels per axis, for extraction scale ``s`` and the ratio ``r`` of the resize to multiples of 32. Previously both points started at the top-left pixel and the displacement was scaled by ``1 / s``, biasing matches under relative scale or rotation; match coordinates move by about 5 pixels per axis at the 0.6 extraction scale and about 2.3 pixels at the 1.3 scale. `XFeat.detectAndComputeDense` with the default ``multiscale=True`` now maps a keypoint ``p`` of the image rescaled by ``s`` back to the input through the pixel centre, ``(p + 0.5) / s - 0.5``, instead of ``p / s``, so its raw keypoints move by ``0.5 / s - 0.5``: 0.333 px down and to the right at the 0.6 scale and 0.115 px up and to the left at 1.3. Descriptors, ``scales``, match indices, ``multiscale=False`` and the sparse extraction are unchanged. This corrects coordinates against kornia's integer pixel-centre convention, as #6637 did for the resize to multiples of 32, so it ships without a deprecation window.
