`extract_tensor_patches`, `combine_tensor_patches`, `compute_padding`, `ExtractTensorPatches` and `CombineTensorPatches` reject a `window_size` or `stride` component below 1 up front with `ValueCheckError` (a `BaseError` and a `ValueError`) naming the argument, also under `python -O`. A zero or negative `stride` used to fail later with `ZeroDivisionError` or a `RuntimeError` from `unfold`/`fold`, and the two module classes accepted it at construction; `combine_tensor_patches` failed the same way for a non-positive `window_size`, or raised `AssertionError` when the stride exceeded it; `compute_padding` raised `ZeroDivisionError` for a zero stride and returned `(0, 0, 0, 0)` for a negative stride or a non-positive `window_size`. `extract_tensor_patches` still raises a `ValueError` for a non-positive `window_size`.
