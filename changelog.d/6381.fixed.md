`remap` and `DepthWarper` now normalize sampling coordinates with the same `align_corners` convention they pass to `grid_sample`. Previously, pixel maps were always normalized corner-aligned, so identity warps with `align_corners=False` (the `remap` default) were shifted. Outputs for those settings now change; `align_corners=True` is unchanged. `normalize_pixel_coordinates` and `denormalize_pixel_coordinates` (2D and 3D) gain an `align_corners` argument, default `True`.
