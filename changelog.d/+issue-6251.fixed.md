`decompose_essential_matrix`, `motion_from_essential`, `motion_from_essential_choose_solution` and `project_to_essential` now have finite and correct gradients at an exact essential matrix, such as the output of `find_essential`, where the two largest singular values are equal: the gradient used to be NaN when they were bit-equal, and finite but wrong when they differed by rounding. The returned values do not change.
