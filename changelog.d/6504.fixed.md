`find_homography_dlt`, `find_homography_dlt_iterated`, `find_homography_lines_dlt`, `find_homography_lines_dlt_iterated` and `sample_is_valid_for_homography` raise `ShapeError`, naming the argument and the expected shape, instead of a bare `AssertionError` (in the first two, an `IndexError` for 0-d or 1-D points of the same shape). Fewer than four segments in `find_homography_lines_dlt` and `find_homography_lines_dlt_iterated` also raise `ShapeError` instead of `AssertionError`, as fewer than four points in `find_homography_dlt` now do. The `repr` of `Similarity` no longer has a run of spaces after the angle.
