`equalize_clahe` and `RandomClahe` now round fast-path lookup-table entries to the nearest integer (ties to even), matching OpenCV's LUT quantization, instead of flooring them. In float32 and float64 each fast-path output pixel rises by at most one gray level (1/255), and for the same tile its lookup table now equals OpenCV's (#5659). The tile blending still differs from OpenCV (#5698). The differentiable path is unchanged.
