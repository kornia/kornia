`RANSAC(prosac_sampling=True)` always ran the whole `batch_size * max_iter` budget. It now stops with PROSAC's termination-length test (Chum and Matas, 2005): a ranked prefix certifies the incumbent when its support there is non-random and the draws it needs for `confidence` fit inside the prefix's growth interval, with OpenCV USAC's guards against short or weakly supported prefixes; the whole set is always a candidate, so PROSAC never runs longer than uniform sampling would. `confidence=1` still runs the whole budget.
