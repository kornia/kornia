`DoGHardNet` and `DoGAffNetHardNet` return LAFs and HardNet descriptors that `LightGlueMatcher("doghardnet")` and `LightGlueMatcher("dog_affnet_hardnet")` take without rescaling. They detect with the DoG detector of the default `"patch"` backend of `SIFTFeatureScaleSpace`, `DoGAffNetHardNet` adding AffNet shapes, estimate the orientation at the detection scale, and return and describe LAFs twice the detection frames: 12 sigma, the scale those LightGlue weights were trained with. The `LightGlueMatcher` documentation states that scale.
