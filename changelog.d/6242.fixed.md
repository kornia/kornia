`copy.deepcopy` of `So2`, `So3`, `Se2`, `Se3`, `Quaternion`, `Hyperplane` and `ParametrizedLine` no longer raises when their state carries autograd history, as for `Se3.exp(v)` with a `v` that requires grad. The copy holds that state as new leaves that keep `requires_grad`, still registered as buffers, as a `torch.save` / `torch.load` round trip does, and the original keeps its history.
