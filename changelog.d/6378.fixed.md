`equalize_clahe(..., slow_and_differentiable=True)` builds the soft histogram bin centres and the 0.001 bandwidth in `torch.promote_types(tiles.dtype, torch.float32)` instead of float32, so a float64 image is centred on the documented levels `k / 255` rather than on their float32 roundings. float32, float16 and bfloat16 results are bitwise unchanged.
