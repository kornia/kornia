`rgb_to_xyz`, `xyz_to_rgb`, `rgb_to_yuv`, `yuv_to_rgb` and the Lab/Luv conversions built on them now compute float32 input in float32 on CUDA regardless of cuDNN's TF32 setting. With older cuDNN (for example torch 2.5.1) some shapes, and `torch.compile`, returned TF32-precision results. CPU results are unchanged.
