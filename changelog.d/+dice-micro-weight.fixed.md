`dice_loss(average="micro", weight=w)` now weights the intersection once, computing the weighted Dice `2 Σ w p t / Σ w (p + t)`. It used to square the weight in the intersection, so a uniform weight scaled the Dice coefficient (a perfect prediction scored -1 with `weight=2`) and non-uniform weights could push the loss below -1. Unweighted, 0/1-weighted and `"macro"` losses are unchanged.
