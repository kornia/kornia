`MultiResolutionDetector`, and so `SIFTFeature`, `KeyNetDetector`, `KeyNetHardNet` and `KeyNetAffNetHardNet`, now reports a detection at level pixel `(u, v)` at that pixel's centre in the input, `((u + 0.5) * W / W_l - 0.5, (v + 0.5) * H / H_l - 0.5)`, instead of `(u * W / W_l, v * H / H_l)`, which lay up to 1.5 px up and to the left of it on the coarsest default level. Keypoints from every level but the input's own move; LAF scales do not.
