`MultiResolutionDetector`, and so `SIFTFeature`, `KeyNetDetector`, `KeyNetHardNet` and `KeyNetAffNetHardNet`, now reports a detection at level pixel `(u, v)` at that pixel's centre in the input, `((u + 0.5) * W / W_l - 0.5, (v + 0.5) * H / H_l - 0.5)`, instead of `(u * W / W_l, v * H / H_l)`, which lay about 1.5 px up and to the left of it on the coarsest default level and about 0.15 px down and to the right on the up level. This corrects wrong coordinates against kornia's integer pixel-centre convention, so it ships without a deprecation window. Keypoints from every level but the input's own move; LAF scales do not. `LightGlueMatcher("keynet_affnet_hardnet")` returns about 2 % fewer matches on the moved keypoints, with IMC pose accuracy unchanged beyond noise.
