`ZCAWhitening` now stores its fitted mean and transforms as buffers: `state_dict` saves them, loading a fitted state into an unfitted module fits it, and `.to()` moves and casts them. Before, a fitted module's `state_dict` was empty and `.to()` left the fit on its original device and dtype, so the moved module raised on input of the new dtype. A checkpoint saved before this change still loads with `strict=True` and keeps the current fit.
