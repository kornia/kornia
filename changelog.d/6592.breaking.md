With `random_apply`, `ImageSequential`, `AugmentationSequential`, `VideoSequential` and `PatchSequential` now sample with replacement only when the drawn count exceeds the number of children that can be drawn: the children other than mix augmentations with a nonzero weight in `random_apply_weights`, plus the place a certain mix insertion takes. They used to sample with replacement also when the count exceeded the sum of the weights, although the weights are otherwise relative, so `random_apply=2` repeated a child in 366 of 1000 calls with weights `[0.5, 0.3, 0.2]` and in none with `[5, 3, 2]`, and `random_apply=True` with a mix augmentation and the default weights repeated children. Those calls no longer repeat a child, which changes what they select for a given seed and the random numbers they consume; every other call keeps both.
