`KORNIA_CHECK_SAME_DEVICES` now returns `False` with `raises=False` and raises `TypeCheckError` with `raises=True` for an empty list, a non-list container or a list holding a non-tensor. Previously `[]` and tuples returned `True` with `raises=False`, and a non-tensor element raised `AttributeError`, or was compared by its `device` attribute if it had one (a `Vector3`, for example).
