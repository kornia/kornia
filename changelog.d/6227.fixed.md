`mean_absolute_disparity_error`, `root_mean_squared_disparity_error`, `mean_bad_pixel_error` and `kitti_d1_error` move a `valid_mask` that is on another device than the disparity maps to the maps' device. In 0.9.0, a CPU mask with CUDA or MPS maps raised torch's `RuntimeError` under every reduction; it works again and gives the same result as a mask on the maps' device. Maps on two different devices now raise kornia's `DeviceError` instead of torch's `RuntimeError`. `DeviceError` is a `ValueError`, not a `RuntimeError`, so an `except RuntimeError` around such a call no longer catches it. A 0-d map on the CPU still combines with a 0-d map on another device, because torch treats a 0-d CPU tensor as a scalar.
