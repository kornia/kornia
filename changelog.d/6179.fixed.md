`get_closest_point_on_epipolar_line` runs with autocast disabled, as the solvers of #6072 do, so a caller's `torch.autocast` region no longer changes its result. Under CPU autocast it computed the epipolar lines in half precision and raised `RuntimeError: expected scalar type Float but found BFloat16` (or `Half`) for float32 points and `Fm`. `Fm` now takes the dtype of `pts1`: autocast used to cast a float32 `Fm` to half-precision points' dtype, and an `Fm` in another dtype than the points, which raised outside autocast, now gives the result in the points' dtype. A call with points and `Fm` of one dtype outside autocast is unchanged, and a TorchScript-compiled call keeps working as before.
