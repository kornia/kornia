`get_closest_point_on_epipolar_line` runs with autocast disabled, as the solvers of #6072 do, so a caller's `torch.autocast` region no longer changes its result. Under CPU autocast it computed the epipolar lines in half precision and raised `RuntimeError: expected scalar type Float but found BFloat16` (or `Half`) for float32 points and `Fm`; under MPS autocast it returned float32 points several pixels off. `Fm` and `pts2` now take the dtype of `pts1`, as autocast used to arrange for half-precision operands: an `Fm` or `pts2` in another dtype than `pts1`, which raised outside autocast (on MPS a mixed `pts2` returned wrong values instead), now gives the result in the dtype of `pts1`. A call with all three in one dtype outside autocast is unchanged, and a TorchScript-compiled call keeps working as before.
