Add the Double Sphere camera model (Usenko et al., 3DV 2018) to `kornia.sensors.camera` and `kornia.geometry.camera`:
- `kornia.geometry.camera.distort_points_double_sphere`: maps normalized $z=1$ points to pixels with 6 parameters `[fx, fy, cx, cy, xi, alpha]`.
- `kornia.geometry.camera.undistort_points_double_sphere`: exact, closed-form algebraic unprojection from pixels to normalized $z=1$ points without iterative root-finding.
- `kornia.geometry.camera.dx_distort_points_double_sphere`: analytic Jacobian of the Double Sphere distortion with respect to the point.
- `kornia.sensors.camera.distortion_model.DoubleSphereTransform`: sensor distortion component for `Vector2` points.
- `kornia.sensors.camera.DoubleSphereModel`: camera model for wide-angle and fisheye cameras with `CameraModelType.DOUBLE_SPHERE`.
