The mutual information losses (`mutual_information_loss`, `normalized_mutual_information_loss`, their `_2d` and `_3d`
forms and the `MILossFromRef*` and `NMILossFromRef*` modules) now normalise each signal onto the bin centres
`0 … num_bins - 1`, so every sample contributes to the histogram and the result no longer depends on the intensity
polarity (#5546). Previously the signals were normalised onto `[0, num_bins]` and the pixels at an image's maximum
were left out of the histogram: a binary mask had mutual information 2.4e-13 with itself instead of its entropy,
normalised mutual information 1.77 instead of 2. Every value changes, a little on continuous images and a lot on
binary, label or saturated images.
