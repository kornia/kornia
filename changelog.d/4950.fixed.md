`NamedPose.from_rt` keeps the batch of a rotation tensor: a `(B, 3, 3)` or `(B, 2, 2)` rotation with a `(B, 3)` or `(B, 2)` translation now builds a `(B, 4, 4)` or `(B, 3, 3)` pose like `NamedPose.from_matrix`, instead of raising a `RuntimeError` for `B > 1` or silently returning an unbatched pose for `B = 1`. A batched rotation whose translation is not `(B, D)` raises a `ValueError`. Unbatched `(D, D)` inputs are unchanged. (#4950)
