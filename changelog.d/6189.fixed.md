`RandomMixUpV2` and `RandomCutMixV2` now apply `batch_prob` to the class labels. A `params` dictionary whose `batch_prob` selects only some rows, such as a draw replayed on a subset or hand-built parameters, used to label the unselected rows as mixed with their drawn partner and weight although their images came back unchanged; those rows now get their own class twice with lambda 0, matching the image.
