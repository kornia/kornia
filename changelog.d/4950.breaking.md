`NamedPose.from_rt` keeps the batch of a rotation tensor. A `(B, 3, 3)` or `(B, 2, 2)` rotation tensor with a `(B, 3)` or `(B, 2)` translation now builds a `(B, 4, 4)` or `(B, 3, 3)` pose, as `NamedPose.from_matrix` and the `So3`/`So2` rotation path do. Previously `B > 1` raised a torch `RuntimeError`, and `B = 1` returned an unbatched `(4, 4)` or `(3, 3)` pose with a `(3,)` or `(2,)` translation; it now returns `(1, 4, 4)` or `(1, 3, 3)` with a `(1, 3)` or `(1, 2)` translation. The translation must now have exactly the rotation's batch shape, otherwise `from_rt` raises a `ValueError`: a `(1, D, D)` rotation with a `(D,)` translation, and a `(D, D)` rotation with a `(1, D)` translation, used to return an unbatched pose and now raise. An unbatched `(D, D)` rotation with a `(D,)` translation is unchanged. (#4950)
