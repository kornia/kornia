`kornia.metrics.root_mean_squared_disparity_error` now has a finite gradient where the prediction matches the target exactly. The derivative of the square root is infinite at a zero error, so an exact match used to get a `NaN` gradient: every matching pixel under `reduction='none'`, and every pixel of a perfect prediction under `'mean'` or `'sum'`, even where a `valid_mask` excluded the differing pixels. It now gets `0`, as `aepe` does since #6299. The value is unchanged.
