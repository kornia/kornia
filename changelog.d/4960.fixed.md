`Se2.exp` and `Se2.log` now keep the translation at and near `theta = 0` (#4924). Both guarded their 0/0 coefficients
with a fallback of 0 where the limit is 1, so `Se2.exp([1, 2, 0]).t` was `[0, 0]`, the log of every pure translation was
`[0, 0, 0]`, and just above `theta = 0` the closed forms cancelled: `Se2.exp(v).log()` was off by 2.0 at `theta = 1e-4`
in float32 (2e-2 in float16, 1e-8 in float64). `sin(theta) / theta`, `(1 - cos(theta)) / theta` and
`(theta / 2) cot(theta / 2)` are now evaluated through the cancellation-free `So3` series, and the gradients at the
identity are the true ones instead of 0 and `nan`. For a `v` that does not require grad, `Se2.exp(v).t` is now an
`nn.Parameter` like `Se3.exp(v).t`, instead of a tensor whose autograd graph led to the hidden `So2` parameter.
