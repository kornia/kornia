`get_perspective_transform` no longer returns a NaN/inf matrix for a homography whose bottom-right entry is zero, such as one that sends source pixel `(0, 0)` to infinity, or for one whose `M[2, 2] = 1` form does not fit the output dtype, such as a float16 homography with entries beyond 65504. When `M[2, 2]` is zero up to rounding, or that form overflows, the matrix is scaled to a largest absolute entry of 1 instead. Every other result is unchanged.
