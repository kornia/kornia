`get_perspective_transform` no longer returns a NaN/inf matrix when its `M[2, 2] = 1` form is not finite in the output dtype: a homography whose computed bottom-right entry is exactly zero, such as one that sends source pixel `(0, 0)` to infinity, or a float16 homography with entries beyond 65504. Such a matrix is divided instead by the geometric mean of its largest and smallest nonzero magnitudes, which keeps both within the dtype's range. Every result that was finite before is unchanged.
