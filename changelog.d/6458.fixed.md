`get_perspective_transform` no longer returns a NaN/inf matrix for a homography whose bottom-right entry is zero, such as one that sends source pixel `(0, 0)` to infinity, or for one whose `M[2, 2] = 1` form does not fit the output dtype, such as a float16 homography with entries beyond 65504. When `M[2, 2]` is zero up to rounding, or that form overflows, the matrix is divided instead by the geometric mean of its largest and smallest nonzero magnitudes, which keeps both within the dtype's range. Every other result is unchanged.
