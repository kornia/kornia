On torch 2.9.1, `torch.onnx.export(..., dynamo=True)` exports `pyrdown`, `build_pyramid` and `gaussian_blur2d` with a dynamic batch again. They used to fail with "Constraints violated (batch)": the batch check of `filter2d` and of `gaussian_blur2d`'s `sigma` compared a kernel or `sigma` batch of 1 with the input batch before it accepted the 1, and the size-oblivious `torch.export` that this exporter runs then guarded the batch against 1. `KORNIA_CHECK_SAME_SIZE` with `allow_one=True` now accepts a size of 1 before comparing it with the other size.
