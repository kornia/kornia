`find_essential` on CUDA computed the roots of Nistér's degree-ten polynomial with `torch.linalg.eigvals`, which has no batched CUDA kernel and factors the companion matrices one cusolver call at a time (about 3.6 ms each), so `RANSAC("essential")` spent 6.4 s per batch of 2048 samples. The eigenvalues are now computed on the host with LAPACK for CUDA inputs (about 15 ms for that batch, transfers included), which makes the solver about 150x faster on the GPU; results are unchanged to roundoff.
