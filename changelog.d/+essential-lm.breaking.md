`RANSAC("essential")` now defaults to `local_optimization="lm"`: the run's eight best five-point models are refined by Levenberg-Marquardt on the essential manifold, and the winner by a final Cauchy refinement on its inliers, in the caller's calibrated coordinates. Previously `"essential"` only supported `"dlt"`, which refitted eight-point fundamental matrices on inlier subsets and projected them onto the manifold; `local_optimization="dlt"` keeps that behavior. Estimates for a given seed change; the returned E has unit Frobenius norm and a positive largest entry, and the returned mask holds the inliers of the returned matrix. Automatic batches for essential matrices now start at 64 samples on CPU (up to 1024) and 256 on CUDA and MPS (up to 8192), instead of 256 (up to 2048) and the whole budget.
