An augmentation with a learnable `nn.Parameter` range now reads it before each draw: a 0-d half-width no longer fails the second `backward` with "Trying to backward through the graph a second time", and an optimizer step, `load_state_dict` or `AveragedModel.update_parameters` reaches the next draw, for 0-d and `(2,)` ranges alike. Ranges that are not learnable pay nothing and draw bitwise as before.
