When the samples `x1` and the bin centres of `histogram2d` promote to float16 or bfloat16, it now evaluates them in float32 and casts the result back to that dtype. In float16 the joint histogram of an ordinary input, such as a 128x128 image or 1024 samples with a 0.2 bandwidth, used to come back all zero or NaN; the histogram and its gradient now follow a float64 reference.
