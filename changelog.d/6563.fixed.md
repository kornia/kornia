float16 and bfloat16 geometry warps now build their sampling grid and sample in float32 and return the input dtype on
every device, not only on CPU (#5814): `warp_affine`, `warp_perspective`, `remap`, `homography_warp` with
`normalized_homography=False` and the 3D warps, and the crops and augmentations built on them. On CUDA and MPS they used
to compute in the half dtype: a bfloat16 `warp_affine` of a 256 px `[0, 1]` image was off by up to 0.98 on CUDA and on
MPS, `warp_affine3d` raised for a half CUDA or MPS output with a size-1 axis, and a bfloat16 warp inside a float16
`torch.autocast` raised. `HomographyWarper`, `homography_warp` with `normalized_homography=True` (in normalized or pixel
coordinates), `elastic_transform2d`, `warp_image_tps` and the depth warps also sample in float32 now, but they still
build their grid in the half dtype, so their float16 and bfloat16 accuracy is not fixed yet; that part of #5814 remains
open.
