`convert_affinematrix_to_homography`, `convert_affinematrix_to_homography3d` and `ARKitQTVecs_to_ColmapQTVecs` now reject integer, bool and complex input up front, naming the argument. Before this, an integer affine matrix raised a bare `RuntimeError` from the in-place `+= 1.0` on the padded homography, and an integer `qvec` or `tvec` raised a bare `RuntimeError` from `linalg.vector_norm` inside `ARKitQTVecs_to_ColmapQTVecs`. Their siblings, `Rt_to_matrix4x4` and `ColmapQTVecs_to_ARKitQTVecs`, already rejected this input with a named error.
