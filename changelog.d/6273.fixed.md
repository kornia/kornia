`AugmentationSequential` with a `VideoSequential(RandomMosaic)` and video boxes no longer raises `IndexError`. Each
frame's boxes now follow the mosaic of that frame, as image boxes do, with `same_on_frame=True` or `False` and with
`p < 1`. Frames that a partial gate leaves with fewer boxes than the mixed ones are zero-padded in the dense output,
as for image boxes. `VideoBoxes.to_tensor()` returns such boxes as one `(B, T, N, ...)` tensor whose padding rows are
zero in every mode, instead of raising. Indexing a `VideoBoxes` keeps each frame's padding, and assigning into a
`VideoBoxes` or a list-built `Boxes` moves it with the written rows, so a real box written over padding is no longer
zeroed or trimmed from the export. `VideoSequential` now hands its children frame-level `Boxes` over the video's
frames.
