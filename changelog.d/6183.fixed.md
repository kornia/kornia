`unsharp_mask` and `UnsharpMask` run under `torch.autocast`. Under autocast the blur, a convolution, came back in the autocast dtype, and `torch.lerp`, which does not promote, raised `RuntimeError: expected dtype ... for 'end' but got dtype float` on the mismatch with a float32 image. The blur is now cast to the image's dtype before the `lerp`, so the result keeps the image's dtype, as `2 * image - blur` would and as `kornia.enhance.sharpness` does; outside autocast the cast is a no-op and every result is unchanged.
