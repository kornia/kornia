`unsharp_mask` and `UnsharpMask` run under `torch.autocast`, like `gaussian_blur2d`. Under autocast the blur, a convolution, came back in the autocast dtype, and `torch.lerp` raised `RuntimeError: expected dtype ... for 'end' but got dtype float` on the mismatch with a float32 image. The image is now cast to the blur's dtype, so the result has the autocast dtype, as the blur does; outside autocast the cast is a no-op and every result is unchanged.
