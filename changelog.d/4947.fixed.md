`So2`, `Se2`, `Se3` and `ParametrizedLine` built from a tensor that requires grad keep that tensor, so the gradient reaches it; they used to wrap it in a new `nn.Parameter` and leave the caller's `.grad` at `None`. Such a tensor is now registered as a buffer: it no longer appears in the module's `.parameters()`, but `state_dict()`, `load_state_dict()` and `.to()` still reach it. A tensor that does not require grad still becomes a parameter. (#4943)
