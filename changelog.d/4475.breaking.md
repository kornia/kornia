Camera projection behavior changes at singular and near-singular depth. `project_points_z1`,
`Z1Projection.project`, and the sensor camera models using Z1 projection now keep `(x, y)` unchanged when
`abs(z) <= 1e-8`; they previously divided by `z` unconditionally, producing `inf`/`nan` at `z = 0` and `x / z`
for small non-zero depths. `PinholeCamera.project` now uses that guarded camera-frame branch at singular depth while
preserving its established full 4x4 intrinsics projection, higher-rank camera batching, and point dtype at regular
depths; for the canonical audit point, `z = 0` changes from `[100, 200]` to `[104, 203]`. `cam2pixel` now treats
`eps` as a branch threshold with a default of `1e-8`; it previously defaulted to `1e-12` and divided by `z + eps`,
biasing finite depths and producing coordinates around `1e14` at `z = 0` (`inf` in float16). These changes do not
yet define one complete singular-depth policy across every camera model; #4267 remains open for fisheye, NaN-depth,
and behind-camera semantics.
