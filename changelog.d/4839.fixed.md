PlanckianJitterGenerator keeps its index sampler in float32 when the augmentation is moved to float16 or bfloat16, so the sampled Planckian table index cannot go one past the end. On MPS, half-precision `torch.rand` returns exactly 1.0 for about 1 in 4096 (float16) or 1 in 512 (bfloat16) draws, which made `RandomPlanckianJitter` raise an out-of-bounds index error. float32 and float64 draws are unchanged; for the same seed, float16 and bfloat16 pipelines now draw some different indices than before.
