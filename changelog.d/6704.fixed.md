`kornia.core.utils.get_cuda_device_if_available` now falls back to the CPU device for an index outside `[0, torch.cuda.device_count())`, `batched_forward` rejects models that return anything but a single tensor with a clear error, and the enum `get` helpers (`Resample`, `BorderType`, `SamplePadding`, `DataKey`, `DType`) reject `bool` values and non-integral tensors (`DType.get(torch.tensor(2.7))`) instead of resolving or truncating them.
