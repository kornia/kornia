`torch.export.export` of `JPEGCodecDifferentiable` with a dynamic height and width now succeeds, and the exported program holds at every size. It used to raise `ConstraintViolationError` for named dims; with `Dim.AUTO` it exported but asserted outside the traced padding class (zero or non-zero padding) and at a size of 16 pixels. The chroma patches and the crop of the padding now take a route that torch's shape reasoning accepts while exporting (the eager route is unchanged, so eager values and gradients are bit for bit the same), and the encoder hands Cb and Cr to the decoder as one tensor. Fixes #6294.
