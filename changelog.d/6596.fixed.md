`warp_affine`, `warp_perspective`, `warp_affine3d`, `warp_perspective3d`, `warp_grid`, `warp_grid3d`, `homography_warp`, `homography_warp3d`, the thin plate spline functions for float32 points and the builders `get_rotation_matrix2d`, `get_affine_matrix2d`, `get_affine_matrix3d` and `get_projective_transform` now run their matrix products outside a caller's `torch.autocast` region, except in a scripted call, so these functions, `rotate`, `scale`, the crops and the output images of the geometric augmentations built on them give the same result in and out of it, and mixed float32 and half-precision inputs, such as a half-precision matrix a model predicts under AMP with a float32 image, run in float32 there.
