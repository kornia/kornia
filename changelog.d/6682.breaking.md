`kornia.models.paligemma` (`PaliGemma`, `PaliGemmaConfig`) is removed without replacement. It was an undocumented, unfinished PaliGemma 1 re-implementation: its forward pass matched transformers' `PaliGemmaForConditionalGeneration`, but kornia shipped no way to load the released checkpoints (whose So400m vision tower kornia's SigLip2 builder cannot build), no tokenizer and no generation, so it only ran with random weights. Use transformers for PaliGemma. The SigLIP/SigLip2 image and text encoders in `kornia.models.siglip2` stay.
