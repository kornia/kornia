`hessian_response` (and `BlobHessian`, the default response of `ScaleSpaceDetector`) forms the Hessian determinant in at least float32. Inside a float16 autocast region, a float32 or bfloat16 image now gets a finite gradient through `ScaleSpaceDetector`; the determinant's backward used to overflow float16 and make every pixel of the image gradient non-finite. float32 and float64 results are unchanged.
