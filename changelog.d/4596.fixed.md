`draw_line` now always draws both endpoints: it could stop one pixel short of `p2`, or pass a row or column off a pixel centre the line runs exactly through, because the minor coordinate was stepped in floating point. It is now placed at `ceil(t * minor / major)` in integers, one pixel per step along the major axis. `draw_line` also builds every line's pixels on the image's device instead of in Python loops over 0-d tensors, accepts an empty `(0, 2)` batch, and rejects a `p1` and `p2` of different shapes instead of broadcasting a `(2,)` point against a `(B, 2)` batch.
