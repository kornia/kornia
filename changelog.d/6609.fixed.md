`PatchDominantGradientOrientation`, `SIFTDescriptor`, `DenseSIFTDescriptor` and `SIFTDescriptorFromPyramid` no longer take gradients and angles from convolution rounding on flat pixels: `sq > 0` read exactness from a convolution result. A constant 0.1 patch on x86 CPU or CUDA now has a zero gradient. The large change is on macOS arm64 CPU for a grad-enabled float32 batch of 16 or more, where the gradients of low-contrast patches come down by orders of magnitude, and a float16 low-contrast patch no longer gets an `inf` or NaN gradient from `PatchDominantGradientOrientation`. Untracked input takes the same differences, so the result no longer depends on `requires_grad`: on x86 CPU and CUDA, `SIFTDescriptor` and `DenseSIFTDescriptor` do not change and `PatchDominantGradientOrientation` moves by at most 2.62e-06 rad on random patches, while a constant patch now gets the angle pi of an all-zero gradient.
