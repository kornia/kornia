On macOS arm64 CPU, `PatchDominantGradientOrientation`, `SIFTDescriptor`, `DenseSIFTDescriptor` and pyramid `SIFTFeature` no longer take gradients and angles from convolution rounding noise for a grad-enabled float32 batch of 16 or more. Calls under `torch.no_grad()` and calls on an untracked patch or image are unchanged, except for `SIFTFeature` with the patch backend in default grad mode, whose descriptors move closer to the `torch.no_grad()` ones with the same Oxford matching accuracy.
