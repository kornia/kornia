`fit_line` now raises `ValueCheckError` when the points with positive weight are all the same point, for example when
the weights are positive on a single point: a point whose weight is 0 or negative does not count, so such a row does
not determine a line. It used to return the fallback direction (0, 1) in 2-D and (1, 0, 0) in 3-D, except that with a
negative weight on another point, as in weights `(2, -1, 0)`, the 3-D fit returned the line through the two points.
A batch is rejected when any of its rows is degenerate. The check is skipped under `torch.compile` and after
`disable_checks()`, where the previous results come back. (#5082)
