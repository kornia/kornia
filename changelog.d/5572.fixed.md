`kornia.image.tensor_to_image` now maps a one-channel `(1, H, W)` tensor to `(H, W)` for every H and W, so a
1-row or 1-column grayscale image keeps its orientation and `kornia.io.write_image` saves it like any other (#5570).
Previously the 3-D branch squeezed every unit axis, so `(1, 1, W)` and `(1, H, 1)` came out as a 1-D array and
`write_image` of such an image, including `ImageModuleMixIn.save()`, raised a `TypeError` from the encoder.
