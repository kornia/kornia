`torch.jit.script` of an `MS_SSIMLoss` restored from a whole-module pickle of an older release, such as 0.9.0, now
raises `RuntimeError: Module 'MS_SSIMLoss' has no attribute 'sigmas'`; it used to script the module. Such a module
still runs eagerly and under `torch.compile`, and keeps filtering float64 images with its restored masks, as before.
Script a newly built `MS_SSIMLoss` instead.
