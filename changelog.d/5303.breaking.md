`lovasz_softmax_loss`, `LovaszSoftmaxLoss`, `lovasz_hinge_loss` and `LovaszHingeLoss` now return the loss in the
dtype of the prediction, promoted with the dtype of `weight` for the softmax variant, and compute the Jaccard weights
and the sum over pixels in that dtype, or in float32 for a float16 or bfloat16 prediction (#5289). Previously the
Jaccard weights took `torch.get_default_dtype()`: a float16 or bfloat16 prediction returned a float32 loss, a float32
prediction under a float64 default returned float64, and a float64 prediction had float32 rounding in its weights
and gradient. Float32 predictions under the default float32 are unchanged.
