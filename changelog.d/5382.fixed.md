`solve_quartic` no longer loses the small real roots of a quartic whose other root pair is much larger. Ferrari's small quadratic factor is now rebuilt from the large one with Vieta's relations instead of a cancelling difference. In float32, `(x - 0.5)(x + 0.25)(x^2 + 1e7)` used to return no real roots and `(x - 0.5)(x + 0.25)(x - 1e4)(x - 2e4)` only the two large ones; both now return all their real roots. In float64, a close small pair next to a large complex pair could lose both roots (`(x - 0.01)(x - 0.0105)(x^2 + 2000 x + 4e9)`) or come back about 1e-7 off; such roots are now accurate to about 1e-15. Rows whose two quadratic factors are not separated (constant terms within a factor of 16) are unchanged.
