`ScalePyramid` now raises `ValueCheckError` when `n_levels` is not a positive integer or `min_size` is negative or NaN, as it does for `extra_levels`. `n_levels=0` used to fail with a `ZeroDivisionError`, `n_levels=-1` with `extra_levels=1` returned octaves of a single level without an error, other negative values failed inside `forward`, and `n_levels=True` was read as 1; a negative or NaN `min_size` failed inside `forward` once the octaves reached an empty image. Every positive integer `n_levels` and non-negative `min_size` builds the same pyramid as before.
