`guided_blur(..., subsample=s)` and `GuidedBlur(..., subsample=s)` now work for any image size. They raised a broadcast `RuntimeError` (1-channel guidance) or an `einsum` error (multi-channel guidance) when the height or width was not a multiple of `s`, because the upsampled coefficient maps came out `floor(H / s) * s` pixels tall and wide; an axis shorter than `s` failed earlier, in a zero-sized `interpolate`. The maps are now resized to the input's own size, and an axis shorter than `s` is subsampled to one pixel instead of none. For a size that is not a multiple of `s`, row `i` of the subsampled image is pixel `floor(i * H / rows)`, and likewise for columns. Sizes that worked give bit-identical results.
