`warp_affine`, `warp_perspective` and `RandomAffine` with `padding_mode="fill"` keep the input's channel count. A `fill_value` with neither 1 element nor one per channel now raises a `ValueError` that names both numbers. Previously it was broadcast against the image, so a one-channel image with a three-value fill came back with three channels, and other mismatches raised a raw broadcasting error. Fills of other shapes that `warp_affine` and `RandomAffine` used to broadcast without documenting them, such as a per-sample `(B, C, 1, 1)` or a per-pixel `(B, C, H, W)` fill, now raise the same error. `warp_perspective` now defaults to one zero per channel and accepts `(C)` or `(1)` fills like `warp_affine`. Previously it substituted `zeros(3)` and required a three-value fill, so a one-channel image gained channels even without a `fill_value`. In `AugmentationSequential`, a per-channel `RandomAffine` `fill_value` pads masks with zero unless the mask sets its own `fill_value`, as a per-channel `fill` already does, both when augmenting and when inverting. Matching fills give the same results as before.
