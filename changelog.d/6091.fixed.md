`kornia.geometry.camera.dx_distort_points_affine` returns one Jacobian per point: its batch shape is the broadcast of the points' and the params' leading shapes, as documented and as `distort_points_affine` and `dx_distort_points_kannala_brandt` give. `N` points with one set of params returned a single `(2, 2)` Jacobian, and `(B, N, 2)` points with `(B, 1, 4)` params returned `(B, 1, 2, 2)`. A params batch that does not broadcast against the points now raises, where it was ignored.
