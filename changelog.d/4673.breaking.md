`median_blur` and `MedianBlur` now accept a configurable `border_type` (`"constant"`, `"reflect"`, `"replicate"`, or `"circular"`) and default to `border_type="reflect"` instead of zero-padding (`"constant"`). As a result, border medians on constant images now preserve the image value instead of returning 0 at corners and borders, consistent with `filter2d`, `gaussian_blur2d`, and `box_blur`. Callers relying on the previous zero-padding behavior can pass `border_type="constant"` explicitly.
