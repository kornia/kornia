`kornia.contrib.interp` computes a `float16` or `bfloat16` result in `float32` and rounds once, so every half-precision result can differ from before (it was up to about two ulps of the larger end value off in an ordinary segment). Integer coordinates, and half-precision coordinates, with a difference above 65504 no longer overflow the weight: `interp(x=100000, xp=[0, 200000], fp=float16 [0, 2])` gave NaN, `x = 60000` and `x = 1000` gave a silent 0 where the values are 0.6 and 0.01, and `float16` coordinates `[-60000, 60000]` with `fp = [0, 2]` gave NaN or 0 where the values are in `[0, 2]`. An integer `x` or `xp` mixed with half-precision coordinates is no longer rounded onto the half-precision grid: `interp(x=3001, xp=float16 [3000, 3010], fp=float16 [-40000, 40000])` gave -40000 where the value is -32000. A segment whose value difference overflows the half dtype is within about half an ulp of its larger end value, and its knots are exact.
