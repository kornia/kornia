`kornia.contrib.interp` interpolates `float16` and `bfloat16` values in `float32` and rounds once. Integer coordinates, and half-precision coordinates, with a difference above 65504 no longer overflow the weight: `interp(x=100000, xp=[0, 200000], fp=float16 [0, 2])` gave NaN, `x = 60000` and `x = 1000` gave a silent 0 where the values are 0.6 and 0.01, and `float16` coordinates `[-60000, 60000]` with `fp = [0, 2]` gave NaN or 0 where the values are in `[0, 2]`. A segment whose value difference overflows the half dtype is now within about half an ulp of its larger end value (it was up to two ulps off).
