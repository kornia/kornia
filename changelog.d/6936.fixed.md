`motion_blur`, `motion_blur3d`, `get_motion_kernel2d`, `get_motion_kernel3d`, `MotionBlur`, `MotionBlur3D`, `RandomMotionBlur` and `RandomMotionBlur3D` no longer fail in the default `mode="nearest"` when PyTorch's default device is an accelerator, for example after `torch.set_default_device("cuda")`.
