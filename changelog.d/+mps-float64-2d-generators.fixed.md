On MPS with a float64 default dtype, `RandomMotionBlur`, `RandomSaltAndPepperNoise`, `RandomJPEG`, `RandomRotation90` and `RandomErasing` return their sampled parameters. Before, they raised on torch 2.15 and came out as zeros or wrong values on torch 2.14: no motion-blur angle or direction, no salt-and-pepper noise, a JPEG quality of 0, no rotation, and erased rectangles of the wrong size and position.
