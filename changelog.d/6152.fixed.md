`transform_points` no longer truncates integer operands. Integer points, such as pixel indices from `torch.nonzero`, used to come back in their integer dtype, truncated whenever the transformed coordinate was fractional; they now return in the floating dtype of the transform, or in the default floating dtype when the transform is integer too. An integer transform used to truncate floating points before the product; it is now applied in the points' dtype. `transform_bbox` and `Keypoints.transform_keypoints` change with it.
