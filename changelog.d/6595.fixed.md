`Boxes` built from a list now records its padding per box, so item assignment and `index_put` no longer make the trimmed `to_tensor` export drop real boxes or keep padding rows, and a `VideoBoxes` merged with list-built boxes exports and draws masks, with the padding as invalid zero rows. A selection such as `boxes[0]` now shares its validity with the original exactly as long as it shares its coordinates: after `to`, `type` or an in-place transform of either, filtering or writing through one no longer blanks rows of the other.
