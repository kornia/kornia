The docstrings of `rotate3d`, `RandomRotation3D`, `RandomAffine3D`, `RandomMotionBlur3D` and their 3D parameter generators read as if `yaw`, `pitch` and `roll` were Euler angles, most of them as rotations about the x, y and z axes. They now say that the three form one axis-angle vector in degrees, converted with Rodrigues' formula: a single non-zero component is the rotation about its axis, while `(90, 90, 0)` is one turn of 127.3 degrees about `(1, 1, 0) / sqrt(2)`, which neither Euler order gives. The behaviour is unchanged.
