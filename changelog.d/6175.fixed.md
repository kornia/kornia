`KRt_from_projection` and `triangulate_points` run with autocast disabled, as the solvers of #6072 do, so a caller's `torch.autocast` region no longer changes their result. Under float16 or bfloat16 autocast, for float32 inputs, `KRt_from_projection` returned `K` and `R` in the autocast dtype on the CPU, with `R` about 3e-3 from orthogonal in bfloat16, and raised `RuntimeError: geqrf: MPS currently supports float32 only` on MPS; `triangulate_points` with its default `solver="eigh"` raised `NotImplementedError` on MPS. A call made outside autocast is unchanged, and a TorchScript-compiled `KRt_from_projection` keeps the caller's precision as before.
