`KRt_from_projection` and `triangulate_points` run with autocast disabled, as the solvers of #6072 do, so a caller's `torch.autocast` region no longer changes their result. Under float16 or bfloat16 autocast, `KRt_from_projection` raised `NotImplementedError: "lu_cpu" not implemented for 'BFloat16'` (or `'Half'`) on the CPU and `RuntimeError: geqrf: MPS currently supports float32 only` on MPS for float32 inputs; `triangulate_points` with `solver="eigh"` raised on MPS, and with `solver="cofactor"` returned float32 points off by up to 7.5e-2 on the CPU under bfloat16 autocast. A call made outside autocast is unchanged, and a TorchScript-compiled `KRt_from_projection` keeps the caller's precision as before.
