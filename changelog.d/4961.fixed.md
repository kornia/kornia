A weight of exactly 0, the documented way to drop a correspondence, now gets its derivative from above in `find_fundamental`, `run_8point` and the weighted `normalize_points`, and so in the weighted `find_homography_dlt` and `find_homography_lines_dlt`, on every supported torch. `find_fundamental` and `run_8point` used to return `NaN` for it on torch 2.5.1 and 2.9.1, and on torch 2.14 `clamp_min(0)` masked the gradient to 0 in `run_8point` and dropped the Hartley-normalization part of it in `normalize_points`.
