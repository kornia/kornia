`RandomResizedCrop`'s generator (`ResizedCropGenerator`) and `CropGenerator` now compute crop sizes and corners in float32 for a float16 or bfloat16 range or size and cast the result once. With a float16 `scale` or `ratio` tensor, a candidate area of 65520 pixels or more overflowed to inf and was rejected: crops were drawn only from candidates smaller than about 256 x 256 pixels, and when none of the 10 candidates was that small (every draw at 1024 x 1024 with the default scale) the crop fell back to the whole image. Tuple ranges were not affected; bfloat16 rounded the area to 256-pixel steps and the corners by up to a pixel. `PerspectiveGenerator` and `PerspectiveGenerator3D` offset their corners in float32 and round them once (they were up to two ulps off), and `adjust_hue` and `adjust_saturation`, with `RandomHue` and `RandomSaturation`, adjust a float16 or bfloat16 image in float32 and round once, instead of an HSV round trip in half precision that was up to 7.5 eps off even at a zero shift or a factor of 1. float32 and float64 results are unchanged.
