`RandomResizedCrop`'s generator (`ResizedCropGenerator`) and `CropGenerator` now compute crop sizes and corners in float32 for a float16 or bfloat16 range or size and cast the result once. A float16 candidate area of 256 x 256 pixels or more overflowed to inf, so on large float16 images every candidate was rejected and the crop fell back to the whole image; bfloat16 rounded the area to 256-pixel steps and the corners by up to a pixel. `PerspectiveGenerator` and `PerspectiveGenerator3D` offset their corners in float32 and round them once (they were up to two ulps off), and `adjust_hue` and `adjust_saturation`, with `RandomHue` and `RandomSaturation`, adjust a float16 or bfloat16 image in float32 and round once, instead of an HSV round trip in half precision that was up to 7.5 eps off even at a zero shift or a factor of 1. float32 and float64 results are unchanged.
