`RandomResizedCrop`'s generator (`ResizedCropGenerator`) and `CropGenerator` now compute crop sizes and corners in float32 for a float16 or bfloat16 range or size and cast the result once. With a float16 `scale` or `ratio` tensor, a candidate area of 65520 pixels or more overflowed to inf and was rejected: crops were drawn only from candidates smaller than about 256 x 256 pixels, and when none of the 10 candidates was that small (every draw at 1024 x 1024 with the default scale) the crop fell back to the whole image. Tuple ranges were not affected; bfloat16 rounded the area to 256-pixel steps and moved corners past 256 by up to a few pixels. `PerspectiveGenerator` and `PerspectiveGenerator3D` offset their corners in float32 and round them once (they were up to two ulps off), and `adjust_hue` and `adjust_saturation`, with `RandomHue` and `RandomSaturation`, adjust a float16 or bfloat16 image in float32 and round once, instead of an HSV round trip in half precision that was up to 7.5 eps off even at a zero shift or a factor of 1. Their gradients for a half image are now accurate as well: the factor gradient of `adjust_hue` was 5 to 17% off. The float32 hue path costs more than the half one did: float16 `adjust_hue` on CUDA takes about 2.4 times as long (6.4 ms against 2.6 ms for 32 x 3 x 512 x 512), and float16 `ColorJiggle` about 1.6 times; float16 `adjust_saturation` got faster. float32 and float64 results are unchanged.
