`convert_affinematrix_to_homography` and `convert_affinematrix_to_homography3d` now reject integer and bool input up front with a `ValueError` naming the dtype, as `Rt_to_matrix4x4` does; before, it failed with a bare `RuntimeError` from the in-place `+= 1.0` on the padded homography. `ARKitQTVecs_to_ColmapQTVecs` now rejects an integer, bool or complex `qvec` and a complex `tvec` with a `TypeCheckError` naming the argument, as `ColmapQTVecs_to_ARKitQTVecs` does; before, they failed inside `linalg.vector_norm` or a comparison. An integer `tvec` with a floating `qvec` still promotes and returns the floating result.
