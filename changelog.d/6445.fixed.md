`So3 * points` and `Se3 * points`, and so `NamedPose.transform_points` for a 3D pose, now accept an `(N, 3)` point set with an unbatched pose, as `Se2` does for `(N, 2)`. They used to raise in `torch.linalg.cross`. A point layout outside the contract of either group, such as a batch of poses with a point set `(B, N, 3)` or `(B, N, 2)`, or `B` poses with `N` points, now raises a `ValueError` that names the accepted layouts, instead of a `torch.linalg.cross` or broadcast error, or `Se2`'s "Invalid translation shape".
