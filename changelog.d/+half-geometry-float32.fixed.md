More geometry operations compute `float16` and `bfloat16` input in `float32` and round the result once, as the warps already did; `float32` and `float64` results are unchanged. The rotation conversions between axis-angle vectors, quaternions, rotation matrices and Euler angles were up to 5 eps off (177 eps for `rotation_matrix_to_quaternion` in `bfloat16`) and are now correctly rounded. `get_rotation_matrix2d`, `angle_to_rotation_matrix` and `get_projective_transform` return exact zeros and ones for a 90 degree turn instead of `4.8e-4`, and no longer raise `Unexpected floating ScalarType in at::autocast::prioritize` for a `float16` angle inside a CPU `bfloat16` autocast region, which broke `RandomAffine` and the augmentation containers there, and `normalize_homography`, `denormalize_homography` and their 3D forms return the identity for equal sizes. `crop_and_resize` and `crop_by_boxes` keep the solved homography in `float32`, which was up to 0.875 off at a value of 30 for a `bfloat16` trapezoid crop. `cam2pixel` no longer returns `nan` once a homogeneous numerator such as `fx * x + cx * z` exceeds the `float16` range (a 500 px focal length at a depth of 132), `distort_points_kannala_brandt` no longer overflows beyond a normalized radius of 256, and a `float16` `Q` of `StereoCamera` no longer becomes `inf` where its entries fit, as for a 707 px focal length and a 0.12 baseline.
