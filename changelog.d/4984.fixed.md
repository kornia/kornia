`RandomRGBShift` now accepts a `(low, high)` pair (a tuple, a list or a 1-D tensor) for each `*_shift_limit` as the sampling range, as `albumentations.RGBShift` does, instead of failing at construction with a raw `TypeError` from negating the tuple. A number is still a non-negative half-width sampled from `[-limit, limit]`.
