`copy.deepcopy` of an augmentation, or of a container such as `AugmentationSequential` or `ManyToOneAugmentationDispather`, no longer raises `RuntimeError: Only Tensors created explicitly by the user (graph leaves) support the deepcopy protocol` when a sampling range is a learnable `nn.Parameter`, before or after a forward. `torch.optim.swa_utils.AveragedModel` and other EMA copies of such modules now work. A pickled or `torch.save`d copy used to draw from detached copies of the range, so its own parameter never received a gradient; every copy now samples from its own parameter, which gets the gradients, while the original keeps its own. Copies draw the same values as the original under the same seed, and the drawn parameters a copy inherits from the last forward are kept, detached. `RandomRGBShift` also accepts a 0-d float64 tensor limit such as `torch.tensor(0.3, dtype=torch.float64)`, which used to be reported out of its own bounds.
