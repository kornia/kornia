`copy.deepcopy` of an augmentation whose sampling range is a learnable `nn.Parameter`, or of a container such as `AugmentationSequential` or `ManyToOneAugmentationDispather` that holds one, no longer raises `RuntimeError: Only Tensors created explicitly by the user (graph leaves) support the deepcopy protocol`, before or after a forward. `torch.optim.swa_utils.AveragedModel` and other EMA copies of such modules now work. A pickled or `torch.save`d copy used to draw from detached copies of the range, so its own parameter never received a gradient; every copy now samples from its own parameter, which gets the gradients, while the original keeps its own. Copies draw the same values as the original under the same seed, and the drawn parameters a copy inherits from the last forward are kept, detached. `RandomBrightness`, `RandomContrast`, `RandomSaturation` and `RandomHue` now keep a learnable range as the parameter that was passed, whatever its device and dtype and also as a 0-d half-width: the module owns it, `.to()` moves it, and a copy gets its own. A parameter on another device or in another dtype than the CPU default used to be replaced by a CPU copy in the default dtype, and a 0-d one by the range derived from it; neither was among the module's parameters, and no copy could take them. Their draws are unchanged. A checkpoint saved from one of these four classes with a 0-d parameter no longer loads strictly, because the generator's `<name>_factor` entry is now that 0-d parameter instead of the derived `(2,)` range. `RandomRGBShift` also accepts a 0-d float64 tensor limit such as `torch.tensor(0.3, dtype=torch.float64)`, which used to be reported out of its own bounds.
