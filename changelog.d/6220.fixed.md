`kornia.metrics.accuracy` no longer reports accuracies above 100 % when `target` holds a different number of samples than `pred`: a one-entry `target` was compared with every row of `pred` and the count divided by 1, so `accuracy(torch.tensor([[0.0, 1.0], [0.0, 1.0]]), torch.tensor([1]))` returned `[tensor(200.)]`. Such calls now raise `ShapeError`, as do other malformed `pred` and `target` shapes. `topk` entries must be positive integers (Python or NumPy integers or integer tensors, not bools): a `k` of 0 used to report 0 %, and a negative `k` counted from the other end. A `pred` or `target` that is not a tensor now raises `TypeCheckError`, and tensors on different devices raise `DeviceError`.
