`StereoCamera.Q` of a `float16` or `bfloat16` rig is now `float32` instead of the rig's dtype, and `reproject_disparity_to_3D` with that `Q` computes in `float32` and returns the disparity dtype. A real rig's `Q[2, 3] = fx * fy * baseline` is past the `float16` range (`-5.4e6` for a 721 px focal length and a 0.54 m baseline), so a `float16` `Q` was `inf` and the reprojected points were wrong. `float32`, `float64` and integer rigs are unchanged.
