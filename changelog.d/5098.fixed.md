`jpeg_codec_differentiable`, `JPEGCodecDifferentiable` and `RandomJPEG` now keep the DCT basis cache out of `torch.compile`, `torch.export` and dynamo ONNX tracing, and build eager cache entries outside inference mode. A first inference-mode call no longer breaks later image-gradient computations. Exporting the codec no longer leaves cached fake or functional tensors that break subsequent eager calls. Eager calls still reuse the basis per dtype and device.
