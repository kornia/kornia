`kornia.models.sam3` is removed: `import kornia.models.sam3` raises `ModuleNotFoundError`. It shipped in v0.8.3 and v0.9.0rc1 as randomly initialised Phase 1–2 modules of #3406 (an image encoder that was a plain single-scale ViT, and a prompt encoder and mask decoder whose masks ignored point and box prompts), with no weights and no loader. SAM 3 is a different model under Meta's SAM License; a port would be a separate decision.
