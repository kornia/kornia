`match_mnn`, `match_snn` and `match_smnn` wait for the device once per call, where they waited two, three and nine
times: each selects its matches with one `torch.nonzero` instead of indexing several tensors with a boolean mask, and
`match_smnn` runs both ratio tests and the mutual check on one distance matrix instead of calling `match_snn` both
ways. On CUDA (RTX 4090, 2,048 to 4,096 descriptors per image), `match_smnn` is 1.4-1.9x faster per pair, `match_snn`
1.2-1.3x and `match_mnn` up to 1.1x. Outputs are unchanged.
