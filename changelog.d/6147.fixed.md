`decompose_essential_matrix`, `decompose_essential_matrix_no_svd`, `motion_from_essential`, `motion_from_essential_choose_solution` and `project_to_essential` run with autocast disabled, as the solvers of #6072 do, so a caller's `torch.autocast` region no longer changes their result. Under bfloat16 autocast a float32 essential matrix was decomposed with its matrix products in bfloat16: the rotations came back in bfloat16, about `1.6e-3` off and no longer orthogonal, next to a float32 translation, and `motion_from_essential_choose_solution` triangulated its points with bfloat16 projection matrices, which moved them by up to `0.2` at depths of 5 to 9. `motion_from_essential_choose_solution` now casts `K1` and `K2` to the dtype of `E_mat`, so half-precision intrinsics with a float32 matrix, which autocast used to cast to one dtype, still run, and run outside autocast too, where they raised. Any other call made outside autocast is unchanged, and a TorchScript-compiled call keeps the products in the caller's precision as before.
