`confusion_matrix`, `mean_iou` and `mean_iou_bbox` now trace under `torch.compile(fullgraph=True)` on torch 2.14: their data-dependent label-range and box checks are skipped wherever `torch.compile` traces them, as they already were under `torch.export`. Eager calls still raise on an out-of-range label or an invalid box; a compiled `mean_iou_bbox` now gives an invalid box an IoU of 0 or NaN instead of raising.
