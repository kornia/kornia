`kornia.enhance.solarize` applies a one-element `thresholds` or `additions` tensor, such as `torch.tensor([0.5])`, to the whole batch, as its docstring says and as a 0-d tensor does. It used to read a `(1,)` tensor as one value per sample and raise `AssertionError` unless the batch, or for an unbatched `(C, H, W)` image the channel count, was 1; a `(1, 1)` tensor always raised.
