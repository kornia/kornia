`fit_plane` returns a finite gradient when the two in-plane singular values of the centred points are equal, as for the corners of a square checkerboard. torch's SVD backward divides by the difference of every pair of squared singular values, so for those points, such as a 5 x 5 or 7 x 7 board posed in 3D, every entry of the gradient was NaN, although the plane was correct. The normal's value is unchanged; its gradient is taken from the adjugate of `X^T X - s_min^2 I`, which equals the SVD's gradient wherever that is finite.
