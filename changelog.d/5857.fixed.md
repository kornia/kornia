`filter2d`, `filter2d_separable` and `filter3d` accept an empty batch with an empty per-sample kernel, so `gaussian_blur2d` with a `(0, 2)` sigma, `motion_blur`, `motion_blur3d`, `RandomGaussianBlur` and fixed-size `RandomMotionBlur` and `RandomMotionBlur3D` return an empty batch instead of raising `RuntimeError: cannot reshape tensor of 0 elements`. Ranged motion-kernel sampling remains outside this fix. Part of #4429.
