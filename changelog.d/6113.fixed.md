`ScaleSpaceDetector`, `conv_quad_interp3d` / `ConvQuadInterp3d` and `iterative_quad_interp3d` / `IterativeQuadInterp3d` no longer return an all-NaN `float32` image gradient for a flat or nearly flat image. On such an image the Hessian entries of the sub-pixel fit are rounding noise of about 1e-15: their determinants reached float32's smallest subnormal, the relative floor `1e-7 * m**3` underflowed to 0, and the backward of the 3x3 Cramer solve overflowed, so a constant, a ramp, or a 0.5 image with 1e-6 or 1e-4 noise gave NaN at every pixel. The solve now scales each system by the power of two that brings its largest Hessian entry to about 1. The shifts of those tiny systems were also inaccurate (up to 190 % off a `float64` solve of the same system, now within 4e-6), so the LAFs of a nearly flat `float32` image can move by up to about 3e-5. Every other output and gradient is unchanged bit for bit.
