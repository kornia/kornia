Auto-augment operations with a magnitude (`Brightness`, `Rotate`, `ShearX`, `Posterize`, `TranslateX` and the rest) now run after `.to(device)`: `magnitude_range` reads the generator's current range, and the magnitude's factor is placed on the magnitude's device. `RandAugment` computes its magnitudes on the range's device, so a seeded `RandAugment(...).to("cuda")` draws the signs of `rotate` and `shear` from the CUDA generator.
