Auto-augment operations with a magnitude (`Brightness`, `Rotate`, `ShearX`, `Posterize`, `TranslateX` and the rest) now run after `.to(device)`: `magnitude_range` reads the generator's current range, and the magnitude's factor is placed on the magnitude's device. `RandAugment` computes its magnitudes on the range's device, so a seeded `RandAugment` moved to an accelerator draws the signs of `rotate` and `shear` from that device's generator, and `.to(torch.float16)` or `.to(torch.bfloat16)` computes its magnitudes from the range in that dtype, a relative change of at most 2.6e-3.
