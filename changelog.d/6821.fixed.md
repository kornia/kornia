Compute float16 bilateral and joint bilateral window arithmetic and tensor sigmas in float32, preserving the output dtype while avoiding inverse-variance overflow for positive color sigmas representable in float16 and lost weights from squared-difference underflow. Arbitrarily small wider color sigmas can still exceed float32's forward or backward range. Spatial kernels whose nonzero offsets all have zero weight retain the impulse limit's zero sigma gradient. Eager inference reuses its color-difference buffer; gradient-recorded window temporaries use four bytes per value instead of two.
