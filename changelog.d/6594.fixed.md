The `[0, 1]` clamp of the `kornia.enhance` intensity ops, of the augmentations built on them, and of the linear and Gaussian illumination, plasma and snow augmentations now uses the one-sided derivative from inside the range at a result exactly on `0` or `1`, on every torch version. That derivative was 0 on torch 2.14 and later and nonzero on earlier releases: `adjust_brightness(x, 0.0)`, for example, returned its input with a zero gradient at black and white pixels. Plain `torch.clamp` still gives 0 on torch 2.14 and later; this is a Kornia convention, and a graph captured by `torch.jit.trace` or `torch.export` keeps the plain clamp. Values are unchanged, and forward-mode `adjust_gamma` now gives 0 at a black pixel for a gamma between 0 and 1 on every release.
