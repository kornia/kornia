When `raise_if_not_floating_point=False`, the `Keypoints`, `Keypoints3D`, `Boxes`, `Boxes3D` and `VideoBoxes` constructors cast integer tensor coordinates to `float32`, or to `float64` when that is the default dtype; they still reject integer tensors by default. `Boxes.from_tensor`, `Boxes3D.from_tensor` and `VideoBoxes.from_tensor` accept integer input and use the same dtype promotion. `Boxes` used to cast integers to the default dtype, which under a `float16` or `bfloat16` default moved coordinates above 2048 or 256; `Keypoints` used to cast to `float32` even under a `float64` default.
