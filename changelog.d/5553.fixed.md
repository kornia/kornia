`ssim`, `ssim3d`, `SSIM`, `SSIM3D`, `ssim_loss`, `SSIMLoss`, `ssim3d_loss` and `SSIM3DLoss` now validate their
`padding` argument case-insensitively, as the filters do (#5537). `'VALID'` and `'Valid'` crop like `'valid'`, and any
other value raises `Invalid padding mode` naming the accepted options. Previously every string other than
`'valid'` silently returned the `'same'` map.
