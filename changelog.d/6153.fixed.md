`depth_to_3d`, `depth_to_3d_v2`, `warp_frame_depth` and `depth_to_normals` now build the pixel grid in the promoted dtype of `depth` and `camera_matrix` instead of in the dtype of `depth`. In 0.9.0, `uint8` and `int8` depth wrapped the column index past 255 and 127, and `uint16` and `uint32` depth raised `NotImplementedError`, in all four. In the first three, a bfloat16 depth map with float32 intrinsics also rounded the column index, putting `X` up to 2 px off at 640 px wide; float16 depth did so past 2048 px.
