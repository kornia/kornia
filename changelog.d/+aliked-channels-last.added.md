`ALIKED.extract_dense_map` takes a keyword-only `channels_last` flag, `False` by default, which returns the feature map with the strides of `torch.channels_last`. `ALIKED.forward` and `ALIKED.forward_laf` use it for a float32 or float64 image on CPU, where normalising the map and sampling it for the descriptors are several times faster: on an Apple M1 the ALIKED-n16 forward is 1.37x faster at 640 x 480 and 1.22x at 1024 x 768. Keypoints, scores and LAFs are unchanged, and descriptors change by rounding (at most 2.5e-6). MPS and CUDA keep the previous layout, as do float16 and bfloat16 on CPU, where `grid_sample` returns NaN for a channels-last input.
