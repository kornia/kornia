`warp_image_tps` now provides an opt-in corrected output grid through
`use_correct_grid=True`, and `RandomThinPlateSpline` forwards the option. The
default remains the legacy corner-aligned grid for now and emits a deprecation
warning when `align_corners=False`; a future minor release will switch the
default to the corrected `align_corners`-consistent grid.

Reduced-precision TPS parameters are computed in float32 and returned in the
requested dtype to avoid float16 NaNs and backend-dependent interpolation drift.
