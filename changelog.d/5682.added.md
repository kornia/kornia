The docstrings of `shear`, `Shear`, `Affine`, `get_affine_matrix2d`, `get_shear_matrix2d` and `RandomAffine` now say what their shear arguments mean: raw factors about pixel (0, 0) for `shear` and `Shear`, and angles in radians about the centre, sheared by their negative tangent, for `Affine`, the two matrix helpers and `RandomAffine` (in degrees there). That two entry points read `shear` differently is a known defect (#5661).
