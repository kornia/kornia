`sampson_epipolar_distance`, `symmetrical_epipolar_distance`, `left_to_right_epipolar_distance` and `right_to_left_epipolar_distance` no longer compute their matrix products in half precision inside a caller's `torch.autocast` region. With float32 inputs on a pixel-scale scene, that had put the distances up to 85% off in float16 and up to 2.9 times off in bfloat16 on CUDA, and in the one-way distances on the CPU too. float16 and bfloat16 inputs are now computed in float32 and the distance is rounded once to their dtype, where the half-precision computation was up to 5.6% off in float16 and 27 times too large in bfloat16. float32 and float64 results are unchanged. Under autocast, the output dtype is now that of the same call without autocast. This changes some calls that used to take their dtype from autocast. On CUDA, float16 and bfloat16 inputs used to return float32 there. On the CPU, the one-way distances used to return the autocast dtype: bfloat16 points with a float32 `Fm` in a bfloat16 region now return float32. TorchScript calls, and Inductor-compiled code on MPS, which ignores the nested autocast region (pytorch/pytorch#201630), keep the caller's precision.
