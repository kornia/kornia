`adjust_gamma` and `AdjustGamma` now raise `ValueError` when `gamma` or `gain` has more dimensions than the image, the same error `adjust_brightness`, `adjust_contrast`, `adjust_saturation` and `adjust_hue` raise for such a `factor` (#4720). Previously the over-ranked factor silently broadcast the image up to a larger output shape: a `(3, 4, 4)` image with a `(2, 1, 1, 1)` `gamma` returned a `(2, 3, 4, 4)` tensor.
