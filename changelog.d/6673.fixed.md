On MPS with a float64 default dtype, `RandomAffine` and `RandomPerspective` return their sampled parameters, and a float64 CPU kernel passed to `filter2d`, `filter3d` or `fft_conv` with an MPS image gets its gradient. A copy between MPS and a float64 CPU tensor now casts on the CPU side. Done in one step, a copy from MPS to a float64 CPU tensor and the backward of a copy the other way raise on torch 2.15, and on torch 2.14 they leave the result unwritten. Here that came out as zeros: every angle, scale, shear and translation 0, no perspective distortion, a zero kernel gradient.
