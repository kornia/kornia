`ImageStitcher` now raises `RuntimeError` naming the sample and its match count when a sample has fewer than 4 matches. Before, a sample with 1 to 3 matches raised `ValueError` from RANSAC (`estimator="ransac"`) or `AssertionError` from `find_homography_dlt` (`"vanilla"`), and a batch with an unmatched sample failed later with a `grid_sampler` `RuntimeError`.
