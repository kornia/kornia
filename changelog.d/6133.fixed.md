`AugmentationSequential`, `ImageSequential` and `VideoSequential` now track the size that `PadTo`, and `RandomMosaic` with an `output_size`, return. Every later child used to draw its parameters for the canvas before them, so the pipeline differed from applying the children one at a time. An integer-size `Resize`, `LongestMaxSize` or `SmallestMaxSize` returned the wrong size: `PadTo((40, 40))` then `Resize(16)` gave a `(16, 26)` image instead of `(16, 16)`. A resample-mode crop, `RandomCrop`, `RandomResizedCrop`, `RandomAffine`, `RandomPerspective` and `RandomErasing` used the old size for their windows, centres and corners, `RandomElasticTransform` raised a size-mismatch `RuntimeError`, and a later `Resize`, slice-mode `CenterCrop` or `RandomHorizontalFlip` moved boxes and keypoints for the old canvas. The resample-mode crop was also wrong under `torch.export`.
