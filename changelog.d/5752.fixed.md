`warp_perspective` gives the same image and homography gradient for `s * M` as for `M` when `s` is very large or
very small. For a homography with entries between 0.01 and 1, `1e15 * M` returned NaN images and `1e-12 * M` NaN
gradients in float32, and `1e110 * M` and `1e-110 * M` returned NaN images in float64. Under `torch.jit.trace`
(float32 and float64) and the legacy ONNX exporter, `1e8 * M` already sampled the wrong pixels without any NaN. A
matrix whose nonzero entries are all above 1 or all below 1 is now divided by a detached scale before the warp,
which changes its results at rounding level; any other matrix, such as one with `M[2, 2] = 1`, gives bit-identical
results.
