`sampson_epipolar_distance`, `symmetrical_epipolar_distance`, `left_to_right_epipolar_distance` and `right_to_left_epipolar_distance` no longer compute their matrix products in half precision inside a caller's `torch.autocast` region. With float32 inputs on a pixel-scale scene, that had put the distances up to 85% off in float16 and up to 2.9 times off in bfloat16 on CUDA, and in the one-way distances on the CPU too. float16 and bfloat16 inputs are now computed in float32 and the distance is rounded once to their dtype, where the half-precision computation was up to 5.6% off in float16 and 27 times too large in bfloat16. float32 and float64 results are unchanged. TorchScript calls keep the caller's precision.
