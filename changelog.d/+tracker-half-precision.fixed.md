`HomographyTracker` tracks with `float16` and `bfloat16` matchers. The second frame raised: in `warp_perspective`, which got the half frame with the float32 inverse of the first frame's float32 homography, or in `linalg.inv` for a half `previous_homography`. The prior is now inverted and the frame warped in float32, and the fast matcher gets the warped frame in the frame's dtype. `no_match()` and every homography the tracker returns or stores are float32 for a half target and have the target's dtype otherwise, whatever the dtype of the frame, of the matcher's keypoints or of a `previous_homography` assigned from outside. `no_match()` returned the half dtype, and a homography found by matching had the keypoints' dtype: a `float64` tracker whose matcher returns float32 keypoints returned float32 homographies, and a float32 tracker given float64 frames, by a matcher that returns keypoints in the frame's dtype, returned float64 ones. A float32 tracker whose matcher returns float64 keypoints, or with a float64 `previous_homography` assigned from outside, raised in `track_next_frame`.
