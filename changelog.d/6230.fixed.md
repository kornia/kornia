`dice_loss` and `DiceLoss` raise `TypeCheckError`, a `TypeError`, when `target` is not a tensor; it used to be an `AttributeError`, or a misleading device-mismatch `ValueError` for a NumPy array. They raise `ValueError` when `eps` is a negative Python `int` or `float` (`DiceLoss` at construction); such an `eps` used to be accepted: `eps=-1e-8` gave the `eps=0` loss, and `eps=-inf` gave 1. A tensor `eps`, including one per class, is not checked and works as before. `DiceLoss` also rejects an unknown `average` when it is created instead of at its first call.
