On CUDA, `nms2d` and `NonMaximaSuppression2d` with a `(5, 5)` or `(7, 7)` window take the separable max-pool path instead of their 24- or 48-comparison chains for floating-point maps, with the same masks. On an RTX 4090 the suppression is 2-6x faster for 480 x 640 to 960 x 1280 maps, and the extraction of `XFeat`, `DISK` and `ALIKED`, which suppress with a 5 x 5 window, is 3-5% faster. CPU, MPS and integer maps keep the comparison chains: they are faster on CPU, and before torch 2.16 MPS `max_pool2d` mishandles nan and -inf.
