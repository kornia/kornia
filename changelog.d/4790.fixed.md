`solve_pnp_dlt` validation errors now name the offending argument (for example "world_points must hold at least 6 points (N >= 6), got N = 5.") instead of a bare "Validation condition failed". The convention blocks of `kornia.geometry.bbox`, `kornia.geometry.boxes`, `kornia.geometry.depth`, `kornia.geometry.camera`, `kornia.geometry.calibration` and `kornia.sensors.camera` are trimmed to kornia's own contract, with the pixel-centre convention stated once on `PinholeCamera`, and a `torch.where:` typo for "where:" is fixed in several docstrings.
