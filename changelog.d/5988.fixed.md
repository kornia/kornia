`image_histogram2d` now returns float32 counts and pdf for float16 and bfloat16 images, with automatic centers or centers of the image dtype, instead of the image dtype; cast the result if you need the image dtype. In float16 a bin count above 65504, such as the black half of a 512x512 image, used to become `inf` and its pdf `NaN`, and bfloat16 counts were rounded (66048 instead of 65998). The 1-D `histogram` now evaluates float16 and bfloat16 kernels in float32 and still returns the input dtype: at 65536 samples, 64 bins and bandwidth 0.01 its float16 gradient had a 34 % relative error against float64 and now follows it.
