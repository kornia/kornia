`transform_points` now divides every nonzero homogeneous coordinate directly, so finite Euclidean points stay the
same for nonzero scalar multiples of a projective matrix whose homogeneous products remain representable. The same
change reaches `warp_grid`, `warp_grid3d`, `homography_warp` and `homography_warp3d` through those grids, and the
traced `warp_perspective` grid. This replaces the undivided numerator returned when a scaled `w` was smaller than
`1e-8`. Direct division can change projective results with `w != 1` at rounding level; affine results with `w = 1`
remain bitwise unchanged.
