`RANSAC` gains opt-in `degensac=True` (off by default), DEGENSAC's dominant-plane recovery for seven-point fundamental matrices (Chum, Werner and Matas, CVPR 2005), following Chum's implementation in pydegensac: record-setting seven-point models are tested for five or more correspondences related by one homography, and a degenerate one hands that homography to a plane-and-parallax search over the correspondences off the plane. The new `kornia.geometry.homography.sampson_homography_distance` returns the Sampson distance of correspondences to homographies, the first-order geometric error over both images (Hartley and Zisserman, section 4.2.6): unlike `oneway_transfer_error`, it accounts for noise in both images and does not depend on the scale of `H`, and an exact correspondence scores 0.
