`AugmentationSequential` now repeats a single mask (batch 1, or one `(C, H, W)` mask) over a batched image, so each sample's own transform applies to its copy and the output mask has the image's batch size. Previously the mask was transformed with sample 0's parameters only (`RandomHorizontalFlip`, `RandomCrop`) or a per-sample warp such as `RandomAffine` raised `grid_sampler(): expected grid and input to have same batch size`, and the output kept batch 1.
