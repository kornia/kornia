`AugmentationSequential` now repeats a single tensor mask, `(H, W)`, `(1, H, W)`, `(1, C, H, W)` or one `(C, H, W)` mask, to the batch of a `(B, C, H, W)` image before the first child, so each sample's own parameters apply to its copy and the mask comes back with batch `B`. Previously the result depended on the child: per-sample warps such as `RandomAffine`, `RandomPerspective` and `RandomElasticTransform` raised `grid_sampler(): expected grid and input to have same batch size`, `RandomTransplantation` refused the mask, and flips, crops, resizes and color children such as `ColorJiggle` returned it at batch 1, with flips and crops applying sample 0's parameters only, so it did not match images 1 to `B - 1`. For example, with a batch-2 image a `(1, 1, H, W)` mask through `RandomHorizontalFlip` used to come back as `(1, 1, H, W)` and now comes back as `(2, 1, H, W)`; under `keepdim=True` a `(1, H, W)` mask used to come back as `(1, H, W)` and an `(H, W)` mask as `(H, W)`, and both now come back as `(2, H, W)`. Mask lists, unbatched images, batch-1 images and masks with one entry per image are unchanged.
