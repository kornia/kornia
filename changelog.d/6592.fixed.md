With `random_apply=True`, or an integer count equal to the number of children, `ImageSequential`, `AugmentationSequential`, `VideoSequential` and `PatchSequential` with `patchwise_apply=False` no longer repeat a child when the weights sum to less than the count, unless the children cannot fill the count. This changes what those calls select for a given seed. This correctness fix ships without a deprecation window.
