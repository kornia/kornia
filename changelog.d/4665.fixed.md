`AugmentationSequential` returns each mask in the dtype of its own argument. It used to cast every mask output to the
dtype of the last mask argument, so an integer semantic mask followed by a boolean one came back boolean and lost its
labels. An empty batch with a mask no longer raises `IndexError`, and a mask placed before the image, in `data_keys` or
dictionary insertion order, is now converted with that call's image dtype instead of the previous call's, so a float64
mask keeps its precision. Masks that already round-tripped correctly are unchanged.
