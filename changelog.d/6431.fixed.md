`cart2pol` no longer returns `nan` for `rho` when `x ** 2 + y ** 2` overflows (a float16 radius of about 256 or more), and no longer loses tiny radii when the square sum underflows or the point is inside the `eps` neighbourhood. Where the square sum is finite and normal, `rho` is unchanged; elsewhere it is computed as a scaled norm, and float16/bfloat16 compute it in float32 and round once. The zero gradient inside the `eps` neighbourhood is unchanged, and the function still exports to ONNX.
