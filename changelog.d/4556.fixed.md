`warp_affine3d`, `warp_perspective3d` and `homography_warp3d` now normalize their homography and
build their sampling grid under the same `align_corners` convention they pass to `grid_sample`.
Previously the 3D normalization was always corner-aligned, so with `align_corners=False` a
whole-voxel translation through `warp_affine3d` was off by 6.67 voxels on a 2x3x4 `arange` volume
and an identity homography through `warp_perspective3d` deviated by 55.125 on a 4x4x4 cube.
`normal_transform_pixel3d`, `normalize_homography3d` and `create_meshgrid3d` gain an
`align_corners` parameter (last, default `True`, so existing calls are bit-identical), mirroring
their 2D counterparts; the `False` mapping is scale `2 / size`, offset `(1 - size) / size`, and
agrees bitwise between eager and graph capture. Fixes #4503.
