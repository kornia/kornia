ONNX exports of `pyrdown`, `PyrDown`, `build_pyramid` and `rescale` with a dynamic height or width now follow the input size instead of returning the output size of the traced input. Where the exported graph computes the size in float32 (`rescale` under both exporters, `pyrdown` under the dynamo exporter), a factor that is not a power of two can be one pixel off eager (`rescale` 0.7 at a side of 90, `pyrdown` 1.1 at a side of 33); the `rescale` modes that stay static are listed in its docstring.
