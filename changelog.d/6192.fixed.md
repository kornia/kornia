`ObjectDetector.to_onnx` exports a detector whose post-processor returns a tuple with one tensor per image for a batch of one image, as it does for a list, and raises a `ValueError` when a `dynamic_axes` or `dynamic_shapes` argument makes that batch dynamic. It used to declare a dynamic batch: the TorchScript exporter's graph then returned only the traced images' detections at a larger batch, and the dynamo exporter of torch 2.9 and later failed with an error that did not name the cause. A tuple of batched outputs, such as `(boxes, scores)`, still exports a dynamic batch.
