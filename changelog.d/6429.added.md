On MPS with torch 2.13 or later, the descriptor matchers compute distances with `torch.cdist` instead of a manual fallback: `match_snn` and `match_smnn` run 1.4-1.9x faster at 6000 descriptors, and unmasked `match_smnn_batched` runs 7.7x faster and needs about a third of the memory at 2 x 3000. Under export the distance fallback broadcasts instead of repeating the squared norms, with the same result, and runs 1.3-1.8x faster.
