`mean_average_precision` now computes integer boxes in float32, returning the AP of their float copies as a float32
tensor, and its input checks raise kornia's error naming the five list lengths or the per-image box, label and score
counts (#5551). Previously integer boxes raised torch's `mean(): could not infer output dtype` and a mismatch raised
a message-less `AssertionError`. Floating boxes are unchanged.
