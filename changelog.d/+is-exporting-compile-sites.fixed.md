`Resize`, `LongestMaxSize` and `SmallestMaxSize` with an int size, `Boxes.from_tensor`, `Boxes.to_mask`, `Boxes3D.from_tensor`, `Boxes3D.to_mask`, `validate_bbox3d` and the 3D box functions that call it (`bbox_to_mask3d`, `infer_bbox_shape3d`, `center_crop3d`, `crop_and_resize3d`, `crop_by_boxes3d`), `StereoCamera`, `So2.from_matrix`, `Se2.from_matrix` and `jpeg_codec_differentiable` / `JPEGCodecDifferentiable` now compile with `torch.compile(fullgraph=True)` on torch 2.14. Their data-dependent input checks and host-side code paths now follow any graph capture, as they already did under `torch.export` and under `torch.compile` on earlier torch releases. A compiled call on torch 2.14 therefore no longer checks for invalid input, as on earlier torch: non-finite or empty boxes, unequal 3D box edges, a stereo rig with differing intrinsics or a zero or reversed baseline, a rotation block that is not of the form `[[a, -b], [b, a]]`, or a JPEG quality outside `[0, 100]`. Eager calls still raise. Under capture, `Boxes.to_mask` and `Boxes3D.to_mask` take their vectorized path, which fills the same mask.
