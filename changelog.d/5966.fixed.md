Float16 and bfloat16 `LoFTR`, `DISK`, `ALIKED`, `ScaleSpaceDetector`, `MultiResolutionDetector` and `SIFTFeatureScaleSpace` (both backends), with `KeyNetDetector` and the `LocalFeature` presets built on them, now return float32 keypoints and LAFs, formed in float32 where they are computed; scores, responses, confidences and descriptors keep the model's dtype. In bfloat16 the coordinates were rounded to a 2 px grid above x = 256 and a 4 px grid above 512, and in float16 to a 0.25 px grid above 256. A half-precision module fed these coordinates directly has to cast them: `LightGlue` now accepts float32 keypoints with a float16 or bfloat16 model (it raised `expected m1 and m2 to have the same dtype`), and `LocalFeatureMatcher` returns the same keypoint and LAF dtype whether or not a pair matches (it returned half keypoints and LAFs when no pair matched and float32 ones otherwise). `ImageStitcher` with a float16 matcher and `estimator="vanilla"` could give a NaN panorama, because the DLT ran on float16 keypoints; it now runs on float32 keypoints.
