Improve float16 and bfloat16 accuracy in Lab/Luv conversions, `normals_to_rgb255`/`rgb255_to_normals` visualization, Gaussian kernels and blur, and Canny by computing intermediates in float32 and returning the input dtype. Canny no longer overflows when squaring gradients of common 255-level float16 inputs, and decoding a zero float16 normal remains finite.
