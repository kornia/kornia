`ScaleSpaceDetector` no longer gives a NaN image gradient for a float16 image with exactly `ScalePyramid`, exactly a `BlobHessian`, `BlobDoG`, `CornerHarris` or `CornerGFTT` response, and a response and refiner holding only scalar parameters and buffers: the image is now detected in float32 with autocast off and its scores are returned in float16, also in an autocast region, where they used to follow the region in some cases. This moves its keypoints closer to the float32 ones. Any other pyramid or response function, a subclass or a `CornerHarris` with a non-scalar `k` included, and a refiner holding non-scalar state, gets the float16 image as before and can still give a NaN gradient, and so does `LAFOrienter`.
