`ScaleSpaceDetector` no longer gives a NaN image gradient for a float16 image with `ScalePyramid`, a `BlobHessian`, `BlobDoG`, `CornerHarris` or `CornerGFTT` response and a refiner without weights: the image is now detected in float32 with autocast off and its scores are returned in float16, which also moves its keypoints closer to the float32 ones. Any other pyramid or response function, and a refiner with weights, gets the float16 image as before and can still give a NaN gradient, and so does `LAFOrienter`.
