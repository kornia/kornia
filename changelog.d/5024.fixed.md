`solve_cubic` evaluates the closed form on each row scaled by an exact power of two to a unit root bound and scales
the roots back, and takes Cardano's `A + B` as `2R / (A^2 + B^2 + Q)` when `Q > 0`, so a tiny leading coefficient
keeps its finite root in float32 and float64 (#4914). The `|R| > 1e-16` floor that returned `-b/3` for `D > 0` rows
with a small `R` is gone, which also solves the #4833 literal. `D <= 0` rows are bit-identical to before; `D > 0` rows
can move at rounding level.
