`CenterCrop`, `RandomCrop`, `center_crop` and `CenterCrop2D` now accept a crop with a size-1 axis. The crop matrix is built as the translation it is, instead of being solved from the box vertices, which returned a matrix of `NaN` because those vertices are collinear. For every other crop the matrix is now the exact translation rather than the solver's approximation of it, so `transform_matrix`, transformed boxes and keypoints, and resampled pixels can differ from before in the last bits in float32, and by more in bfloat16, where the solve was inexact for coordinates above 256.
