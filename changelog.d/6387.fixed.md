Under CPU autocast, `kl_div_loss_2d` took the logarithm of a float16/bfloat16 `pred` in half precision before `F.kl_div` promoted to float32, so two identical bfloat16 heatmaps gave a divergence of about `1e-3` instead of `0`, and other values were off by a similar amount. The logarithm is now taken in float32 there, as CUDA and MPS autocast already did. Results outside autocast and on other devices are unchanged.
