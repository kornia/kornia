`focal_loss` and `FocalLoss` accept a one-element tensor `alpha` (shape `()`, `(1,)` or `(1, 1)`, also one that requires grad) again with `reduction="mean"` or `"sum"`. Since the reduced path of #5997, these raised `TypeError: new_full(): argument 'fill_value' ... must be Number, not Tensor`, while `reduction="none"` accepted them. The loss equals the dense path's, which takes `1 - alpha` in `alpha`'s dtype and does not propagate a gradient to `alpha`.
