`gftt_response` returned a NaN gradient wherever the discriminant `trace**2 - 4 * det` of the structure tensor rounded to exactly 0: on every flat 9 x 9 patch of the image (zero padding, a black border, a saturated region) in every dtype, and in float16 and bfloat16 on many textured pixels too (766 float16 and 3364 bfloat16 pixels of a random `(2, 3, 33, 29)` image, out of 5742). The gradient is now finite, and 0 on flat patches. The discriminant is now computed as `(dx2 - dy2)**2 + 4 * dxy**2`, so the response values also move at rounding level, toward the exact smaller eigenvalue: in float32 the largest deviation from it falls from up to 2e-5 to below 2e-7 of the largest response.
