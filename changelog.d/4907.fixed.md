`enhance.equalize_clahe` and `augmentation.RandomClahe` now raise kornia's `ValueError` for an image
that is not larger than `grid_size` on both axes, naming the smallest image the grid admits. An image
exactly as large as the grid, such as `8 x 8` at the default `grid_size=(8, 8)`, previously passed the
size check and failed inside the reflect padding with torch's raw error. Closes #4783.
