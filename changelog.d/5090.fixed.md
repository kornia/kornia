`rgb_to_grayscale` and `bgr_to_grayscale` on a uint8 image no longer wrap the weighted sum modulo 256 (white came back as 1 and pure red as 180), which also garbled `load_image(..., ImageLoadType.GRAY8)` of a colour file. The uint8 result now matches `cv2.cvtColor(..., cv2.COLOR_RGB2GRAY)` bit for bit.
