`kornia.geometry.transform.crop_by_indices` no longer reads pixels from the opposite edge for a box that starts left of or above the image. The part of the box outside the image is clipped, as past the right or bottom edge, and `shape_compensation` then applies; with `"pad"` the padding goes on the side where the box overflowed. The `torch.compile` path gives the same result. `CenterCrop2D` in its default slice mode now zero-pads a size larger than the input, as its resample mode does, instead of stretching the last row or column.
