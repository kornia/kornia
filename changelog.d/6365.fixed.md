`kornia.feature.match_adalam` and `kornia.feature.adalam.AdalamFilter` no longer repeat sample couples in their local RANSAC. The couple sampler used the size of the last, incomplete row of couples as its first index, so that row repeated couples already drawn instead of continuing with new ones: with the default `ransac_iters=128`, 8 of the 128 hypotheses in each region were duplicates and the 17th-ranked sample was never paired with the 8 best. A region with too few samples for `ransac_iters` distinct couples (16 or fewer at the default) still wraps its sample indices and reuses couples, as before. Matches and their confidences can therefore differ slightly from earlier releases.
