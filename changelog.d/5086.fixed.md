`conv_soft_argmax2d`, `conv_soft_argmax3d`, `ConvSoftArgmax2d` and `ConvSoftArgmax3d` no longer zero-pad the coordinate grid of the window centres. With an even `kernel_size` and `padding = k / 2`, the first and last window on an axis averaged the coordinate 0 of a padded pixel into its centre and reported a point far inside the image (x = 3.5 for a peak at x = 8). Odd windows, and even windows with smaller padding, are unchanged. (#5066)
