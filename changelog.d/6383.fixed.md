`equalize_clahe(..., slow_and_differentiable=True)` now builds its soft-histogram bin centres and bandwidth in float64 for float64 tiles, and in float32 for every other dtype, even when the default dtype is float64. In 0.9.0 both were built in the default dtype (normally float32), so a float64 image kept float32-level error of up to about 3e-5 relative (2.8e-5 measured), and a global float64 default changed every input's result, for example by 7.8e-3 on a bfloat16 image. The fast path is unchanged, and float32, float16 and bfloat16 results under the usual float32 default are bit-identical to 0.9.0.
