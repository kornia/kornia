Assigning a `kornia.geometry.boxes.VideoBoxes` value whose `temporal_channel_size` differs from the target's, `video_boxes[key] = other`, now raises `ValueError`. Previously it succeeded and regrouped the value's frames into videos of the target's length: two 2-frame videos assigned to one 4-frame video were stitched into it, and one 4-frame video assigned to two 2-frame videos was split across them. A plain `Boxes` value is still read as frames of the target's length.
