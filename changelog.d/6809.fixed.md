`kornia.enhance.image_histogram2d` with two or more explicit `centers` and no `bandwidth` now uses the mean gap between the centers as the bandwidth (their spacing when they are evenly spaced), instead of `(max - min) / n_bins`, which counted most of a `[0, 1]` image in every bin. One center, or centers that are all equal, keep `(max - min) / n_bins`.
