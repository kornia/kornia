`psnr`, `psnr_loss` and `PSNRLoss` widen narrow integer tensor data ranges to int64 before squaring, except when the promoted image dtype is float16 or bfloat16, which keeps the existing float32 range arithmetic. Narrow integer ranges previously overflowed: a uint8 range of 255 with an MSE of 1 returned 0 dB instead of 48.1308 dB, and a signed int16 range of 255 returned NaN. Python scalar, int64 tensor and floating tensor ranges keep their behavior.
