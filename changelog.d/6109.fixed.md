`jpeg_codec_differentiable` and `JPEGCodecDifferentiable` run with autocast disabled, so a caller's `torch.autocast`
region no longer changes their output, or their gradients when the backward pass runs after the region. Autocast ran
the codec's matrix products and convolutions in its lower dtype, which rounds pixels scaled to [0, 255], DCT
coefficients and the quality scale too coarsely: on random 64x64 `float32` images at qualities 99, 90, 50 and 10,
outputs were up to 0.109 (CPU `bfloat16` autocast), 0.014 (MPS `float16`) and 0.088 (MPS `bfloat16`) away from the
`float64` codec, against 8.4e-7 outside autocast, which they now match everywhere. A backward pass inside the region,
which `torch.compile` assumes by default, still computes part of the gradient in the autocast dtype.
