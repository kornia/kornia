`KRt_from_projection` and `triangulate_points` run with autocast disabled, as the solvers of #6072 do, so a caller's `torch.autocast` region no longer changes their result. Under CPU autocast `KRt_from_projection` raised `NotImplementedError: "lu_cpu" not implemented` for a float32 projection matrix, and under MPS autocast it reached a half-precision QR. Under MPS autocast `triangulate_points` handed `torch.linalg.eigh` a half-precision matrix and raised, and its `"cofactor"` solver ran in the autocast dtype on every device (up to `7.8e-2` off on the CPU under bfloat16 autocast). Half-precision points under autocast in the other half-precision dtype, which raised `RuntimeError` from autocast's casts, now give the result outside autocast. A call made outside autocast is unchanged, and a TorchScript-compiled `KRt_from_projection` keeps the caller's precision as before.
