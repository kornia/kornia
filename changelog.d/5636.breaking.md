`AugmentationSequential` now repeats a single mask to the batch in two more cases, so each sample's own parameters apply to its copy and the mask comes back with batch `B`: a `(1, T, C, H, W)` mask next to a `(B, T, C, H, W)` video (a `VideoSequential` child), and a mask replayed alone with `params=` and no image, which is repeated to the batch recorded in those params (`forward_input_shape[0]`). Previously both came back at batch 1: flips and crops applied sample 0's parameters only, and `RandomAffine` raised `grid_sampler(): expected grid and input to have same batch size`. For example, with a batch-2 video a `(1, T, 1, H, W)` mask through `RandomHorizontalFlip` used to come back as `(1, T, 1, H, W)` and now comes back as `(2, T, 1, H, W)`. Some inputs that used to run, or to fail late, now raise `ValueError`: a mask that is not 5D next to a video (it used to fail later inside the `VideoSequential` reshape), a 5D mask next to a non-video image, a mask whose frame count differs from the video's, and a replayed mask whose batch is neither 1 nor the recorded batch (`RandomHorizontalFlip` and `RandomCrop` used to run silently with a wrong result). Full-batch masks and 3D-augmentation containers are unchanged. A `VideoSequential` nested inside another container keeps the old behavior.
