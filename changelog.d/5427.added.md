Convention blocks for `spatial_gradient`, `spatial_gradient3d`, `sobel`, `laplacian`, `canny`, `in_range` and `otsu_threshold`: the `(dx, dy)` output layout and default normalisation of the derivative filters, the Laplacian kernel's sign and scale, the unnormalized Sobel magnitude that `canny`'s thresholds are compared with and its tie rule, `in_range`'s inclusive bounds and accepted bound shapes, and the per-plane Otsu threshold read from the upper edge of the split bin. The Filtering section of the Conventions page maps the derivative scales and `canny`'s thresholds to OpenCV and scikit-image.
