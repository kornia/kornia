2D intensity augmentations whose arithmetic assumes unit-range images now clamp inputs to `[0, 1]` before operating, fixing negative-input `RandomGamma` NaNs, platform-dependent `RandomPosterize` conversion and negative `RandomPlanckianJitter` outputs. `RandomEqualize` and `RandomClahe` clamp before their histogram operations. Range-independent augmentations and samples skipped by the probability gate retain their values; `RandomBrightness` and `RandomContrast` retain unclipped arithmetic with `clip_output=False`.
