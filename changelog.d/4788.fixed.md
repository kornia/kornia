`geometry.transform` docstrings: removed the false `crop_by_boxes` note claiming a source box smaller than the destination box raises, and documented four known defects with their issues: `homography_warp(normalized_homography=False)` ignores `mode` and `align_corners` (#4772), `remap` resamples under an identity pixel map at its default `align_corners` (#4504), `warp_perspective` and `warp_perspective3d` warp a `float64` input through a `float32` grid (#4776), and `ScalePyramid` labels later levels below their real blur when `init_sigma` is below the assumed input blur (#4796).
