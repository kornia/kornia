`RandomBoxBlur`, `RandomGaussianBlur` and `RandomSharpness` now raise a `ValueError` naming the class, the kernel, the border type and the input shape when the image is too small for the filter, instead of letting a raw torch padding error out. The sizes they accept are unchanged: the minimum is derived from the wider of `filter2d`'s two pads, which for an even kernel extent is the rear one, so `RandomBoxBlur((4, 4), border_type="reflect")` reports the 3 pixels it needs rather than 2.
