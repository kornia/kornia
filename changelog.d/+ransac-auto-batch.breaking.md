`RANSAC` gains `batch_size="auto"`, now the default, and `max_samples`. Before, every device drew batches of 2048 hypotheses and `max_iter` counted them. Now the budget is `max_samples` if given, otherwise `2048 * max_iter` as before, and the batch is chosen per call: the whole budget in one batch of at most 8192 on CUDA and MPS, and on CPU 256 to 2048 hypotheses for homographies or 128 to 512 for the epipolar models, fewer for more correspondences (`RANSAC.resolve_batch_size`). Pass an integer `batch_size` to keep a fixed batch. Results for a given seed depend on the batch, so default results change on accelerators and on CPU inputs large enough to lower the batch below 2048.
