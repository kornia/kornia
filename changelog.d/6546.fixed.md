`kornia.geometry.transform.rotate` and `scale`, and the `Affine`, `Rotate` and `Scale` modules, build their default center in the dtype of the angle or scale factor, so leaving `center` out gives the same result as passing the image center in that dtype. With parameters in a narrower floating dtype than the image, such as float32 parameters and a float64 image, they used to raise `ValueError` ("Inputs must have same dtype") unless `center` was passed; they now warp in the image dtype, as `warp_affine` does. Parameters that `warp_affine` rejects for the image, such as float64 parameters with a float32 image, still raise, now with its `RuntimeError`, as with an explicit center.
