`denormalize` and `Denormalize` used to accept a `(C,)` mean or std whose length matched dimension -3 of a rank-3 or rank-5 input instead of its channel count, and broadcast a 1-channel input to C channels (a `(2, 1, 4, 2, 2)` input with 4-element statistics returned a `(2, 4, 4, 2, 2)` tensor). They now raise `ValueError` for that input, as `normalize` does.
