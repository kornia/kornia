`kl_div_loss_2d` and `js_div_loss_2d` now raise `NotImplementedError` for a `reduction` outside `'none'`, `'mean'`
and `'sum'`, and check that `pred` and `target` are 4-D `(B, N, H, W)` tensors of the same shape (#5535). Previously
any unknown reduction, including torch's `'batchmean'`, silently returned the sum, B · N times the `'mean'` value;
kornia's `'mean'` is the equivalent of torch's `'batchmean'` here. A `pred` with H and W swapped was reinterpreted
in the layout of `target` instead of raising.
