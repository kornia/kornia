`zca_mean`, `zca_whiten` and `ZCAWhitening.fit` now raise `ValueError` when the sample dimension has fewer than two entries with `unbiased=True` (the default), or none with `unbiased=False`. Previously the covariance was divided by zero, and the result was NaNs, an empty `zca_whiten` output, or a `torch.linalg.LinAlgError` from the SVD, depending on the input shape. Whitening one sample with `unbiased=False` is still supported and returns zeros.
