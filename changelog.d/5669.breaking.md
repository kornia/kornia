For an integer or bool input, `filter2d`, `filter2d_separable`, `filter3d`, `gaussian_blur2d`, `box_blur`,
`laplacian`, `bilateral_blur`, `joint_bilateral_blur` and both `canny` outputs now return float32, on the input's
scale and without rounding or clamping, instead of the input's integer dtype. Floating inputs keep their dtype (#5155).
