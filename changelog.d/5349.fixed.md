`add_weighted` and `AddWeighted` now accept tensor `alpha`, `beta` and `gamma` that broadcast to the input shape, such as a 0-d scalar or a per-image `(B, 1, 1, 1)` weight. Before, a tensor coefficient had to match the input shape exactly, even though the docstring said broadcastable shapes were allowed. Shapes that would change the output shape are still rejected.
