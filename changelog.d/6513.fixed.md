`Se2` and `Se3` now reject non-broadcastable rotation and translation batch shapes at construction time while correctly handling broadcastable poses across `matrix()`, `log()`, `adjoint()`, `inverse()`, point transforms, and indexing. `Quaternion.from_coeffs()` now creates floating-point quaternions with the default dtype for integer inputs and rejects complex coefficients with `ValueError`. `average_quaternions()` now accepts `(M, 1)`-shaped weights in addition to `(M,)`.
