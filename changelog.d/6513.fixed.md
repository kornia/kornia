`Se2` and `Se3` now validate rotation and translation batch compatibility during construction and support broadcast-compatible batch shapes across `matrix()`, `log()`, `adjoint()`, and indexing. `Quaternion.from_coeffs()` now creates floating-point quaternions with the default dtype for integer inputs and rejects complex coefficients with `ValueError`. `average_quaternions()` now accepts `(M, 1)`-shaped weights in addition to `(M,)`.
