`Se2` and `Se3` reject a translation whose batch does not broadcast with the rotation batch when they are built; before, the pose was built and `matrix()` raised a raw shape error later. Their `matrix()`, `log()`, `adjoint()` and indexing now expand a rotation and translation that broadcast, such as one `Se3` rotation with `(N, 3)` translations or a batch of one on either side. `Quaternion.from_coeffs` builds a quaternion of the default floating dtype from integer or `bool` coefficients, which used to give an `int64` or `bool` quaternion, and rejects complex coefficients. `average_quaternions` accepts `(M, 1)` weights, which raised a raw `RuntimeError`.
