`StereoCamera` accepts a rectified pair whose `cx` differs between the two cameras, which is what OpenCV's `stereoRectify` produces without `CALIB_ZERO_DISPARITY`. `Q[3, 3] = fy * (cx_left - cx_right)` is then non-zero, and the reprojection matches OpenCV's. In 0.9.0 such a pair raised a `StereoException` about mismatched parameters. A differing `fx`, `fy` or `cy` still raises, now with a message naming what must match.
