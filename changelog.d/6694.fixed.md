On MPS with a float64 default dtype, `RandomShear`, `RandomTranslate`, `RandomCrop`, `RandomResizedCrop`, `RandomCrop3D`, `RandomPerspective3D` and `ColorJiggle` return their sampled parameters. Their samples now reach the CPU through the same copy as `RandomAffine` and `RandomPerspective`, which casts on the CPU side. Before, they raised on torch 2.15 and came out as zeros or wrong crops on torch 2.14: no shear or translation, brightness, contrast and saturation factors of 0.
