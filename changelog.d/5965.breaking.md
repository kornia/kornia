`ImageStitcher` now raises `RuntimeError` naming the sample and its match count when a sample has fewer than 4 matches (sample 0 when nothing matched), and naming the index when a match's batch index is outside the batch. Before, a sample with 1 to 3 matches, or an unmatched sample ahead of a matched one, raised `ValueError` from RANSAC (`estimator="ransac"`) or `AssertionError` from `find_homography_dlt` (`"vanilla"`); unmatched samples at the end of a batch failed later with a `grid_sampler` `RuntimeError`; a batch without any match raised a `RuntimeError` that named no sample; and a batch index outside the batch raised one of these errors, depending on the indexes. `HomographyTracker.track_next_frame` called without a previous homography (before `forward()` or `match_initial()` found one) now raises kornia's `BaseError` naming the missing previous homography; before, it raised `UnboundLocalError`. It also raises that error when called right after a target is assigned directly, because the previous homography maps the old target's coordinates; before, it tracked the new target with that homography.
