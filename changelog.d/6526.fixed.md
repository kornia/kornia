`fit_line` raises a `ShapeError` when `weights` does not have the shape `(B, N)` of the points, instead of accepting a `(B, 1)` tensor. Such weights broadcast in the weighted sums but not in the normaliser, so the centroid came out as the sum of the points rather than their mean, and they skipped the zero-weight-sum and negative-weight checks: a zero weight gave a NaN line in 2-D and a negative weight was accepted. Weights with another second dimension raised a `RuntimeError` from torch, and a mismatched batch size a bare `BaseError`; both now raise the same `ShapeError`.
