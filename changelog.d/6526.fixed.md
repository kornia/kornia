`fit_line` raises a `ShapeError` when `weights` does not have the shape `(B, N)` of the points, instead of accepting a `(B, 1)` tensor. Such weights broadcast in the weighted sums but not in the normaliser, so the centroid came out wrong (the sum of the points for `D >= 3`, the first point plus the sum of the offsets from it for `D = 2`), and they skipped the zero-weight-sum and negative-weight checks: a zero weight gave a NaN line in 2-D and a negative weight was accepted. Weights with another second dimension raised a `RuntimeError` from torch, and a mismatched batch size a bare `BaseError`; both now raise the same `ShapeError`. The sizes are compared as numbers, so the check also holds for a symbolic `N` under a dynamic `torch.export`.
