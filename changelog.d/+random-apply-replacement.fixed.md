With `random_apply`, `ImageSequential`, `AugmentationSequential`, `PatchSequential` and `VideoSequential` sample with replacement when the drawn count exceeds the number of children with a nonzero weight in `random_apply_weights`, as they already did when it exceeded the sum of the weights. A count above the number of children raised `RuntimeError: cannot sample n_sample > prob_dist.size(-1) samples without replacement` whenever the weights summed to at least the count, for example `random_apply=4` with weights `[7, 2, 1]`. A count above the number of nonzero weights but not of children filled the remaining places with children of weight 0, which the caller had excluded, or with a mix augmentation outside the mix-insertion step.
