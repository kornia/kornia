`gaussian_blur2d` no longer waits for the device when `sigma` is a tensor on CUDA, or on MPS from torch 2.13: its positivity is checked there with `torch._assert_async`. A value that is not positive in such a `sigma` used to raise `BaseError` during the call and now raises `RuntimeError` at the next synchronization, which on CUDA is a device-side assert. A tuple or a CPU tensor `sigma` is still checked during the call.
