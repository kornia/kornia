Fix `kornia.metrics.angle_error_mat` and `kornia.metrics.angle_error_vec` returning exactly `0` for every angle below about 0.03 degrees in float32 (and `180` for 179.99 degrees): the angle is now computed with `atan2(sin, cos)` reading the sine from the skew part of the relative rotation (or the cross product of the vectors), which keeps full precision next to 0 and 180 degrees. The gradient at exactly 0 and 180 degrees is now the finite subgradient 0 on every supported torch version instead of `inf`/`nan` on part of them.
