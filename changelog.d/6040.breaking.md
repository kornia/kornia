`VisualPrompter.predict(keypoints=...)` without `keypoints_labels` raises; the keypoints were ignored and the prediction was the one without prompts. `VisualPrompter.set_image` takes one image, `(3, H, W)` or `(1, 3, H, W)`, and raises for a batch: it accepted `(B, 3, H, W)` with B > 1, after which `predict()` without prompts returned one prediction per image and `predict` with keypoint or `Boxes` prompts raised. Use `Sam.forward` to segment a batch of images with per-image prompts.
