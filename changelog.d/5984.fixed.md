The image and mask inverse of the 2D crops also changed in resample mode. With `align_corners=True`, `CenterCrop` and `RandomCrop` spread a crop with a one-pixel axis over the whole canvas axis, and now paste it back. `RandomResizedCrop` with a one-pixel output or crop axis and corner alignment returned NaN in resample mode, and now samples the crop's first row or column, as slice mode does. `RandomCrop` inverted an oversized crop (larger than the padded canvas) without its scale, returning the top-left corner of the upscaled crop, and now scales it back. Its mask inverse raised `ValueError` when the image `fill` had one value per channel, and now removes the padding without a fill.
