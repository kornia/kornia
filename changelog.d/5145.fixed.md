`LightGlueMatcher("sift")` now gives LightGlue each keypoint's scale and orientation in the convention of the COLMAP keypoints its weights were trained on: the orientation is the negated LAF orientation in (-pi, pi], and the scale is a sixth of the LAF scale, which is sigma for 6-sigma DoG frames such as `SIFTFeatureScaleSpace`'s. It used to pass the LAF scale and kornia's orientation in [0, 2pi). With OpenCV-layout descriptors, correct matches over the eight Oxford affine sequences rise by 1–2.4% for the kornia SIFT pipelines, and by up to 28% on bark, whose pairs rotate.
