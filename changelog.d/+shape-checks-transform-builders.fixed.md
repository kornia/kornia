`warp_grid`, `warp_grid3d`, `homography_warp`, `homography_warp3d`, `HomographyWarper`, `get_translation_matrix2d`, `get_projective_transform` and `get_affine_matrix3d` now raise a `ShapeError` for an input of an unsupported rank instead of misreading it. An unbatched `(3, 3)` or `(4, 4)` homography used to have its first dimension read as the batch, so `warp_grid` returned a batch of 3 grids and `homography_warp` worked for exactly 3 images and failed for any other number; a `(2,)` translation gave two matrices; a `(3,)` scale was read as `(sx, sy, sz)` when the batch was 1 or 3 and failed for any other batch. Add a leading batch dimension. Inputs of the documented shapes, `(B, 3, 3)`, `(B, 1, 3, 3)`, `(B, 2)`, `(B, 3)` and `(B, 1)`, are unchanged.
