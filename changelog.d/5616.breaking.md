With static p=1.0, p_batch=1.0, a batch_prob put into params is now ignored by every handler, in eager and compiled runs (#5585). The image and transform_matrix already ignored it, but inverse and, for augmentations such as the flips and RandomCrop, the mask, box and keypoint handlers honored it, so a sample it deselected was transformed in forward and left alone by inverse. Pass p < 1 to skip samples by hand.
