CropGenerator, ResizedCropGenerator, CropGenerator3D and PatchMixGenerator keep their position sampler in float32 when the augmentation is moved to float16 or bfloat16, so a crop or patch start cannot land one past the last valid position. On MPS, half-precision `torch.rand` returns exactly 1.0 for about 1 in 4096 (float16) or 1 in 512 (bfloat16) draws, which made `RandomCrop(cropping_mode="resample")` blend a zero padding line into the crop and put `RandomCrop3D` boxes and `PatchMix` patches one pixel past the edge. float32 and float64 draws are unchanged; for the same seed, float16 and bfloat16 pipelines now draw some different positions than before.
