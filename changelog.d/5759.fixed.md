`spatial_gradient3d` and `SpatialGradient3d` differentiate an integer or bool volume in float32: uint8 differences no
longer wrap, and the mixed second derivatives of an integer volume are no longer zero.
`get_gaussian_discrete_kernel1d` and `gaussian_discrete` return a floating kernel for an integer `sigma` tensor, as
`gaussian` and `gaussian_discrete_erf` do, instead of casting its taps to that dtype, which truncated them all to 0.
