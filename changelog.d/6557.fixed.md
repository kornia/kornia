`kornia.color.ColorMap` resamples its palette from the first colour of the base to the last, with entry `i` at `i / (num_colors - 1)` along it. With a `num_colors` other than the base length, it used to repeat the end colours when upsampling (`viridis` with 256 colours started and ended with two equal entries) and drop them when downsampling (`viridis` with 8 colours started and ended inside the map). The default 64-colour built-in maps are unchanged.
