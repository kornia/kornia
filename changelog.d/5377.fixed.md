`MS_SSIMLoss` now filters float16 and bfloat16 images in float32 with autocast disabled and returns the loss in the input dtype, as `ssim` and `SSIMLoss` do (#5148). A float16 pair with local means above about 181, such as pixel-range data at `data_range=255.0`, used to give a NaN loss, and bfloat16 was 2.5 % low on such images (12.06 instead of 12.37). A float16 or bfloat16 module renormalises its rounded Gaussian masks, which no longer summed to one. Under `torch.autocast` a float32 input used to be filtered in the autocast dtype (12.04 instead of 12.37 under CPU bfloat16 autocast, NaN under float16) and is now filtered in float32. Images whose dtype differs from the module's, which used to raise torch's dtype-mismatch error, are now accepted: integer and bool images count as the mask dtype, and the loss is returned in the promoted dtype of the two images.
