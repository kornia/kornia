`Boxes`, `VideoBoxes` and `Boxes3D` store boxes as exclusive regions (`width = xmax - xmin`), so `compute_area`, `get_boxes_shape` and `filter_boxes_by_area` give `width * height`, and fractional and normalized `[0, 1]` boxes round-trip through `from_tensor` and `to_tensor`. `transform_boxes` reads its matrix on pixel centres, as kornia's image matrices are: flips, quarter turns and integer translations give the same numbers as before, while scaling, shear and perspective now move boxes with the image instead of shrinking or offsetting them. In `AugmentationSequential` this fixes `Boxes` objects under any key and tensors under `"bbox_xywh"`: a 2x resize maps `[10, 30)` to `[20, 60)` instead of `[20.5, 59.5)`. Tensors under the bare `"bbox"` and `"bbox_xyxy"` keys give the same numbers as before. `RandomMosaic` clips `Boxes` objects and `"bbox_xywh"` boxes at the far image edge, and a box wholly outside the output collapses to zero size.
