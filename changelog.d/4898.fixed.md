`solve_cubic` no longer returns NaN for `float16` cubics whose `Q^3` underflows to 0 while `R` is 0, such as `x^3 - 0.003x` or `x^3 + 0.003x`. Half-precision cubics are now solved in `float32` and the roots are cast back to the input dtype, as `solve_quartic` already did for its Ferrari path. This also fixes the `bfloat16` reference quartic that went through the cubic fallback. (#4856)
