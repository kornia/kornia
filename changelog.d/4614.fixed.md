`LoFTR` fine matching returns finite gradients through `expec_f` in `float16` on torch older than 2.14. The `1e-10` floor under `sqrt` underflows to zero in `float16`, so a peaked heatmap -- what a confident match produces, not a degenerate corner -- sent `sqrt'(0) = inf` back into the features; since `std` is summed into the loss, one such match is enough to make the whole gradient `nan`.
