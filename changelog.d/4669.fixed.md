`solve_quartic` no longer returns values that are not roots of the quartic it was given. When the
resolvent cubic has a near-double root, `solve_cubic` can lose it, and Ferrari then factored a
different polynomial and reported its roots: `(x^2 + 2x + 5)(x^2 + 2x + 5.01)`, which has no real
roots, came back as `[1.0012, -3.0012, 1.0012, -3.0012]` with a residual of 64. Each candidate is now
Newton-polished (two steps, each kept only when it lowers the residual), checked against the quartic
with a `sqrt(eps) / 4` tolerance for its dtype, and replaced with the existing zero placeholder for
"no real root" if it does not satisfy it; a simple root that polishing carried two candidates onto is
reported once. Measured over 100k-row families with `numpy.roots` as ground truth: rows returning a
non-root on the near-square family fall from 47,031 to 0 in float32 and 21,313 to 0 in float64, and
rows missing a genuine root are at or below `main` in every float32 family (429 to 368 with four
real roots, 337 to 196 with two) and within 8 rows of it in float64. Affects float64 as well as
float32. Fixes #4474.
