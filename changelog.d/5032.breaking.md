`homography_warp` with `normalized_homography=False` now passes `mode` and `align_corners` on to `warp_perspective`. Previously that path always sampled with `mode="bilinear"` and `align_corners=True`, whatever the caller passed. A call with the default arguments now uses the signature's `align_corners=False`: with `padding_mode="zeros"` or `"border"` the output moves only by float rounding, but with `padding_mode="reflection"` the out-of-bounds samples change. Pass `align_corners=True` to get the previous output. Callers who passed `mode="nearest"` or `mode="bicubic"` now get that interpolation instead of bilinear.
