Under `torch.no_grad()` or `torch.inference_mode()`, `kornia.feature.aliked.deform_conv2d` builds the `grid_sample` coordinates straight from the offsets, with one fused multiply-add per axis on a zero-offset grid, and computes a single-group convolution with one `torch.baddbmm`. That is about half the kernel launches per call. On CUDA (RTX 4090, torch 2.15) the `ALIKED` forward of a 1024 px image is 8-11 % faster, closing most of the gap to `torchvision.ops.deform_conv2d`; on a single CPU thread it is about 5 % faster. Outputs change by float32 rounding, and half-precision inputs are still sampled in float32.
