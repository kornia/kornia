The bare `"bbox_xyxy"` and `"bbox"` keys of `AugmentationSequential` are aliases of `"bbox_xyxy_index"` and `"bbox_index"` and warn once per process with `BoxFrameWarning`; their transformed coordinates do not change. Boxes under them now follow the pixel-centre frame where the region matters: `RandomMosaic` clips them to `[-0.5, W - 0.5]` instead of `[0, W - 1]`, a box it clips to zero width or height comes back as an all-zero row, and `min_bbox_size` compares the float32 area of half-precision boxes, so a float16 `49 x 51` box no longer passes `min_bbox_size=2500`. A call that reads edge-frame boxes with `"keypoints"`, or pixel-centre boxes with `"keypoints_edge"`, warns once. Dictionary keys `bbox_xyxy_edge`, `bbox_xyxy_index`, `bbox_edge`, `bbox_index` and `keypoints_edge`, read before as a bare key with a suffix, now select the new keys.
