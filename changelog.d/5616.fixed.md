A `batch_prob` put into `params` is now the gate for augmentations with `p=1.0, p_batch=1.0` too (#5585). The image, `transform_matrix` and `RandomCrop`'s padding offset used to ignore it, while `inverse` and the label handlers honored it, so a sample the gate skipped was transformed in `forward` but not restored by `inverse`, and its image, matrix and labels disagreed. They now all skip a deselected sample, as they do for `p < 1`. A mixed gate on an augmentation that changes the sample shape (`Resize`, a shrinking `RandomCrop`) raises `ValueError`, as it already did for `p < 1`, instead of transforming every sample. Gates sampled by the augmentation itself, and `forward` under `torch.compile` or export, behave as before.
