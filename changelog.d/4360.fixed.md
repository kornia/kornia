`kornia.enhance.image_histogram2d` no longer collapses distinct bins together on a `float16` or `bfloat16` image. The auto-built bin centers were created at the image dtype, which cannot represent the bin indices exactly: `bfloat16` lost 63 of 256 centers at the default `n_bins`, and `float16` lost 511 of 2048, so those bins came back numerically identical. The centers are now built at `float32` for half-precision images (a `float64` image keeps `float64`), and a floating-point image still gets its own dtype back. Integer images and explicitly passed `centers` behave exactly as before.
