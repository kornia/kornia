`GaussianBlur2d`, and `UnsharpMask` which blurs with it, now export through the legacy ONNX exporter (`torch.onnx.export(..., dynamo=False)`, the default on torch 2.5.1) with a dynamic batch, height and width. The export used to fail with "Unsupported: ONNX export of convolution for kernel of unknown shape". A per-sample `sigma` keeps a static batch, and its height and width are dynamic too.
