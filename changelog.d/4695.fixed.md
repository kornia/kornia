`RandomTransplantation` no longer misaligns donor labels when one donor's labels are all excluded. The label list was built only for donors that had an eligible label and then zipped positionally against the donors, so a single fully excluded donor shifted every later label onto the wrong donor and dropped the last acceptor — at a batch size of two the whole call became a silent no-op, and at three an acceptor could receive the region chosen for a different donor. An acceptor whose donor has no eligible label is now dropped from `acceptor_indices` and `donor_indices` and its `batch_prob` entry is cleared, so the remaining labels stay aligned with their own donors for every mask dtype, and an empty `selected_labels` follows the mask's dtype and device instead of defaulting to float32 on CPU. Labels are no longer drawn, and the RNG no longer consumed, when the caller supplies a `selection`. The length-check message no longer reads "than images torch.where this augmentation should be applied".
