`TinyViT.from_config("21m", ...)` with the `'in1k_384'` or `'in1k_512'` checkpoint (also reached by `pretrained=True` at `img_size` 384 or more) builds with `drop_path_rate=0.1`, the stochastic depth rate those checkpoints were fine-tuned with (Cream `tiny_vit_21m_384` and `tiny_vit_21m_512`). It used TinyViT-21M's 224-px rate of 0.2. Only training changes: DropPath does nothing in evaluation mode. A `drop_path_rate` argument still overrides the default.
