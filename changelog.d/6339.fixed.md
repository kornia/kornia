`RandomPosterize` now draws `bits` uniformly over the whole numbers of its closed range. The draw used to be a continuous number that was then rounded, which gave the two end points half the probability of interior values: `bits=(0, 8)` drew 0 and 8 about half as often as values 1 through 7 (#6334). The auto-augment `Posterize` op draws through the same generator, so `TrivialAugment`'s posterize draws over `(4, 8)` are now uniform too; `RandAugment` sets the magnitude directly and `AutoAugment`'s posterize bins have fractional bounds, so neither changes. Seeded draws therefore differ from earlier releases. A range with a fractional bound is drawn as before.
