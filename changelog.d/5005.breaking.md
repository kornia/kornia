`So2` squeezes a `(B, 1)` complex number or angle to `(B,)` on entry: `So2(z)`, `So2.exp(theta)` and `So2.hat(theta)`
given a column now return `z` of shape `(B,)`, `matrix()` and `hat()` of shape `(B, 2, 2)`, and `So2 * points` rotates
`(B, 2)` points pairwise into `(B, 2)`. They used to keep the column, so `z` was `(B, 1)`, `matrix()` and `hat()` were
`(B, 1, 2, 2)` (which `vee()` rejects), `So2 * points` broadcast into `(B, B, 2)` with every rotation applied to every
point, and `Se2` rejected the element. `(B,)` and `()` inputs are unchanged. Fixes #4932.
