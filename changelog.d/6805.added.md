`kornia.feature.CLIDD` and `kornia.feature.CLIDDModel` port CLIDD (Cross-Layer Independent Deformable Description, arXiv:2601.09230), a family of nine very small learned local features with 48-, 64- or 128-D descriptors, from 4,252 to 4.4 M parameters. `CLIDD.from_pretrained(variant)` loads the authors' weights, `CLIDD(images)` returns keypoints, scores and descriptors per image, and `CLIDD.match` matches two images by the authors' dual-softmax mutual nearest neighbours. The authors' Triton kernels are optional: the descriptor sampling also runs as `grid_sample` plus a convolution on any device, matching them to float32 rounding, and `use_triton` selects the implementation (by default Triton for the 128-D variants on CUDA from torch 2.7, where it is faster and holds far less memory).
