`focal_loss` and `FocalLoss` accept a one-element tensor `alpha` again with `reduction="mean"` or `"sum"`. Since #5997, a `(1,)` or `(1, 1)` tensor, or a 0-d one that requires grad, raised `TypeError: new_full(): argument 'fill_value' ... must be Number, not Tensor` while `reduction="none"` accepted it, and `torch.compile(fullgraph=True)` failed for every tensor `alpha`. An `alpha` that is not a Python number takes the dense path again, as before #5997; a Python-number `alpha` keeps the reduced path.
