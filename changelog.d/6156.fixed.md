`RandomCrop3D` and `CropGenerator3D` now reject a fixed crop size larger than the input volume for empty batches, consistently with non-empty batches. Crops that fit after padding remain valid. A fixed size is now checked without reading tensor data, so on torch 2.14 `torch.compile(..., fullgraph=True)` of `CropGenerator3D`, and of `RandomCrop3D` with `p=1.0`, no longer fails with a data-dependent branch.
