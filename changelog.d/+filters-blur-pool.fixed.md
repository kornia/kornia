`bilateral_blur`, `joint_bilateral_blur`, `BilateralBlur` and `JointBilateralBlur` reject an even `kernel_size` with kornia's "Kernel size must be an odd integer" error, the modules when they are constructed, instead of failing inside the filter with a raw torch shape error (#5163). `edge_aware_blur_pool2d` and `EdgeAwareBlurPool2D` now accept an even `kernel_size` and keep the input shape, where they also failed with a shape error, because `blur_pool2d` keeps the size for an even kernel. The `ceil_mode` argument of `max_blur_pool2d` and `MaxBlurPool2D` is deprecated (#5165): it went to a stride-1 max pool, where floor and ceil rounding agree, so it never changed the output. Passing it, `True` or `False`, emits a `DeprecationWarning` and leaves the output unchanged; it will be removed in a future release.
