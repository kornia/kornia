Extend the CUDA `torch.compile` constant-transfer workaround to shear, thin-plate spline, erasing, crop, resize, 3D affine/perspective, and illumination/mix parameter generators. Eager execution, `torch.compile`, and `make_fx` build these constants with the same scalar factories, without a host-device copy. Crop operations retain their existing graph breaks.

Fix `RandomShear` and `RandomAffine` sampler migration so scalar and tuple shear ranges honor the requested device and dtype; moving `RandomAffine` with a scalar or two-element `shear` to CUDA used to raise a device-mismatch error. Fix mixed-device sign generation in both linear-illumination generators after moving their samplers. `CenterCrop3D` parameters for an empty batch are now `long` tensors on the requested device, matching non-empty batches, instead of CPU `float32` tensors.
