The GPU acceleration page lists the geometry solvers that need a newer PyTorch on MPS, where older releases lack a linear-algebra kernel and raise `NotImplementedError`: `RANSAC` with the homography and fundamental models and its default `local_optimization="lm"`, and `find_fundamental(method="7POINT")`, need torch 2.7; `find_homography_dlt` with its default `solver="lu"`, `find_homography_dlt_iterated`, `safe_solve_with_mask` and `RANSAC("homography", local_optimization="dlt")` need torch 2.10; `find_fundamental` with its default `method="8POINT"` (2.7 for exactly eight correspondences without weights) and `RANSAC` with the fundamental and essential models and `local_optimization="dlt"` need torch 2.14. `PYTORCH_ENABLE_MPS_FALLBACK=1` runs them on an older torch. The docstrings of these functions point to the table.
