`dice_loss(average="micro", weight=w)` and `DiceLoss(weight=w)` now weight the intersection once, computing the weighted Dice `2 Σ w p t / Σ w (p + t)`. They used to square the weight in the intersection, so a uniform weight scaled the Dice coefficient (a perfect prediction scored -1 with `weight=2`) and non-uniform weights could push the loss below -1. Weighted `"micro"` losses with weights other than 0 and 1 change value. Unweighted, 0/1-weighted and `"macro"` losses are unchanged.
