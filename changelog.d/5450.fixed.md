Made the eager backward pass of `ColorJiggle` and `ColorJitter` with a fixed `order` about 30x faster (about 170 ms to 6 ms with gradients on a `(8, 3, 64, 64)` CPU batch) by selecting each step with `torch.where` instead of `torch.cond`. Every step is now computed even when its range is neutral, so without gradients a fixed order with a step configured off (for example `hue=0`) costs up to about 1.6x more than before.
