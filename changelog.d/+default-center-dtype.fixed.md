`kornia.geometry.transform.rotate` and `scale`, and the `Affine` module, build their default center in the dtype of the angle or scale factor, as `warp_affine` builds its matrix. With float32 parameters and a float64 image they used to raise `ValueError` ("Inputs must have same dtype") unless `center` was passed; they now give the same float64 result as with the default center passed explicitly.
