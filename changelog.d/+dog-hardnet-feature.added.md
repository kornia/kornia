`DoGHardNet` and `DoGAffNetHardNet` detect with the DoG detector of `SIFTFeatureScaleSpace`, without and with AffNet shapes, and describe with HardNet on 12-sigma LAFs, twice the detection frames, with the orientation estimated at the detection scale. These are the frames the `"doghardnet"` LightGlue weights were trained with, so `LightGlueMatcher("doghardnet")` and `LightGlueMatcher("dog_affnet_hardnet")` take their LAFs and descriptors without rescaling. The `LightGlueMatcher` documentation states the LAF scale these weights expect.
