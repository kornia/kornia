`solve_quartic` keeps repeated roots, tells close real pairs from nearby complex pairs across coefficient scales, finds roots that span many decades or sit in a tight cluster, and returns its genuine roots in descending order followed by padding. `solve_cubic` keeps exact double roots. `solve_quadratic` and `solve_cubic` handle coefficients whose products leave the dtype range, float32 cubics are solved in float64 on every device, so a row's roots no longer depend on the rest of its batch, and all three solvers keep finite gradients in mixed batches and support full-graph compilation. On MPS, float32 quadratics and cubics are solved on the CPU in float64, as quartics already were, which fixes flushed subnormal products and misclassified close pairs; this is slower than an on-device solve from about 10^3 rows for quadratics and beyond about 5 * 10^5 rows for cubics. Under `torch.compile` with Inductor on CUDA, Triton's fused multiply-adds can still lose exact double roots of `solve_cubic` and `solve_quartic`; this is documented and not planned to be fixed.
