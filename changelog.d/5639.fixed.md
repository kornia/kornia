`solve_quartic` keeps repeated roots, tells close real pairs from nearby complex pairs across coefficient scales, finds roots that span many decades or sit in a tight cluster, and returns its genuine roots in descending order followed by padding. `solve_cubic` keeps exact double roots. `solve_quadratic` and `solve_cubic` handle coefficients whose products leave the dtype range, float32 cubics on CPU are solved in float64 so a row's roots no longer depend on the rest of its batch, and all three solvers keep finite gradients in mixed batches and support full-graph compilation.
