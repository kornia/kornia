`kornia.metrics.mean_iou_bbox` now computes integer and bool boxes in `float32`, so `uint8`, `int8` and `int16` boxes no longer wrap around in widths, areas and the union (which gave IoUs outside [0, 1] or NaN, accepted inverted `uint8` boxes and rejected wide `int8` ones). The result is still `float32`. An integer box set paired with a float16 or bfloat16 one in `xyxy` or `xywh` format now returns `float32`, as `cxcywh` already did, instead of the half dtype.
