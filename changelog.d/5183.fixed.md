`torch.compile(fullgraph=True)` now traces `RandomCrop` at `p=1`, `crop_by_indices` with `size` and bilinear, bicubic or nearest resampling, and the `ImageModule`, `ImageSequential` and `AugmentationSequential` call paths, including a fixed-order `ColorJiggle`. The containers no longer go through `functools.wraps` inside the traced call. Part of #5137.
