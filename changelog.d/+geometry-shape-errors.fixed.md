`KRt_from_projection`, `projections_from_fundamental`, `get_perpendicular`, `get_closest_point_on_epipolar_line` and `multiply_deg_one_poly` raise `ShapeError`, naming the argument and its expected shape, when `P`, `F_mat`, `points`, `pts2`, `a` or `b` lacks the batch dimension or has an extra one. Previously they raised an `IndexError`, a `RuntimeError` or an `AssertionError` from a later step, or, for some inputs with an extra dimension, returned wrong values: `multiply_deg_one_poly` with `(A, 4, 4)` inputs, and the two epipolar functions with `(A, B, 3, 3)` points. The too-few-correspondences message of `RANSAC` and the matrix-shape message of `NamedPose.from_rt` and `NamedPose.from_matrix` no longer contain runs of spaces, and the latter prints each expected shape once, as `[2, 2]` instead of `[2, 2]x[2, 2]`.
