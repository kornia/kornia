`AugmentationSequential` no longer builds the transformation matrix of each child on every call. Children whose matrix is lazy (flips, resizes, `RandomResizedCrop` in its default slice mode, and intensity augmentations) keep it lazy inside the container, and the chained matrix is built from the recorded calls when `.transform_matrix` is first read. Outputs, matrices, boxes, keypoints and masks are unchanged. On CUDA (Tesla T4, torch 2.11) with 224x224 images, a `RandomResizedCrop`, `RandomHorizontalFlip`, `ColorJitter`, `RandomGrayscale`, `Normalize` pipeline takes 31%, 15% and 11% less time at batch 16, 32 and 64 (12.1 to 8.4 ms at batch 16), and `RandomResizedCrop`, `RandomHorizontalFlip`, `RandomAffine` takes 31%, 28% and 24% less.
