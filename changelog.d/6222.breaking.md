`kornia_config.hub_cache_dir`, `hub_models_dir` and `hub_onnx_dir` default to `<torch.hub.get_dir()>/kornia` and its `models` and `onnx_models` subdirectories (following `TORCH_HOME`) instead of `.kornia_hub` in the working directory. Files in an existing `.kornia_hub` are not moved: they are downloaded again, and `download=False` raises until they are. Set the three fields to keep the old location.
