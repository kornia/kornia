`solve_quadratic` no longer loses the root of smaller magnitude to cancellation when `|4ac|` is much smaller than `b^2`: it uses `q = -(b + sign(b) sqrt(D)) / 2` and returns `c / q` and `q / a` in the previous slot order. `[1e-8, 2, -6]` gave `[0, -2e8]` in float32 and now gives `[3, -2e8]`, and the root's gradient is correct too.
