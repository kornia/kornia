Functions that flattened a batch with `reshape(B, -1)` or `view(B, -1)` now accept empty batches instead of raising
"cannot reshape tensor of 0 elements" (#6266, group A): the mutual-information losses and their `FromRef` modules,
`lovasz_softmax_loss`, `lovasz_hinge_loss` and their modules, `determinant_to_polynomial`, `ALIKED`, `XFeatModel` and
`LoFTR`. `XFeat.detectAndCompute` returns an empty list and `XFeat.match_xfeat_star` an empty list of matches for an
empty batch. A mutual-information signal with no samples raises a clear error instead of an `IndexError`.
