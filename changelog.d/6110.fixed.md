`sampson_epipolar_distance`, `symmetrical_epipolar_distance`, `left_to_right_epipolar_distance` and `right_to_left_epipolar_distance` accept a single `(3, 3)` fundamental matrix, as their `(*, 3, 3)` shapes document, and return the distances of the batched call without its batch axis. They raised `ValueError: Fm must be a (*, 3, 3) tensor. Got torch.Size([3, 3])`, except the first two on CUDA with fewer than 10,000 points, which accepted it. An `Fm` whose last two dimensions are not `(3, 3)` now raises that `ValueError` on every path. On CUDA the first two raised a `RuntimeError` or `IndexError` from the matrix product instead, and returned values for a `(4, 4)` `Fm` with 4-component points; with one point set and several matrices on the CPU, `sampson_epipolar_distance` raised a `RuntimeError`, or reshaped an `Fm` such as `(2, 9, 1)` into `3 x 3` matrices.
