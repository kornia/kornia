`sampson_epipolar_distance`, `symmetrical_epipolar_distance`, `left_to_right_epipolar_distance` and `right_to_left_epipolar_distance` accept a single `(3, 3)` fundamental matrix, as their `(*, 3, 3)` shapes document, and return the distances of the batched call without its batch axis. On the CPU they raised `ValueError: Fm must be a (*, 3, 3) tensor. Got torch.Size([3, 3])`, while the CUDA path of the first two accepted it.
