Preserve range bounds when float64 sampling is selected via `set_rng_device_and_dtype` while the default dtype is float32, instead of first rounding them to float32. This applies to `RandomAffine`, `RandomTranslate`, `RandomMotionBlur`, `RandomMotionBlur3D`, `RandomSaltAndPepperNoise`, `RandomLinearIllumination` and `RandomLinearCornerIllumination`.
