Fractional masks in `contrib.distance_transform` and `DistanceTransform` now use a piecewise differentiable generalized squared-distance transform: `sqrt(min(distance_squared - h * log(v)))`. Their values and gradients change. A selected source can receive gradients from distant outputs; zero distances and nonpositive inputs have zero gradient, and ties select one minimizing source. Dense fractional pixels with `-h * log(v) < 1` select themselves; increase `h` to let stronger neighbours compete over longer distances. `kernel_size` remains accepted but no longer affects the result. `h` must be positive and finite and no longer has convolution-dependent bounds.
