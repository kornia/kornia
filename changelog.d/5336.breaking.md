`integral_tensor` and `IntegralTensor` now raise a kornia error for a repeated axis, including a negative alias of a positive one such as `(1, -1)` on a rank-2 input, and for an axis outside the input's rank. A repeated axis used to be integrated twice (`integral_tensor(torch.ones(2, 2), (1, 1))` returned `[[1, 3], [1, 3]]`), and an axis outside the rank raised `IndexError` from `cumsum`. Distinct axes are accepted in any order.
