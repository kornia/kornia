`PatchDominantGradientOrientation`, `LAFOrienter`, `SIFTDescriptor`, `DenseSIFTDescriptor`, `SIFTDescriptorFromPyramid`,
`SIFTFeature`, `LightGlueMatcher`, `MKDDescriptor`, `RandomHue`, `ColorJiggle` and `ColorJitter` now use `math.pi`
instead of the float32 tensor `kornia.constants.pi` in their forward arithmetic (#5127). Their float64 results carried
the float32 value of pi and change by up to 7e-7 rad for the orientation angle, 1.4e-7 for a SIFT descriptor and 3.5e-8
for a hue shift. float32 results are unchanged. float16 and bfloat16 results are unchanged except in
`PatchDominantGradientOrientation` and `LAFOrienter`, where the bin-to-angle product used to round 2 pi to the half
dtype first: a third of the float16 angles move by up to 3.9e-3 rad and 2% of the bfloat16 angles by up to 3.1e-2 rad.
