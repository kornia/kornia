`warp_affine`, `warp_perspective`, `warp_affine3d`, `warp_perspective3d`, `warp_grid`, `warp_grid3d`, the normalized paths of `homography_warp` and `homography_warp3d`, and the builders `get_rotation_matrix2d`, `get_affine_matrix2d`, `get_affine_matrix3d` and `get_projective_transform` now run their matrix products outside a caller's `torch.autocast` region, except in a scripted call, so these functions, `rotate`, `scale`, the crops and the matrix-based geometric augmentations give the same result in and out of it. Under `autocast(bfloat16)` a float32 warp of a `[0, 1]` image was up to 0.97 off and still returned float32, and the builders returned a bfloat16 matrix for float32 inputs.
