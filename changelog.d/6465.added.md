On MPS, `match_snn` and `match_smnn` find the nearest and second-nearest distance of each descriptor with two row reductions instead of `torch.topk`, with the same matches and ratios, nan and infinite distances included. On an Apple M1, given 2048 x 2048 to 8000 x 8000 distance matrices, `match_snn` is 2.3x faster and `match_smnn` 1.5-1.8x faster; with the distance computation, both are 1.2-1.7x faster. The reductions take a transient copy of the distance matrix, 2.25 times its size for `match_smnn`. CPU and CUDA keep `torch.topk`, which is faster there: the reductions were 2-5x slower on an x86 CPU and no faster on CUDA.
