`normalize_points_with_intrinsics` and `denormalize_points_with_intrinsics` now pair camera `b` with every point of batch `b` for points with more than one non-batch dimension, such as a `(B, H, W, 2)` grid. They used to line the cameras up with `H`, which raised for `H != B` and gave a wrong result silently for `H == B`. `(B, N, 2)` results are unchanged. The cameras' batch is now the points' leading axis, so points with extra leading axes, such as `(T, B, N, 2)` against `(B, 3, 3)` cameras, no longer pair camera `b` with axis 1; pass `camera_matrix[None]` to keep that pairing.
