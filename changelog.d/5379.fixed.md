`filter2d`, `filter2d_separable`, `filter3d`, `fft_conv`, `box_blur`, `laplacian`, `get_spatial_gradient_kernel2d`,
`get_spatial_gradient_kernel3d`, `spatial_gradient` and `spatial_gradient3d` now accept the upper- and mixed-case
spellings of `border_type`, `padding`, `behaviour` and `mode` that their checks already accepted (#5156). Previously
`border_type='REFLECT'` passed kornia's check and then raised torch's `NotImplementedError: Unrecognised padding mode
REFLECT`, and `mode='Sobel'` or `'Diff'` raised `NotImplementedError` from the kernel dispatch. Each spelling now gives
the result of its lower-case one, a spelling outside the set still raises kornia's error, and lower-case calls are
unchanged.
