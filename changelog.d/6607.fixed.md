`XFeat` runs with float16 and bfloat16 weights instead of raising on every extraction outside autocast: the resized image is cast to the weights' dtype, keypoints stay float32, and scores and descriptors keep the weights' dtype. A zero descriptor stays zero in float16 instead of becoming NaN. `InterpolateSparse2d` samples float16 and bfloat16 maps in float32, because torch's half-precision `grid_sample` returned wrong values, NaN or overflow on CPU.
