`kornia.models.qwen25.Qwen2VLVisionTransformer` and the `kornia.models.qwen25.qwen2_vl` module with its blocks (`Qwen2VLPatchMerger`, `Qwen2VLRotaryEmbedding`, `Qwen2VLVisionAttention`, `Qwen2VLMLP`, `Qwen2VLVisionBlock`) are removed, so importing them now raises `ImportError`. They shipped in v0.8.3 and v0.9.0rc1 as a randomly initialised placeholder that was not the Qwen2.5-VL architecture (a plain ViT with a Conv2d patch embedding, LayerNorm, global attention and no positional information), and no weights existed for them. Use `kornia.models.qwen25.Qwen25VisionTransformer`, which takes a `Qwen25VisionConfig` and loads the released Qwen2.5-VL checkpoints.
