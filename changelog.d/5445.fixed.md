The `kornia.core.check` failure paths now report the rejected value: `KORNIA_CHECK_TYPE` handles a `int | str` union instead of crashing while building its message, `KORNIA_CHECK_IS_LIST_OF_TENSOR` names the first non-tensor element, the colour and gray checks print the failing shape and raise their `ImageError` under TorchScript, and `KORNIA_CHECK_DM_DESC` returns `False` (or raises `ShapeError`) for a distance matrix or descriptor of the wrong rank instead of an `IndexError`.
