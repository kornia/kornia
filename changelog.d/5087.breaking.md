`conv_quad_interp3d`, `iterative_quad_interp3d` and their modules now treat a fit as singular when `|det H| <= 1e-7 * m**3`, with `m` the largest magnitude among the Hessian entries. Previously the floor was an absolute `|det H| <= 1e-7`, and the determinant is cubic in the response amplitude, so well-conditioned maxima of a low-amplitude response kept their integer coordinates: `ScaleSpaceDetector` on a `[0, 1]` image returned almost every keypoint unrefined. Those keypoints are now refined to sub-pixel positions, which changes detector output. (#5065)
