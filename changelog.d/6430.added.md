The DINOv2 attention of the `DeDoDe` `G` descriptor uses `torch.nn.functional.scaled_dot_product_attention` instead of materialising the attention weights of every head; only training mode with a non-zero `attn_drop`, which no kornia builder sets, still materialises them. On an Apple M1 the attention layer is about 2x faster on the CPU, and the whole 24-block ViT-L backbone 1.3x faster on the CPU at 476 x 644 and 1.1 to 1.2x faster on MPS at 476 x 644 and 784 x 784. The extra CPU memory of one attention layer at 784 x 784 falls from 1.3 GB to 73 MB, and the outputs agree with the previous ones to float32 rounding.
