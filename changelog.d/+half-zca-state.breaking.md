`zca_mean` and `ZCAWhitening.fit` now retain the mean and transforms in float32 for float16 and bfloat16 inputs; previously they rounded fitted state to the input dtype, which could corrupt rank-deficient whitening. `linear_transform`, `zca_whiten`, and `ZCAWhitening` still return the input dtype, computing half-input transforms in float32. Callers applying `zca_mean` results directly must promote half inputs to float32 or use `linear_transform`. Explicitly casting fitted buffers to half precision still discards their accuracy.
