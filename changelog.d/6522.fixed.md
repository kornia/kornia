`kornia.geometry.transform.translate` and `shear`, and the `Translate` and `Shear` modules, read an unbatched `(2,)` parameter as one shift or pair of factors, `(1, 2)`, that serves every image. With one image, `(C, H, W)` or `(1, C, H, W)`, they used to read its 2 components as 2 parameter sets and return the image twice, as a batch of 2, with no error; with 3 or more images they raised a `RuntimeError`. With 2 images the result is unchanged.
