`ssim` and `SSIM` now raise the `NotImplementedError` of building the Gaussian window in a complex dtype when one image is
real and the other complex, in either order (#5574). Previously a real `img1` with a complex `img2` returned a complex
map, while a complex `img1` or two complex images raised that error. `ssim_loss` and `SSIMLoss` already raised for such
pairs.
