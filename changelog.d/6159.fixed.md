The torch 2.14 default (dynamo) ONNX exporter now exports `BoxFiltering` and the detectors that contain it. With `dynamo=True` (the default from torch 2.9), a `BoxFiltering` without a `confidence_threshold` exported a graph that filtered at `0.1` wherever the export succeeded (torch 2.9.1, for example); it now filters at `0.0`, as eager does.
