The weighted builders of `kornia.models` return the model in evaluation mode: `Sam.from_config` with `pretrained=True` or a `checkpoint` (and so `VisualPrompter` with such a config), `RTDETR.from_pretrained`, `RTDETR.from_config` with a `checkpoint`, `TinyViT.from_config` with `pretrained`, `EfficientViT.from_config`, `VisionTransformer.from_config(pretrained=True)`, `SigLip2Builder.from_pretrained_hf` and `KimiVLBuilder.from_pretrained_hf`. They returned training mode, so MobileSAM, RT-DETR, TinyViT and EfficientViT normalised with batch statistics, an image's output depended on the rest of the batch, and inference overwrote the BatchNorm running statistics; TinyViT-11M and -21M also dropped residual paths at random. Randomly initialised models stay in training mode; after loading your own weights, call `.eval()`. TinyViT's attention bias is no longer cached at `eval()`, so `load_state_dict`, a parameter update, or `.to()` / `.half()` / `.bfloat16()` after `eval()` no longer gives a stale bias (wrong logits) or a device/dtype error. The cache was filled without gradient, so in evaluation mode `attention_biases` received none, and fine-tuning with BatchNorm frozen through `.eval()` silently froze them; they now get gradients.
