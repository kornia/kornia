`crop_and_resize3d`, `crop_by_boxes3d` and `center_crop3d` no longer round the boxes and crop matrix of a float16 or bfloat16 volume to its dtype before sampling. A resized crop's corners missed the requested voxels, so a unit value read as 0.96 (float16) or 0.79 (bfloat16), and a box past depth 256 (bfloat16) or 2048 (float16) read a neighbouring slice or raised `torch.linalg.LinAlgError`.
