Two-view and calibration estimators now compute float16 and bfloat16 inputs in float32 and round the result once to the input dtype: `decompose_essential_matrix`, `motion_from_essential` and `motion_from_essential_choose_solution`, which raised for half precision, `KRt_from_projection`, which raised, and `solve_pnp_dlt`, which rejected half inputs; and, more accurate than before, `find_fundamental` (7- and 8-point), `compute_correspond_epilines`, `essential_from_fundamental`, `fundamental_from_essential`, `find_homography_dlt`, `find_homography_dlt_iterated`, `find_homography_lines_dlt`, `find_homography_lines_dlt_iterated`, `distort_points` and `undistort_points`. float32 and float64 results are unchanged.
