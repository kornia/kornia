`ShearX` and `ShearY` in `kornia.augmentation.auto.operations`, and with them the `shear_x` / `shear_y` entries of `AutoAugment`, `RandAugment` and `TrivialAugment`, now read the magnitude as a shear factor and map it to degrees with `rad2deg(atan(magnitude))`, as torchvision does. They used to multiply it by 180, so the top of the default range, 0.3, sheared by 54 degrees (a shear factor of 1.38) instead of about 16.7 degrees (a factor of 0.3).
