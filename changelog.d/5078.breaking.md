`RANSAC("essential")` now defaults to `local_optimization="lm"`: the run's eight best five-point models are refined by Levenberg-Marquardt on the essential manifold, and the winner by a final Cauchy refinement on its inliers, in the caller's calibrated coordinates. Previously `"essential"` only supported `"dlt"`, which refits eight-point fundamental matrices on inlier subsets and projects them onto the manifold; `local_optimization="dlt"` still selects that loop, but its five-point samples now come from the new `find_essential` solver, so its estimates can differ from before at rounding level and in sign. Estimates for a given seed change; the returned E has unit Frobenius norm and a positive largest entry, and the returned mask holds the inliers of the returned matrix. Automatic batches for essential matrices now start at 64 samples on CPU and double up to 1024, and start at 256 on CUDA and MPS and double up to 8192, instead of a fixed 128 to 512 samples per batch on CPU and 2048 on CUDA and MPS.
