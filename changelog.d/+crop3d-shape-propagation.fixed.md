`AugmentationSequential` now propagates all three output spatial dimensions after `CenterCrop3D` and `RandomCrop3D`, so later transforms generate parameters for the volume they actually receive. Previously, later crops used the original D/H/W and could sample incorrect regions even for nonempty batches. Statically applied empty crops also propagate their configured size. This corrects newly generated parameter sequences; previously saved coordinates and shape history are not recalculated. Part of #4429.
