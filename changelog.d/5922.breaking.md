`KMeans.cluster_assignments` now labels each training sample with its nearest final cluster center, so it matches `predict` on the training data. It used to return the labels of the last iteration, computed before that iteration's center update: a `fit` that stopped at `max_iterations`, or on `tolerance` before the labels had settled, could label a sample with a cluster whose returned center is not its nearest. The centers are unchanged; `fit` now runs one more assignment pass.
