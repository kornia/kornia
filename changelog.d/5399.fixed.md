`RgbToYuv420`, `RgbToYuv422`, `Yuv420ToRgb` and `Yuv422ToRgb` no longer declare `ONNX_EXPORTABLE = False`, so reading that attribute on them now raises `AttributeError`, as on the other color modules. All four export with `torch.onnx.export(..., dynamo=True)` and match eager execution under onnxruntime. They declare `ONNX_DEFAULT_INPUTSHAPE` like the other color modules; for the two decoders it is the shape of the luma plane. A test now exports every class that declares these attributes with its declared shape and checks that a class keeping `ONNX_EXPORTABLE = False` (`Canny`, #5398) still fails to export.
