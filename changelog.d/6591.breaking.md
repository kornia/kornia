`decompose_essential_matrix`, `motion_from_essential`, `motion_from_essential_choose_solution` and `project_to_essential` now give correct derivatives only up to the second order on a call that records one, outside TorchScript and where the smallest singular value differs from the other two. At distinct singular values, third and higher derivatives used to be correct through the SVD backward and no longer are. A scripted call still differentiates through the SVD.
