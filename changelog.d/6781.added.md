The inference paths of `ALIKED`, `DISK`, `XFeat`, `LoFTR` and the pyramid patch extraction behind `LAFDescriptor` synchronize the host with the device less often, with bit-identical outputs: small per-call constants are copied to the device without blocking, and selections that read the device once per tensor read it once (DISK picks its `n` strongest maxima without a second read, LoFTR skips the ground-truth filter outside training). Per call, measured with `torch.cuda.set_sync_debug_mode`: ALIKED 5 -> 1, DISK 4 -> 1, `XFeat.detectAndCompute` 8 -> 2, `XFeat.match_xfeat_star` 12 -> 2, LoFTR 5 -> 1, `LAFDescriptor(HardNet)` 4 -> 0. On CUDA (RTX 4090, torch 2.15) XFeat* matching is about 10 % faster and ALIKED and the HardNet patch descriptor about 4 % faster at 1024 px.
