`undistort_points` with 14 distortion coefficients applies each camera's sensor tilt to its own points when the cameras broadcast against points with more than three axes, such as `(T, B, N, 2)` points with `(B, 3, 3)` cameras and `(B, 14)` coefficients, or their `K[None]` and `dist[None]` forms. The tilt step paired its transforms with the first axis of the points while the other coefficients broadcast, so such calls returned wrong points, or raised a `ValueError` when the first axis did not match. Calls that already gave the right result are unchanged bit for bit.
