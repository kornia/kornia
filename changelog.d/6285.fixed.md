`bilateral_blur` and `joint_bilateral_blur` restore half-precision gradient accuracy with respect to `sigma_color` and `sigma_space` on non-MPS devices by computing window reductions over `(-3, -1)` in a single step instead of sequential axes reductions (#6284). This avoids intermediate 16-bit truncation between window axes during backward passes with heavy cancellation. MPS preserves the sequential two-step reduction for reduction performance.
