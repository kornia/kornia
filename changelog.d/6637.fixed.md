``kornia.feature.XFeat`` reports a keypoint found on the image resized to multiples of 32 at the centre of its pixel in the input, ``(i + 0.5) * W / W' - 0.5``, instead of ``i * W / W'``, which moves keypoints by up to nearly half a pixel on small images. Sparse keypoints of images whose sides are multiples of 32 stay identical, and the 0.6 and 1.3 rescale of the dual-scale extraction still divides by its factor. This corrects wrong coordinates against kornia's integer pixel-centre convention, so it ships without a deprecation window.
