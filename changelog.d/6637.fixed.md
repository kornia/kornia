``kornia.feature.XFeat`` reports a keypoint found on the image resized to multiples of 32 at the centre of its pixel in the input, ``(i + 0.5) * W / W' - 0.5``, instead of ``i * W / W'``, which moves sparse keypoints by up to nearly half a pixel on small images and default dense keypoints by up to about 0.8 px, because the 0.6 scale divides the move by its factor. Sparse keypoints of images whose sides are multiples of 32 stay identical, and the 0.6 and 1.3 rescale of the dual-scale extraction still divides by its factor. This corrects wrong coordinates against kornia's integer pixel-centre convention, so it ships without a deprecation window.
