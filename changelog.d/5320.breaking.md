`image_histogram2d` now raises `ShapeError` (a `ValueError`) for an image that is not `(H, W)`, `(C, H, W)` or `(B, C, H, W)`. A rank-0 or rank-1 image used to return a histogram with spurious batch and channel axes, of shape `(1, 1, n_bins)`, and a rank-5 or higher image raised a `RuntimeError` from inside `permute`.
