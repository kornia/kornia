`AugmentationSequential` with a `(B, H, W)` mask now compiles with `torch.compile(..., fullgraph=True)`, with and without `keepdim=True`: the mask's batch size comes from the per-sample gate instead of the `forward_input_shape` tensor. A `(B, T, H, W)` mask under `VideoSequential(same_on_frame=True)` no longer raises.
