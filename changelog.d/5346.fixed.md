`rgb_to_linear_rgb` no longer returns `NaN` gradients for inputs below `-0.055`. The `torch.where`
that selects between the power and linear sRGB arms gave the unselected power arm a negative base, and the
`NaN` survived the backward mask; `rgb_to_lab`, `rgb_to_luv` and the other conversions that call the
function inherited it. The forward result is unchanged.
