`find_fundamental(..., method="AFFINE")` estimates the affine fundamental matrix of two affine cameras (weak-perspective or other parallel-projection views, such as aerial images taken with a long focal length) from four or more correspondences, by the Gold Standard orthogonal regression of Hartley and Zisserman's algorithm 14.1, and `RANSAC("fundamental_affine")` estimates it robustly from four-point samples, with the exact geometric distance as its residual.
