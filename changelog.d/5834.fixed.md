`MKDDescriptor` and `Whitening` with `whitening="pcaws"` and `output_dims` of 40 or less raised `IndexError: index 40 is out of bounds` at construction, because the shrinkage constant (the eigenvalue at index `keval=40`) was read after the spectrum had been truncated to `output_dims`. It is now read from the whitening model's full spectrum, so a smaller `output_dims` keeps the leading columns of the larger transform; `output_dims` of 41 or more gives the same descriptors as before, and a whitening model with no eigenvalue at index `keval` raises a `ValueError` that says so.

`MKDDescriptor` now raises a `ShapeError` for patches that are not `(B, 1, patch_size, patch_size)`; a patch of another size used to fail deep inside with torch's broadcast `RuntimeError`, and a three-channel patch with a `ValueError` that reported five channels.

`laf_is_valid`, `get_laf_scale` and `make_upright` formed products of two LAF entries in float16, so an ordinary float16 LAF of scale 256 px or more (determinant above 65504) was reported invalid, had an `inf` scale and a `nan` upright frame. They now compute in float32 for float16 LAFs and return the mask, scale and frame as before; float32, float64 and bfloat16 results are unchanged. With it, `LAFAffineShapeEstimator` and `LAFAffNetShapeEstimator` estimate such a float16 LAF instead of returning a zero-shape frame (or the input unchanged with `preserve_orientation=True`), and `SIFTFeatureScaleSpace` with `descriptor_backend="pyramid"` describes it instead of returning the zero descriptor of a padded slot.

`get_laf_descriptors` and `LAFDescriptor` raised `RuntimeError: view size is not compatible with input tensor's size and stride` when the descriptor module returned a tensor whose per-patch dimensions are not contiguous, such as a transposed block or a channels-last convolution output; the output is now reshaped instead of viewed.
