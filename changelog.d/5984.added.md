`kornia.augmentation.CenterCrop`, `RandomCrop` and `RandomResizedCrop` support `inverse` in their default `cropping_mode="slice"`: images and masks, and for `RandomCrop` also boxes and keypoints, are warped back onto the input canvas with the inverse of the recorded `transform_matrix`, as in resample mode. Cropped-away content is not restored. These calls used to raise `NotImplementedError`, except that `CenterCrop` and `RandomResizedCrop` returned a tensor for an empty batch or one the crop was not applied to.
