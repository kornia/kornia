`kornia.losses.focal_loss` and `binary_focal_loss_with_logits` now raise `TypeCheckError` for a `pred` or `target` that is not a tensor (was `AttributeError`) and `DeviceError` when they or `weight`/`pos_weight` are on different devices (was a plain `BaseError`); `binary_focal_loss_with_logits` raises `ShapeError` for a shape mismatch (was `BaseError`).
