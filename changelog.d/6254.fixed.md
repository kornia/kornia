`Brightness`, `Contrast` and `Saturate` in `kornia.augmentation.auto.operations`, and with them the `brightness`, `contrast` and `color` entries of `AutoAugment`, `RandAugment` and `TrivialAugment`, now apply the PIL `ImageEnhance` formulas their 0.1 to 1.9 factor ranges come from: brightness multiplies the image by the factor, contrast blends each image with its mean grayscale level, and color blends it with its grayscale version. They used to add `factor - 1` for brightness, multiply the image by the factor for contrast and scale the HSV saturation for color, so a brightness factor of 0.3 clipped most of an image to black. `Saturate` now also returns a single-channel image unchanged instead of raising. `RandomBrightness`, `RandomContrast` and `RandomSaturation` are unchanged.
