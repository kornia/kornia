`HausdorffERLoss` and `HausdorffERLoss3D` raise `TypeError` for a `pred` or `target` that is not a tensor (was `AttributeError`) and `DeviceError` for tensors on different devices (was torch's `RuntimeError`). Their constructors reject a non-finite `alpha` (the loss was NaN), a `k` that is not an integer (`TypeError` in `forward` before) or is negative, and an unknown `reduction` (`NotImplementedError` in `forward` before).
