`HausdorffERLoss` and `HausdorffERLoss3D` raise `TypeError` for a `pred` or `target` that is not a tensor (was `AttributeError`) and `DeviceError`, a `ValueError`, for tensors on different devices (was torch's `RuntimeError`). Their constructors raise `ValueError` for an `alpha` that is NaN or `+inf` (the loss was NaN) and for a negative `k` (the loss was 0), and `TypeError` for a boolean `k` (counted as 0 or 1 erosions) or a `k` that is not an integer (`TypeError` in `forward` before); an unknown `reduction` raises the same `NotImplementedError` in the constructor instead of in `forward`. A `k` given as a NumPy integer or an integer tensor, `k=0`, a negative `alpha` and `alpha=-inf` still work.
