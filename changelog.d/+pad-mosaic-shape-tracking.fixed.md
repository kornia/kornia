`AugmentationSequential` now tracks the size that `PadTo`, and `RandomMosaic` with an `output_size`, return. A following integer-size `Resize`, `LongestMaxSize` or `SmallestMaxSize`, or a resample-mode crop, previously planned for the canvas before them: `PadTo((40, 40))` then `Resize(16)` returned a `(16, 26)` image instead of `(16, 16)`, and a resample-mode crop was centred on the old canvas. This applies in eager mode and under `torch.export`.
