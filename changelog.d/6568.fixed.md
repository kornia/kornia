`ImageRegistrator.register` now optimizes a float16 or bfloat16 model or image in float32 and hands the model back in
its own dtype. It used to optimize in the half dtype, where Adam's second-moment estimate and `eps` round to 0 for
gradients below about 0.0055: a float16 similarity registration ended in NaN parameters on CPU and raised in
`linalg.inv` on CUDA, and half registrations stopped short of the float32 result, such as -5.78 to -5.85 degrees
instead of -6.00 for a 6 degree rotation. A float16, bfloat16 or float32 model now also registers images of another
of these dtypes, which used to raise in `grid_sample`, and `warp_src_into_dst` and `warp_dst_into_src` sample such a
pair in float32 and return the image's dtype.
