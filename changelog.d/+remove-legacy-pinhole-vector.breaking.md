Removed the legacy 12-vector pinhole helpers `pinhole_matrix`, `inverse_pinhole_matrix`, `scale_pinhole`, `get_optical_pose_base` and `homography_i_H_ref` from `kornia.geometry.camera.pinhole`. They were never exported or documented, and their docstrings had announced their deprecation in favour of `PinholeCamera` since before kornia 0.2. `pinhole_matrix` added `eps` to every entry of its output, `inverse_pinhole_matrix` inverted a perturbed matrix, and `get_optical_pose_base` and `homography_i_H_ref` always raised `NotImplementedError`. Importing them from the module now raises `ImportError`. Use `PinholeCamera` instead: `camera_matrix` and `intrinsics_inverse()` for the intrinsics, `scale()` to rescale, and `extrinsics` for the pose (#4268, #4283).
