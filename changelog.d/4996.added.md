`kornia.morphology` gains `engine="shift"`: a running max or min over the shifted views of the padded image. For finite inputs its output equals `engine="unfold"` (`torch.equal`; only the sign of a zero can differ), `torch.compile` fuses it into a single kernel, and forward-only its peak memory is flat in the kernel area instead of growing with it (69 MiB of peak RSS growth against 1380 MiB for `"unfold"` with a 15x15 kernel on 8x3x256x256 float32 on CPU). Under autograd it saves two output-sized intermediates per kernel position, so `engine="auto"` keeps `"unfold"` for the float32/float64 CPU backward path.
