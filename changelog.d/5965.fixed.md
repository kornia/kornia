`HomographyTracker.set_target` now caches the target's features once for `LoFTR` and `LocalFeatureMatcher` matchers, instead of re-extracting them on every frame, and starts a new track. Fewer than 4 matches count as a failed match instead of reaching RANSAC, and `track_next_frame` without a prior homography raises a clear error. `ImageStitcher` warps its validity mask bilinearly, so a sub-pixel shift no longer leaves a dark seam or an extra dark column, and half images are warped in float32 internally (transiently), so half panoramas on MPS lose their dark border pixels and float32 keypoints with half images no longer raise, and a batch sample without enough matches raises an error naming the sample. `kornia.contrib.audit` accepts `(B, H, W)` masks.
