`HomographyTracker.set_target` now extracts the target's features once for `LoFTR` and `LocalFeatureMatcher` matchers and reuses them for every frame, where they were extracted again on every frame; a matcher replaced afterwards, or a target assigned directly, gets the target extracted again, once. `set_target` also starts a new track, where the previous homography, which maps the old target's coordinates, was kept. Fewer than 4 matches count as a failed match instead of reaching RANSAC, which raised. `ImageStitcher` warps its validity mask bilinearly, so a sub-pixel shift no longer leaves a dark seam or an extra dark column, and half images are warped in float32 internally (transiently), so half panoramas on MPS lose their dark border pixels and float32 keypoints with half images no longer raise. `kornia.contrib.audit` accepts `(B, H, W)` masks, which raised a `ValueError` saying the inputs must be batched.
