The `kornia.morphology` functions (`dilation`, `erosion`, `opening`, `closing`, `gradient`, `top_hat`, `bottom_hat`) now default to `engine="auto"`, which runs `"unfold"` on CUDA and the exact, low-memory `"shift"` engine on every other device. They previously defaulted to `"unfold"` everywhere. Where a window has tied maxima, `"shift"` can send the gradient to a different input pixel. Pass `engine="unfold"` to keep the previous behaviour.
