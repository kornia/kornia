`safe_inverse_with_mask` and `_torch_inverse_cast` under graph capture (`torch.jit.trace`, `torch.export`, ONNX export)
now return the finite inverse of a regular 2x2, 3x3 or 4x4 matrix at any scale the inverse fits in, with mask `True`
(#5507). Previously the closed-form `adj / det` overflowed or underflowed the dtype long before the inverse did, so the
traced mask rejected `1e13 * I` and `1e-13 * I` of order 4 in float32 that eager accepts, and `2**40 * I` came back as
the zero matrix with mask `True`. The adjugate is now taken on a copy scaled by powers of two per row and column, which
leaves every result that was finite before unchanged to the bit, short of an entry below `2**-149` of the largest
magnitude of its row in float32, which the row scaling flushes to zero. Eager mode is unchanged.
