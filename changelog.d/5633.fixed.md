`safe_inverse_with_mask` and `_torch_inverse_cast` under graph capture (`torch.jit.trace`, `torch.export`, ONNX
export) now return the finite inverse of a regular 2x2, 3x3 or 4x4 matrix at any scale the inverse fits in, with mask
`True` (#5507). Previously the closed-form `adj / det` overflowed or underflowed the dtype long before the inverse
did, so the traced mask rejected `1e13 * I` and `1e-13 * I` of order 4 in float32 that eager accepts, and `2**40 * I`
came back as the zero matrix with mask `True`. The adjugate is now taken on a copy balanced by powers of two per row
and column, which leaves every result whose adjugate and determinant were normal numbers unchanged to the bit; where
one of them overflowed or went subnormal the result was `inf`, `nan`, zero or rounded, and is now the inverse. Eager
mode is unchanged.
