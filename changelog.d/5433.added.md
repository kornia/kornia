Convention blocks for the blurring operators of `kornia.filters` (`gaussian_blur2d`, `box_blur`, `median_blur`, `bilateral_blur`, `joint_bilateral_blur`, `guided_blur`, `motion_blur`, `motion_blur3d`, `unsharp_mask`, `blur_pool2d`, `max_blur_pool2d`, `edge_aware_blur_pool2d` and their module classes): `kernel_size` and `sigma` given rows first, per-sample tensor sigmas, the bilateral and guided filters' parameter units and argument order, the motion-blur angle and streak weighting, the blur pools' sampling grid and padding. The Filtering section of the Conventions page relates them to OpenCV, scipy, scikit-image and the antialiased-cnns `BlurPool`. `edge_aware_blur_pool2d`, `motion_blur3d`, `EdgeAwareBlurPool2D`, `MotionBlur3D` and `StableDiffusionDissolving` are added to the API reference.
