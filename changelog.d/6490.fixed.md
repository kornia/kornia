`Quaternion * Quaternion`, `So3 * So3` and `Se3 * Se3` now compose an unbatched element with a batched one and return the batched result, as `So2` and `Se2` do. They used to raise in `torch.linalg.cross`. The same holds for an unbatched `Quaternion` times a tensor of scalars. Batch shapes that cannot be broadcast, such as `(3,)` and `(2,)`, raise a `ValueError` that names both shapes.
