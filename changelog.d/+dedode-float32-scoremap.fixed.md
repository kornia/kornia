`DeDoDe.detect` and `DeDoDe.forward` take the scoremap softmax and the keypoint sampling in float32 whatever the model dtype, and the detector logits stay float32, so the scores are float32 like the keypoints. In 0.9.0 a float16 or bfloat16 model ran them in its own dtype: in float16 the probabilities of an image above 128 x 128 fall below the smallest normal number, and on a peaky synthetic map 97 % of the top-10000 scores were 0 and only 15 % of the float32 keypoints were kept; bfloat16 kept 99.5 % of them, in another order. Float64 models now also return float32 scores.
