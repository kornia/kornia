`VisualPrompter.predict(boxes=...)` uses a box prompt given as a tensor; it was dropped, and the prediction was the one without prompts. A `(1, K, 4)` box prompt is read as the K boxes of the image; for K > 1 it raised a tensor size mismatch, and box prompts for more than one image now raise a clear error instead of a batch size mismatch. `VisualPrompter` resizes the image as SAM's reference does, with `torch.nn.functional.interpolate(image, size, mode="bilinear", align_corners=False, antialias=True)`, and keypoint prompts follow the same half-pixel map: it resized with `align_corners=True` while the masks are resized back with `align_corners=False`, which shifted the masks by up to 0.48 original pixels, and it did not antialias when it downscaled. On a 1380 x 2076 image, the mask IoU against SAM's own pipeline goes from 0.957, 0.966 and 0.943 to 0.969, 0.969 and 0.968. Both corners of a box prompt follow the point map, as in SAM's `apply_boxes`; the far corner went through the `Boxes` vertex at `x2 - 1` and landed about `s - 1` resized pixels short of it at a scale `s`. `VisualPrompter.predict` and `Sam.forward` run in float16, bfloat16 and float64, with or without prompts: the SAM prompt encoder built its positional encoding in float32, and they raised `RuntimeError: expected m1 and m2 to have the same dtype`; it now computes the encoding in float32 (float64 for a float64 model) and casts it to the dtype of the model. `Sam.from_config` builds a custom ViT image encoder from `SamConfig(encoder_embed_dim=..., encoder_depth=..., encoder_num_heads=..., encoder_global_attn_indexes=...)` with `encoder_global_attn_indexes` a tuple, a list or an int; it raised `NotImplementedError` for a tuple or a list and `TypeError` for an int. With `pretrained=True` and no `model_type`, it raises a `BaseError` saying that the released weights need a `model_type`.
