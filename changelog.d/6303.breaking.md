`RandomCrop` with `pad_if_needed=False` now raises `ValueError` when `size` is larger than the input, after any explicit `padding`, on either axis. Previously it returned an interpolated upscale of the input under the crop's name: slice mode resized the available slice, and resample mode warped with a mis-scaled matrix that could blend in zero padding. It raises whether or not the batch is selected, so `p < 1` does not hide it. Pass `pad_if_needed=True` to pad the input up to the crop size, or crop to a size that fits. Replaying recorded `params` is unchanged.
