`Quaternion.polar_angle` is computed as `atan2(|v|, w)` instead of `acos(w / |q|)`: the same angle up to rounding (at most 2.6e-6 rad on random float32 unit quaternions; angles below `sqrt(eps)`, which used to come out as exactly 0, are now resolved), and its gradient at the identity and at every other quaternion with a zero vector part is zero instead of `nan`.
