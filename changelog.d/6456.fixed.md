`normalize_points`, `normalize_transformation`, `run_8point`, `compute_correspond_epilines`, `fundamental_from_essential`, `fundamental_from_projections`, `intrinsics_like`, `projection_from_KRt` and `cross_product_matrix` raise `ShapeError`, naming the argument and the expected shape, instead of a bare `AssertionError` (an `IndexError` for a 0-d `cross_product_matrix` input); `intrinsics_like` raises `ValueCheckError` for a focal length that is zero or negative. `multiply_deg_two_one_poly` raises `ShapeError` when `a` is not `(B, 10)` or `b` is not `(B, 4)`, and batch sizes still broadcast as before: an unbatched input raised an `IndexError` or a `RuntimeError`, and some inputs of other shapes, such as `(2, 4)` and `(2, 10)`, returned a tensor.
