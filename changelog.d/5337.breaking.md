`Boxes.from_tensor` with `mode="vertices"` or `mode="vertices_plus"`, and `VideoBoxes.from_tensor`, now raise
`ValueError` on a non-finite vertex coordinate when `validate_boxes=True` (the default), as the `'xy*'` modes already
did. Before, the vertex modes ignored `validate_boxes` and stored `NaN` and `inf` vertices; pass
`validate_boxes=False` to keep them. The vertex modes still take no extent or shape check, so the rotated, sheared and
projective quadrilaterals of `transform_boxes` import unchanged.
