`solve_pnp_dlt` and `get_perspective_transform` run with autocast disabled, as the solvers of #6072 do, so a caller's `torch.autocast` region no longer changes their result. Under bfloat16 autocast, `solve_pnp_dlt` raised `NotImplementedError: "lu_cpu" not implemented for 'BFloat16'` on the CPU for float32 inputs, and `get_perspective_transform` ran its closed form in bfloat16: the homography between the corners of a 640 x 480 image and four targets mapped those corners 1.36 px off. A call made outside autocast is unchanged, and a TorchScript-compiled `get_perspective_transform` keeps the caller's precision as before.
