`ycbcr_to_rgb`, `lab_to_rgb`, `rgb_to_rgb255`, `normals_to_rgb255`, `hls_to_rgb` and the `RandomSnow` that uses it now give the same gradient on every torch version at a result exactly on a clamp bound, the one-sided derivative from inside the range: on torch 2.14 and later that gradient used to be 0. The values do not change.
Forward mode over `torch.vmap` (`torch.func.jvp` or `jacfwd` of a vmapped call) no longer raises "Batching rule not implemented for aten::_unpack_dual" in the intensity ops that clamp to the unit range, such as `adjust_brightness`, `adjust_gamma` and `adjust_contrast`, or in `project_to_essential` and `decompose_essential_matrix`. A gradient taken through `torch.vmap` now follows the same convention at a clamp bound. A graph captured by `torch.jit.trace` or `torch.export` keeps the plain clamp, so its gradient at a bound still follows the torch version, with or without `torch.vmap`.
