`RandomRotation90` now draws `times` uniformly over the whole numbers of its closed range. The draw used to be a continuous number that was then rounded, which gave the two end points half the probability of the others: `times=(-1, 1)` drew no turn about half of the time and each quarter turn about a quarter of the time (#4409). Seeded draws therefore differ from earlier releases, and an integer tensor range such as `torch.tensor([0, 2])` now draws its upper bound too, where it used to draw only `0` and `1`. A range with a fractional bound is drawn as before.
