`mean_iou_bbox` converts float16 and bfloat16 `xywh` and `cxcywh` boxes to corners in float32. The corners used to be rounded in the box dtype first: in float16, `289.5 + 22.375` became `312.0`, which moved the IoU of that pair from 0.4994 to 0.5029, and a 1 px wide box at x = 2048 collapsed to zero width and raised `AssertionError`.
