`euler_from_quaternion` now returns a triple that represents the input rotation at gimbal lock (`pitch = ±pi/2`).
There `roll` and `yaw` used to come from `atan2(0, 0)`, so the triple depended on rounding and its round trip through
`quaternion_from_euler` was off by up to 2 in rotation-matrix entries. Now `pitch` is returned as exactly `±pi/2`,
`roll` as `0`, and the one defined combined angle (`yaw - roll` at `+pi/2`, `yaw + roll` at `-pi/2`) as `yaw`, within
`[-pi, pi]`. The gradient there is finite and carried by `yaw` alone; it used to reach `1e7` in `float32` and `1e16`
or more in `float64`, and was `nan` at many poles on PyTorch 2.5.1.
