`euler_from_quaternion` now detects gimbal lock (`pitch = ±pi/2`) reliably in both `float32` and `float64`. The previous detector recomputed `cos(pitch)` as `sqrt(1 - sin(pitch)**2)` from an already-rounded `sin(pitch)`, which lands within a few ulps of any fixed threshold at a true pole and missed 2-4% of poles at random `(roll, yaw)`; it is now measured as `hypot(sinr_cosp, cosr_cosp)`, which separates poles from resolvable pitches by several orders of magnitude instead of a factor of ~4.
