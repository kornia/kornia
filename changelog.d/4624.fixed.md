Fixed the mixed-precision (`autocast`) guard in `kornia.feature.lightglue` to resolve the accelerator type from the input tensor instead of a hardcoded `"cuda"`. This makes `LightGlue`/`LighterGlue` usable under autocast on any accelerator (Ascend NPU, XPU, MPS) and on CPU-only builds where `torch.accelerator.current_accelerator()` returns `None`. (#4624)
