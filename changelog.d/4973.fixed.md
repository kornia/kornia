`So2.random` and `Se2.random` now draw a unit rotation with a uniform angle on [-pi, pi) (#4930). They built
`z = complex(rand, rand)`, so `|z|` ranged over (0, sqrt 2), `matrix()` was a rotation scaled by `|z|**2` with
`det` down to 3.5e-6, and every angle was in [0, pi / 2). The random values under a fixed seed change.
