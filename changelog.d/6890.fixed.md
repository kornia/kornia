A seeded eager `RANSAC` call no longer shares random draws with calls under nearby seeds: seed `s + 1` used to draw the sample batches of seed `s` shifted by one, and the local-optimization and DEGENSAC streams reused other seeds' sampling streams, so runs over consecutive seeds were not independent. Each batch of each stream now takes its own mixed seed. Seeded calls stay reproducible, but give different results than before.
