`AugmentationSequential` box and keypoint keys name their coordinate frame. `"bbox_xyxy_edge"`, `"bbox_edge"` and `"keypoints_edge"` take COCO and torchvision coordinates, where pixel `i` spans `[i, i + 1)`: a horizontal flip of a 64-pixel image maps the box `[10, 30)` to `[34, 54)`. `"bbox_xyxy_index"` and `"bbox_index"` take pixel-centre coordinates, as `"keypoints"` does, and map it to `[33, 53)`. Under the new box keys an all-zero row, or any zero width or height, is a missing annotation and comes back as zeros. `kornia.augmentation.BoxFrameWarning` is the category of the warnings below, for filtering.
