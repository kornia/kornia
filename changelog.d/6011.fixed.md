`AugmentationSequential` now propagates all three output spatial dimensions after `CenterCrop3D` and `RandomCrop3D`, so later transforms generate parameters for the volume they actually receive. Previously, later 3D crops, `RandomAffine3D` and `RandomPerspective3D` drew their parameters for the original D/H/W even for nonempty batches: a later crop could read outside the cropped volume and return zero-filled voxels, and one larger than the cropped volume returned such a zero-filled result where it now raises the same `ValueError` as a direct call. Statically applied empty crops also propagate their configured size. This corrects newly generated parameter sequences; previously saved coordinates and shape history are not recalculated. Part of #4429.
