On CPU, `match_mnn`, `match_smnn` and `match_fginn` with `mutual=True` reduce the columns of a distance matrix of more than 1024 rows in blocks of 512 rows. A column reduction over all rows walks each column with the stride of a row, which is slow when the row length is a power of two, as keypoint budgets usually are, and when there are many rows. On an Apple M1 with 2,048 to 4,096 descriptors per image, `match_mnn` is 1.8-2.0x faster and `match_smnn` 1.4-1.55x. Outputs are unchanged. CUDA, MPS and exported graphs keep one reduction.
