`guided_blur` and `GuidedBlur` now raise when `subsample` is not a positive integer, and `GuidedBlur` raises when it is built rather than on its first call: a `BaseError` ("`subsample` must be a positive integer. Got: 0") for `0`, `-1`, `True`, `False` and `numpy.int64(0)`, and a `TypeCheckError` for any other value that is not an integer (`1.0`, `0.5`, `1.5`, `2.0`, a NumPy float or `numpy.bool_`, `"2"`, `None`, a tensor). `TypeCheckError` is a `BaseError`, and since #5233 also a `TypeError`. Before, with 1-channel guidance, `0`, `-1`, `False`, `True`, `1.0`, `0.5`, `numpy.True_`, `numpy.False_` and `numpy.int64(0)` ran as no subsampling, and, on images of 8 x 8 and up, a float above 1 ran as that factor when `floor(floor(H / s) * s)` is `H` and `floor(floor(W / s) * s)` is `W` (`2.0` and `numpy.float64(2.0)` on 8 x 12, `1.5` on 12 x 12, `2.5` on 10 x 10) and failed with a broadcast `RuntimeError` at the final blend otherwise; on a tiny image it failed earlier, in `interpolate` (`1.5` on 1 x 10) or in the reflect `pad` (`2.0` on 2 x 12). With multi-channel guidance, `0`, `-1`, `False`, `numpy.False_` and `numpy.int64(0)` failed in `view` with a `RuntimeError`, every float (`1.0`, `0.5`, `1.5`, `2.0`, `numpy.float64(2.0)`) failed in `view` with a `TypeError` on images of 8 x 8 and up (earlier on a tiny image, as above), and `True` and `numpy.True_` ran as `1`. `"2"` and `None` raised a builtin `TypeError` from `subsample > 1`, and a tensor a `TypeError` from `interpolate`, with either guidance. A call that passed `0`, `-1`, `False`, `True`, `1.0` or `0.5` to mean no subsampling now has to pass `subsample=1`, and one that passed `2.0` has to pass `2`. Positive integers behave as before, NumPy integers included, with one exception: `guided_blur` now continues with `int(subsample)`, so a narrow NumPy integer no longer overflows its own dtype in the arithmetic. On NumPy 2, `numpy.uint8(2)` raised `OverflowError` with `kernel_size=301` (`numpy.int8(2)` from `kernel_size=129`), and with multi-channel guidance failed with an `OverflowError` or a `RuntimeError` from `view` on an image of 256 px or more (128 px for `numpy.int8(2)`); those calls now work.
