Vision-language calls of `kornia.models` that ran now raise or read a mask differently:

- `SigLip2Config.from_name`, `SigLip2Builder.from_name` and `SigLip2Builder.from_pretrained_hf` raise `ValueError` for a name outside their supported list whose architecture its parts cannot give: a `google/` name, such as `google/siglip2-so400m-patch14-384` or the SigLIP 1 `google/siglip-large-patch16-256`; a SigLIP 1 repository under another owner, such as `someone/siglip-large-patch16-256`, unless it is a fine-tune or mirror of `google/siglip-base-patch16-224`; and a repository part that says `so400m`, `giant`, `naflex` or a patch size other than 16. `from_name` returned a base or large patch-16 SigLIP 2 model of the wrong shape or vocabulary for these names, and `from_pretrained_hf` failed on loading its weights.
- `MoonViTAttention`, `MoonViT` and `KimiVLModel` read a 2-D attention mask as `(B, N)` padding and a 3-D one as `(B, N, N)`, one mask per sample, as `SigLip2Attention` does, whatever the batch size, and raise for one whose leading dimension is neither the batch size nor 1. A per-head `(H, N, N)` mask and an `(N, N)` mask shared by the batch were passed to `scaled_dot_product_attention` as they were, so they raise now unless `B` equals `H` or `N`, where they are read per sample: pass them as `(1, H, N, N)` and `(1, 1, N, N)`.
