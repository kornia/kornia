`Quaternion.__pow__` now keeps the norm, `q**t == ‖q‖**t (cos tθ + n sin tθ)`, so `q**2 == q * q` and `q**-1 == q.inv()` for non-unit quaternions (previously it returned the power of `q/‖q‖`); its gradients are also finite for quaternions with a zero vector part. Unit-quaternion results and `slerp` are unchanged. (#4951)
