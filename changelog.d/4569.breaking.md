- A scalar augmentation magnitude that reaches past the parameter's upper bound now raises `ValueError` at construction, the same error the explicit range form raises. `RandomHue(0.6)`, `RandomBrightness(3.0)`, `RandomSolarize(2.0, ...)`, `ColorJiggle(brightness=1.5)` and `RandomMotionBlur(..., direction=2.0)` used to be clamped silently to the bound. The lower end is still floored at the parameter's lower bound, so `RandomContrast(1.5)` still means `[0, 2.5]` and `RandomSharpness(0.5)` still means `[0, 0.5]` (#4563).
