`quaternion_exp_to_log` and `euler_from_quaternion` are now scale-safe and normalize their input internally (#3953), matching `quaternion_to_rotation_matrix` and `quaternion_to_axis_angle`. Previously, neither function normalized: `quaternion_exp_to_log([0.5, 0.5, 0, 0])` returned `[1.0471975511965976, 0, 0]` (33% too large) instead of `[0.7853981633974484, 0, 0]`, quaternions with scalar part `w > 1` (such as `[2, 0, 0, 0]`) were clamped to the origin, and `euler_from_quaternion` on `2 * q` saturated `sin(pitch)` to return `pi/2` (falsely reporting gimbal lock). `quaternion_exp_to_log` now computes `atan2(||v||, w)` which is scale-invariant and avoids the infinite derivative of `acos` at `w = +-1`.
