Assigning a `kornia.geometry.boxes.VideoBoxes` value whose `temporal_channel_size` differs from the target's, `video_boxes[key] = other`, now raises `ValueError` instead of regrouping the value's frames into videos of the target's length: two 2-frame videos assigned to one 4-frame video used to be stitched into it, one 4-frame video assigned to two 2-frame videos was split across them, and a frame total that did not divide by the target's length raised a `RuntimeError` from `view`. A plain `Boxes` value is still read as frames of the target's length.
