`transform_points` no longer runs its product in half precision inside a caller's `torch.autocast` region, where it returned float32 points up to 1.36 px off in bfloat16 at 256 px, so `Keypoints`, `Boxes`, `PinholeCamera.project`, `warp_frame_depth`, `depth_warp` and `AugmentationSequential` keypoints and boxes now give the same result in and out of the region. The same holds for `extract_patches_simple`, `extract_patches_from_pyramid`, `elastic_transform2d` and `RandomElasticTransform`.
