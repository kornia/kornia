`HomographyTracker` returns and stores every homography, `no_match()`'s included, in float32 for a `float16` or `bfloat16` target and in the target's dtype otherwise, whatever the dtype of the frame, of the matcher's keypoints or of a `previous_homography` assigned from outside. A homography found by matching used to have the keypoints' dtype: a `float64` tracker whose matcher returns float32 keypoints returned float32 homographies, and a float32 tracker given float64 frames, by a matcher that returns keypoints in the frame's dtype, returned float64 ones. `no_match()` returned the half dtype. The tracker now also tracks with `float16` and `bfloat16` matchers. The second frame raised: in `warp_perspective`, which got the half frame with the float32 inverse of the first frame's float32 homography, or in `linalg.inv` for a half `previous_homography`. The prior is now inverted and the frame warped in float32, and the fast matcher gets the warped frame in the frame's dtype. Half keypoints are widened to float32 before RANSAC; a bfloat16 fit was rounded to bfloat16 and moved the tracked points by pixels. The caller's autocast no longer runs the tracker's own warp, keypoint mapping and `ransac` fit in half precision; under `bfloat16` autocast the keypoint mapping was up to 7.6 px off on a 600 px frame, and a `RANSAC(local_optimization="dlt")` fit was several pixels off. A float32 tracker whose matcher returns float64 keypoints, or with a float64 `previous_homography` assigned from outside, raised in `track_next_frame`.
