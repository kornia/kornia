`adjust_brightness`, `adjust_brightness_accumulative`, `adjust_contrast`, `adjust_contrast_with_mean_subtraction`, `adjust_saturation_with_gray_subtraction`, `adjust_gamma`, `adjust_sigmoid`, `adjust_log`, `posterize`, `solarize`, `sharpness`, `shift_rgb` and `jpeg_codec_differentiable` raise `TypeCheckError` (a `TypeError`) for an integer image, with a message that names the conversion (`image.float() / 255`). Previously eleven of them returned a saturated, wrapped or negative result for a `uint8` image without an error, and the other two raised an unrelated PyTorch error. Floating-point input is unchanged, and the check is skipped under `disable_checks()`.
