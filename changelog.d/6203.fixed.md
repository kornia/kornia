`RandomCutMixV2` with `same_on_batch=True` and `num_mix > 1` repeated the first mix's pairing and cut in every mix, while the class output credited that donor once per mix; each mix now draws its own pairing and cut, shared by every row of the batch. Calls with `num_mix=1` or `same_on_batch=False` draw exactly as before; with `same_on_batch=True` and `num_mix > 1`, a seeded pipeline now draws differently after this augmentation.
