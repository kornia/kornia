`HausdorffERLoss` and `HausdorffERLoss3D` now erode a float64 prediction with a kernel that `get_kernel` builds in
float64, also in an ONNX export, and `MS_SSIMLoss` builds its Gaussian masks in float64 whenever it filters in float64
(float64 images or a float64 module). They used to cast weights built once in float32 (0.2, 1/7 and the Gaussian
masks), so float64 results carried float32 rounding and differed from the same loss constructed under a float64 default
dtype. A `kernel` buffer changed by hand or loaded from a `state_dict` is therefore no longer used for a float64
prediction, only in the other dtypes: a kernel scaled by 1.5 used to change a float64 loss and now does not. A subclass
whose `get_kernel` takes no `dtype` keeps eroding with its buffer in every dtype. A float64 `MS_SSIMLoss` given float32
or half-precision images now returns the float64 result rounded to the images' dtype, which can differ slightly from
before; it is the only result for float32 or half-precision inputs that changes.
