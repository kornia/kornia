`kl_div_loss_2d` and `js_div_loss_2d` now apply the `0 · log 0 = 0` convention, so a distribution with empty cells
compared with itself gives 0 with finite gradients instead of NaN (#5554). Cells where the target is positive keep
their exact values, including `+inf` where the prediction is zero; strictly positive heatmaps are unchanged.
