A wrong input rank raises `kornia.core.exceptions.InputRankError`, with one message format, from every augmentation entry point: the 2D and 3D classes, `validate_tensor`, `AugmentationSequential`, `ImageSequential`, `PatchSequential` and `VideoSequential`. They used to raise `ValueError`, `RuntimeError` or `AssertionError` with four different messages; `InputRankError` is a `ShapeError` (`ValueError`) and also a `RuntimeError` and an `AssertionError`, so existing `except` clauses still catch it. `validate_tensor` now accepts the `(H, W)` and `(C, H, W)` inputs that `forward` accepts (and their 3D counterparts) instead of rejecting them.
