`BaseWarper` and `BaseModel` (the base of `Homography` and `Similarity`) are now real abstract base classes, so constructing one directly, or a subclass that does not implement every abstract method (`forward` and `precompute_warp_grid` for `BaseWarper`; `reset_model`, `forward` and `forward_inverse` for `BaseModel`), raises `TypeError`. Previously the `@abstractmethod` decorators were not enforced: the base class was instantiable and its methods returned `None`, so a subclass that forgot `forward` failed late inside `ImageRegistrator`, or a missing `reset_model` was silently a no-op. Implement all abstract methods in custom warpers and models; `HomographyWarper`, `Homography` and `Similarity` are unchanged.
