`kornia.enhance.normalize`, `denormalize` and the `Normalize`/`Denormalize` modules now convert an integer or bool input to torch's default floating dtype before applying `mean` and `std`, and return that dtype. Previously the statistics were cast to the input's dtype, so for a uint8 image a fractional `mean` or `std` truncated to 0 (`normalize(x, 0.5, 0.5)` returned `nan`/`inf`) and `normalize` with large statistics or `denormalize` wrapped around in the integer dtype. Floating and complex inputs are unchanged, and pixel values are not rescaled (#5403).
