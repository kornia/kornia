`kornia.geometry.homography` now defines `__all__`, so `torch`, `typing.Optional`, `typing.Tuple`, internal helpers (`safe_inverse_with_mask`, `safe_solve_with_mask`, `KORNIA_CHECK_SHAPE`, `TupleTensor`) and `warnings` are no longer star-imported into `kornia.geometry`. None of the removed names is documented public API, and per `docs/source/get-started/stability.rst` undocumented helpers are private regardless of importability. The names are dropped from `tests/api_surface.json` in this change. (#4992)
