`ParametrizedLine.through`, `fit_line`, `fit_plane` and `Hyperplane.through` now raise `ValueCheckError` for input that does not determine a line or a plane: two coincident points for `ParametrizedLine.through`; fewer than two points, identical points, or weights that do not sum to a positive value for `fit_line`; fewer than three points, identical points or collinear points for `fit_plane` and `Hyperplane.through`. They used to return an arbitrary valid-looking result: a zero direction, a fixed direction such as (0, 1) or (1, 0, 0), a NaN origin for all-zero weights, or one of the infinitely many planes that contain the line. A batch is rejected when any of its rows is degenerate. The collinearity tolerance is relative and scales with the machine epsilon of the input dtype. The checks are skipped under `torch.compile` and after `disable_checks()`, where the previous results come back.
