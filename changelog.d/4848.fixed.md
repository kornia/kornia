`find_essential` and `run_5point` recover the pose again for the roughly half of all samples whose
degree-10 polynomial has a negative leading coefficient. Since #3458 that coefficient was clamped
with `clamp_min(1e-8)`, which turns a negative coefficient into `+1e-8`, so the companion matrix had
the wrong sign and scale and none of the 10 candidates was the true essential matrix. On noise-free
scenes the pose was recovered for 992 of 2000 samples at float64 and 880 at float32; it is now 1979
and 1767. At float32 the input gradients of those samples were also NaN, and now they are finite.
Only an exactly zero coefficient is replaced now, as before #3458, and samples with a positive
coefficient are unchanged bit for bit. Fixes #4847.
