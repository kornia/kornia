`StereoCamera` built from integer projection matrices now has a floating-point `Q`, in the default dtype, so
`reproject_disparity_to_3D` works on it. Before, `Q` had the integer dtype of the cameras, with truncated (and in
`int8`/`int16` overflowed) entries, and every reprojection raised `StereoException`. The `int8` baseline `tx` at a
right-camera translation of `-128` now has the right sign. Floating-point rigs are unchanged.
