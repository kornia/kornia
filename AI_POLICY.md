# AI and contributions

AI tools are welcome in kornia development, and maintainers use them too.

Our policy is the social contract at the beginning of
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md): tell us how AI helped and treat the resulting
work with care. We do not classify contributions by how much AI was involved, and
there is no separate set of rules for work made with AI.

The technical guidelines in `CONTRIBUTING.md` apply to all contributions, however they were made.
