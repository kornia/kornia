Sponsor Kornia
==============

.. meta::
   :description: Sponsor Kornia: support the open-source geometric computer vision library for Spatial AI through Open Collective or GitHub Sponsors, or contact sponsor@kornia.org for logo placement and partnerships.

Kornia is developed and maintained by volunteers and backed by the non-profit Kornia AI organization. It is
downloaded millions of times every month and used in research and production worldwide — and it stays free,
open and Apache-2.0 licensed. Sponsorship is what keeps that sustainable.

What your sponsorship funds
---------------------------

- Maintenance: reviewing contributions, fixing bugs, and keeping up with new PyTorch releases.
- Infrastructure: continuous integration, GPU test runners, documentation hosting and model weight hosting.
- New development: operators, pretrained models, and the kornia-rs Rust stack for edge devices.
- Community: tutorials, events, and mentoring new contributors.

How to sponsor
--------------

.. grid:: 1 1 3 3
   :gutter: 3
   :class-container: kornia-cards

   .. grid-item-card:: :octicon:`heart` Open Collective
      :link: https://opencollective.com/kornia

      Transparent budget and one-off or recurring contributions, as an individual or a company.

   .. grid-item-card:: :octicon:`mark-github` GitHub Sponsors
      :link: https://github.com/sponsors/kornia

      Sponsor directly through GitHub, alongside your organization's other open-source support.

   .. grid-item-card:: :octicon:`mail` Talk to us
      :link: mailto:sponsor@kornia.org

      For logo placement in the documentation, partnerships, or support contracts, write to
      **sponsor@kornia.org**.

Corporate sponsors are acknowledged with their logo in the documentation sidebar and on the project pages.
Every contribution, of any size, is appreciated — thank you for keeping open computer vision alive.
